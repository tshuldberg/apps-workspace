04 · The creation layer

One community. Any shape.

Every other community product ships one shape and asks every group to squeeze into it. Meerkat ships the builder instead. The same stamp that proves who wrote a message is what makes it safe to let people design things.

ForumPhoto wallVideo channelEvents boardReading roomPixel boardPersonal pageShop frontMembership tiers
Owner

Compose the product

The community home and every channel render from an owner-signed layout document. 16 block types in the registry today, and 9 channel kinds that change what a channel is: chat, forum, timeline, gallery, video, live, short video, storefront, events, page. No second app, no plugin market, no server administration.

Members

Build on the place

16 canvas node types place freely or flow as a page across 6 surfaces: the commons, a channel topper, a post, a pixel board, a per-community profile, and a full page a member builds that the owner can promote into a tab. Themes and layouts travel as shareable codes.

Why it is safe

Signed, sealed, reversible

Signed authorship. Every object carries its author; forgeries die before storage.
No network reach. Styling is a closed token vocabulary, never a string. Assets are sealed content ids, never URLs.
Append-only log. Vandalism is reversible, and each reader keeps local dials over what their device renders.

Proven demandSpaceHey reached roughly 1.9 million registered users offering little except the freedom to design your own page. Skyrim, Grand Theft Auto, and Super Mario Maker each outlived their own content because the creation layer let players build what the publisher never would.
An open gapThe customization products have no privacy. The privacy products have no presentation layer at all. No encrypted messenger has shipped a freeform creation layer, because signed authorship and sealed assets are what make one defensible.
Private or publicThe same builder serves a community that stays entirely private and one that carries a public face through signed publications anonymous readers can browse. A hard identity wall separates the two, not a setting.

Not shipped, and never marketed as shipped: the storefront and membership-tier blocks can be placed today and render an honest unavailable line until a billing service is configured. Creator checkout, entitlement delivery, and the open-web page renderer are documented plans, not current capability.

05 · How it works

The walls are the product.

Meerkat does not take an ordinary social database and promise it will behave. Who you are, what you may do, what is locked, what the servers can see, and how public posting works are five separate walls, each one drawn on purpose.

1 · Who you are

A key on your phone

Your phone makes it and guards it. There is no user table to breach. People check each other with five emoji.

2 · What people post

Stamped, then checked

Everything carries its author's stamp. A forgery is thrown away before it is stored, not flagged afterwards.

3 · Your files

Locked where they sit

Broken into pieces, locked, stamped, checked, and only then kept. Never readable at rest.

4 · The servers

Never trusted

A server in the middle sees how big a message is and when it went. Not who sent it, not what kind it is, not the contents.

5 · Public

Separate wall

An account verifies eligibility. A blind credential participates without joining the private identity graph.

ScopedEvery synced entity declares its maximum replication scope.
SelectableAvailability can come from peers, hosted service, or owner host.
HonestDelivery, publication, and backup require real evidence.
