Market Anchors Worth Remembering

Exit and valuation datapoints from the competitor research that frame what these categories are worth.

Rocket Money, $1.275B acquisition by Rocket Companies validates budgeting + subs tracking.
Headspace + Ginger, $3B merger anchors the mood and mindfulness cluster.
AllTrails, ~$1B valuation proxy from Permira's $150M investment.
MyFitnessPal: bought $475M (2015), sold $345M (2020). Extractive ownership pattern, users pay for it.
Evernote, ~$200M to Bending Spoons, which capped the free tier at 50 notes. Trust vacuum in notes.
Mint shutdown, 2024: 3.6M users displaced with no migration path. Data portability is a feature.
Flo FTC settlement, 2024: shared cycle data with Facebook and Google despite "private" marketing.
Finch, ~$4M/mo reportedly, bootstrapped. Indie precedent for the habits category.
BeReal, $500M sale to Voodoo after users fell from 73M to under 25M.
Goodreads, ~$150M to Amazon (2013), then a decade of stagnation. Books category is open.
