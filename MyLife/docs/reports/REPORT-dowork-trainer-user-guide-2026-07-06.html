1What DoWork is

DoWork is a workout app with a built-in coaching platform. As a trainer you get:

  • A public trainer profile with a free video library (anyone can watch) and a premium library (paying subscribers and your active clients).
  • A Studio for bulk-uploading exercise and coaching videos from your phone.
  • Client invites: a QR code or a 12-character code that links a client to you. Linked clients get your premium library included, free.
  • Form checks: clients send you a video of a lift; you reply with feedback pinned to exact timestamps, or a video reply.
  • An earnings screen showing subscribers and gross revenue once paid subscriptions go live.

Your clients also get the full workout side of the app: an exercise library, a workout builder, progress tracking, calculators, and a voice-controlled video player.

2Install the app and create your account

  1. Install DoWork from the link Trey sends you (an install link or a TestFlight invitation, depending on the build). Open the app.
  2. Walk through the welcome screens: pick your experience level and goals, or tap Skip.
  3. Before anything else, go to the Profile tab → Account → Sign in and create an account with your real email and a password.
Do not skip the email sign-in
The app works without an account, but everything you build (your trainer profile, your clients, any subscription linkage) is tied to the anonymous session on that one phone. If you reinstall or reset before attaching an email, it is gone permanently. Sign in with email first, then do everything else. Tell your clients to do the same before they join you.

3Become a trainer (redeem your invite)

Trainer accounts are invite-only. Trey creates an invite code for you (it looks like ABCD2345WXYZ, 12 characters, valid for 30 days, single use).

  1. Open the Trainers tab and scroll to the card that says “Are you a trainer?”.
  2. Tap it to open the Redeem your invite screen.
  3. Enter your Invite code and optionally a Display name (this becomes your public coach name).
  4. Tap Redeem invite, then Open your Studio.

You are now live in the trainer directory with a public profile at your handle.

4Your Studio: upload videos and build your profile

The Studio has three sections along the top: Upload, Manage, and Profile, plus buttons for Clients and Earnings.

Upload videos

  1. In the Upload section, tap Add videos to pick from your photo library, or Record to film on the spot.
  2. For each video, set a Title (e.g. “Barbell bench setup”) and a Description (coaching cues, tempo, common mistakes).
  3. Flip the Premium toggle on videos that should be subscriber/client-only. Leave it off for free previews that market you in the directory.
  4. Tap Upload N videos. Uploads run as a background queue with progress bars; you can keep using the app.

Uploaded videos appear in the Manage grid, on your public profile, and in the app's exercise rails.

Build your profile

  1. Open the Profile section of the Studio.
  2. Fill in your Headline (what you help clients achieve), Instagram, and Website.
  3. Tap Save profile.

There is also a shareable QR poster for your profile you can post or print. Note: the QR opens the app directly, so it only works for people who already have DoWork installed.

5Invite your clients

  1. Studio → Clients → Invite a client.
  2. A share sheet appears with a QR code and a 12-character invite code. Either have the client scan the QR in person, or copy the code and text it to them.
  3. When they redeem it, they appear in your Clients roster. Each row has Reactivate and End controls for managing the coaching relationship.
Codes are single use
Generate one invite per client. Linked clients get your premium library included at no charge, which is exactly what you want for people already paying you for coaching directly.

6What your clients do share with clients

Joining you

  1. Install DoWork from the invite link the trainer or Trey sends.
  2. Go to Profile → Account → Sign in and create an account with your email (important, same reason as above).
  3. Scan the trainer's QR code with the phone camera (the app must already be installed), which opens Join your trainer and connects automatically. Or: open My trainer, tap Enter an invite code, type the code, and tap Join.
  4. You'll see “You're connected”, then Go to my trainer.

Watching videos

  1. Open the Trainers tab and tap your trainer, or use the YOUR TRAINER card on Home.
  2. The profile shows the Free library and the Premium library. As a connected client the premium videos show an “Included” badge.
  3. Tap a video to open the player. Playback links are secure and refresh themselves automatically.
  4. Entitled videos can be saved for offline (useful in gyms with bad reception). Manage them under Profile → Offline downloads.

Voice control in the player

The video player supports hands-free voice commands (play, pause, speed, skip) so you can rack the bar and talk instead of touching the phone. Voice settings live under Profile → Voice control.

7The form-check coaching loop

Client sends a form check

  1. Open My trainer and tap Send a form check.
  2. Tap Record to film the set, or pick a video from the library.
  3. The upload shows “Uploading… N%” and then appears under Your form checks.

Trainer reviews it

  1. Studio → Clients → tap the client → open the form check.
  2. Watch the video. Feedback notes are anchored to timestamps: type in the Add feedback composer, keep the Attach current timestamp toggle on, and the note pins to that moment. The client can tap any timestamp chip to seek straight to it.
  3. Optionally attach a video reply (film yourself demonstrating the fix).
  4. The client replies in the same thread. Ended coaching relationships keep the history but become read-only.
Until push notifications are switched on
The app does not yet send push alerts, so neither side gets a ping when a form check or feedback arrives. Check the app manually, or agree with clients to text each other when something is sent. Push is on the launch checklist.

8Subscriptions and earnings not live yet

The plan: strangers who find your profile can subscribe to your premium library at a monthly price you pick from a tier ladder ($4.99 to $39.99/month). Your Earnings screen (in the Studio) shows subscriber count and gross revenue, backed by the real store-purchase ledger.

Right now, purchases are switched off. The paywall honestly says “Subscriptions aren't available yet” and free videos still play. This does not affect your invited clients at all: they get premium included through the coaching link. The revenue split between you and the platform is still being finalized; Trey will confirm it with you directly before paid subscriptions open.
