01 · Launch truth

Market what the build can prove.

The product's strongest marketing behavior is honesty. A reachable relay is not a friend online. A staged share is not sent. A page with an unavailable service is not a failed demo if its copy tells the truth.

Safe to show

Complete private core

Communities, channels, posts, threads, pages, files, libraries, themes, direct messages, friend and safety codes, protected shares, and real session history. Plus the whole creation layer: the layout editor and its block palette, the nine channel kinds, member canvases, asset packs, badges, personas, the pixel board, the theme editor, and the six starting templates.

Show with prerequisites

Native and hosted paths

Public participation, calls, LAN, Nearby, WebRTC, background wake, native playback, and storage providers need their named build or service.

Never imply

Unproven state

No false presence, delivery, publication, backup, public-service, revenue, user, or creator-commerce claim.

Private social,
controlled by you.
Pillar one
One place that holds everything

Chat, post, share, read, listen, and keep it, instead of five different apps.

Pillar two
Privacy is how it is built

Your phone makes its own key. Messages and files are locked. The sign-in for public posting is kept completely apart.

Pillar three
Control, without pretending

Use the free server, pay for ours, or run your own. The app always says what is really reachable.

Pillar four
A place you build

Whoever runs the group builds it from ready-made blocks. Everyone else adds pages, profiles, and decoration on top.

Win organizers, not everybody.

The first customer is the person holding a small community together across too many tools. They feel the cost of scattered chat, files, identity, and access before anyone else does.

One promise, three proofs.

Every surface should use the same ladder. Open with control, prove it by showing a group that actually works, and only explain how it works underneath as far as the person in front of you wants to go.

Primary offer

04 · Creation layer

The group decides what the product is.

This is the part of Meerkat with no direct competitor, and it is the second thing to say, never the first. People arrive for a home they control. They stay for a place they built, and they tell someone else about the place they built.

The owner composes

16 blocks, 9 channel kinds

Whoever runs the group lays out its home page and every channel, and their stamp on it cannot be forged. A channel can be chat, a forum, a timeline, a gallery, a video channel, a live stage, short video, a shop, an events board, or a page they built. No second app, nothing to install, no server to run.

Members build

16 node types, 6 surfaces

Freeform or flowing canvases on the commons, a channel topper, a post, the pixel board, a per-community profile, and a full page a member builds that the owner promotes into a tab. Layouts and themes travel as shareable codes.

Why it is safe

Signed, sealed, reversible

Everything carries its maker's name, and anything that fails the check is thrown away before it is saved.
Nothing can reach the internet. Colours come from a set list, not typed-in code, and pictures live on the device instead of at an address.
Nothing is ever overwritten. Wrecking a page is something you undo, and everyone controls what their own phone will show.
Nobody can fake the app's own markers. No decoration can draw a verified tick, a lock, or a connected light.

Build it into whatever
your group actually needs.

Say this

`Sixteen building blocks, nine kinds of channel. Your group does not have to look like anyone else's.`

`Every sticker, page, and post carries the name of whoever made it, and nobody can fake that.`

`Nothing anyone adds can reach out to the internet. Colours come from a set list, and pictures live on your device instead of being loaded from somewhere else.`

`Anyone can read what a group publishes, without signing up. Nobody at all can read the private side.`

Never this

`Plugins`, `apps`, `scripts`, `extensions`, or `code`. Meerkat deliberately runs nobody's code on a member's device, and that is worth saying plainly.

A placed storefront or membership-tier block demonstrated as a working shop. Both render an honest unavailable line until a billing service is configured.

A Meerkat community published as a website at a custom domain. The open-web renderer is a documented plan.

The public layer demonstrated from a build where the app says the services are off.

Proven demand
The freedom sells itself

SpaceHey reached roughly 1.9 million registered users offering almost nothing except the freedom to design your own page. Skyrim, Grand Theft Auto, and Super Mario Maker each outlived their own content because the creation layer let players build what the publisher never would.

An open gap
Nobody has shipped both

Discord, Slack, and Circle fix the shape and sell accent colors and vendor templates. Signal, SimpleX, Session, and Briar have no presentation layer at all. Member-authored design has historically been a security disaster, which is exactly what signed authorship and sealed assets fix.

The public face
One community, two doors

A group can stay entirely dark, publish a signed reading page anyone can browse without an account, or open the door with an owner-signed join grant. A hard identity wall separates the two, not a setting. Public paths need their services configured, and the app says so when they are not.

08 · Sequence

Thirty days from evidence to learning.

Each phase is a gate for the complete product. It is not permission to market an incomplete path as available.

T-30

Evidence lock

  • Freeze the claim matrix
  • Verify endpoints and purchase
  • Capture signed-build screens
  • Run two-device evidence
  • Complete legal and support URLs
  • Run a restore drill

Done when: every public claim has a screenshot, test, or health record.

T-21

Pilot proof

  • Recruit 5 to 10 organizers
  • Use real community workflows
  • Interview at day 1 and 7
  • Resolve setup and trust failures

Done when: organizers can create or join, add a person, send, sync, and recover without founder intervention.

T-14

Store and press

  • Complete listing and review notes
  • Submit signed candidate
  • Brief independent reviewers
  • Schedule founder and technical notes

Done when: the listing, build, and every external link are reviewable.

T-7

Coordinated preview

  • Announce only after readiness
  • Send consented preview
  • Confirm incident owners
  • Repeat critical smoke tests

Done when: every incident class has a reachable owner and backup.

T0

General availability

  • Publish product, guide, trust, and store links
  • Launch one venue-specific Product Hunt and Hacker News post
  • Monitor billing, health, support, and crashes
  • Report degraded service publicly

Done when: every call to action resolves and the operations board is green.

T+30

Evidence review

  • Publish fixes and limitations
  • Interview activation and abandonment
  • Test aggregate store creative
  • Write incident postmortems
  • Build consented case studies

Done when: the next campaign is based on observed evidence.

Apple supports testing up to three alternative product-page treatments through product page optimization.

09 · Measurement

Measure the launch, not private lives.

Use aggregate store, billing, service, and support evidence. Never build anything that links a purchase, a sign-in, a public name, a phone, a group, and a private message back to one person.

Store

Acquisition

  • Page views to downloads
  • Product-page variant lift
  • Proceeds and refunds
Operations

Reliability

  • Purchase and restore outcomes
  • Service uptime and saturation
  • Invite, relay, and backup incidents
Consent

Activation

  • Organizer time to first community
  • Real session completion
  • Day-7 interview retention
  • Support response time

Privacy wall

App Store Connect, RevenueCat, service operations, email, interviews, and support may each produce aggregate evidence. Do not join their records to Meerkat's device identity or private content graph.

10 · Ownership

Name every launch operator.

One person can cover several roles. Each role still needs a named owner, an observable handoff, and a reachable backup.

Founder

Positioning, price changes, legal and financial decisions, store agreements, public statements, incident severity.

Release engineer

Signed build, environment, upload, manifest, rollback, and health evidence.

Security owner

Threat model, privacy claims, vulnerability intake, identity walls, and investigations.

Safety owner

Reports, blocking, moderation, escalation, contact path, and coverage.

Support owner

Help center, purchase restore, backup recovery, ticket triage, and status updates.

Marketing owner

Assets, calendar, channel execution, consent records, and claim compliance.

11 · General availability gate

No green board, no broad claim.

Check these against the exact signed release build. A failed gate means fix the capability or remove the claim.
