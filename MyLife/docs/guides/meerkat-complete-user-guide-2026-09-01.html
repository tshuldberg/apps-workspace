Know before you begin

Your device is the center.

When you open Meerkat, your phone makes a key and keeps it. That key is who you are here. It never quietly becomes an account with a company, nothing readable is ever uploaded anywhere, and the app never tells you a message arrived unless it really did.

What actually works

Meerkat tells you what actually works right now. A screen can exist while the server or the part of the app behind it is missing, and when that happens the app says so instead of pretending.

Works now

Built and working, as long as the person or the file you need is actually there.

Needs the full app

Only works in the real installed app, not the cut-down preview version some testers use.

Needs a server

Needs a server that is on and that your phone can reach. If there isn't one, the app says so.

Not yet available

May have an interface, but Meerkat does not call it live.

Connection status is not presence. `Free server reachable` means a health probe succeeded. It never means a friend is online or content moved.

Create a community

Where

`Meerkat > Communities`, then the top-right plus.

What you will see

`Create a community`, `Community name`, theme, and `Create community`.

What to click or type

Enter the complete name, choose the intended theme, then tap `Create community`.

What happens next

Meerkat creates an owner-signed identity and starting channel.

Done when

The new page shows `Channels`, `Pages`, `Files`, and `Libraries`.

If it fails: use a non-empty name and read the in-sheet error. A secure-storage failure needs a full build.

Join with an invite

Where

`Meerkat > Communities > + > Join with an invite`.

What you will see

Scan, paste, and `Preview invite` controls.

What to click or type

Scan or paste the complete invite, then tap `Preview invite`.

What happens next

The signature, community identity, and join policy are validated.

Done when

The confirmed community appears under `Communities`.

If it fails: request a fresh invite. Request-only joins need a reachable server and real owner approval.
Owner availability choice: without a community server, content is `Available from members who have it, when a sync connects.` After the owner enters an HTTPS host and taps `Verify and attach`, success reads `Always available via this community's server.`

Attach a community server

Where

`Communities > [community] > Settings > Community server`.

What you will see

The current availability line, an HTTPS address field, and `Verify and attach` for the owner.

What to click or type

Enter the complete HTTPS host and tap `Verify and attach`.

What happens next

Meerkat actually contacts the server to check it is alive and really is a Meerkat server. Meerkat runs a health probe and attaches the host only after the signed publication is accepted.

Done when

The page reads `Always available via this community's server.`

If it fails: verify HTTPS, the health endpoint, and the required community routes. Peer availability remains unchanged.

4 · The builder

Nothing here decides what your community looks like.

Every object is signed by whoever made it, styling comes from a fixed vocabulary rather than free text, and every image is sealed content stored on the device rather than a link, so nothing a member places can reach the network.

Compose the community home

Where

`Communities > [community] > Community settings > Layout > Open layout editor`. Owner only; anyone else sees `Only the community owner can edit its layout.`

What you will see

A `Surface` control (`Home` or one channel), plus `Template`, `Capabilities`, `Add a block`, `Preview`, and `Publish layout`.

What to click or type

`Add a block`, then pick from `Hero`, `Chat`, `Posts`, `Timeline`, `Gallery`, `Video`, `Video player`, `Live stage`, `Short videos`, `Files`, `Storefront`, `Membership tiers`, `Link embeds`, `Events`, `Page`, `Members`. `Edit` changes a block's settings.

What happens next

The editor holds a draft. `Preview` renders it through the real blocks. Publishing writes one owner-signed revision that other devices verify before rendering.

Done when

The community home shows your arrangement.

If it fails: `This community is not on this device.` means it has not synced here. A block showing an honest unavailable line needs a service or a full app build. `Reset to classic layout` restores the default.

Share a layout, or start from another

Where

The `Template` control inside the layout editor.

What you will see

`Share this layout, or start from another community's.`, `Copy layout template`, and `Paste a meerkat-layout template or link`.

What to click or type

`Copy layout template` to hand yours to anyone as a code. To use someone else's, paste it and tap `Import into draft`.

What happens next

An import lands in your draft only. You still review and `Publish layout`.

Done when

The button reads `Copied`, or the arrangement appears in the draft.

If it fails: `That doesn't look like a Meerkat layout.` means the pasted text is not a layout code.

Build a page and promote it

Where

Open the community and tap `Open community pages`.

What you will see

A screen headed `Pages`. With none yet it reads `No pages yet. Build the first one.`

What to click or type

`New page`, then `Build`. Place items and adjust with `Move`, `Size`, `Turn`, `Stack`, `Front`, `Back`, `Remove`. `Templates` starts you from a layout instead of an empty page. `Done building` leaves build mode.

What happens next

Each item is signed by you and saved as its own row. Only you or a curator can change or remove what you placed.

Done when

The page is listed. An owner can `Promote to a tab`, type a `Tab name`, and tap `Promote`. `Demote` removes it from the tab bar without deleting it.

If it fails: `Your role cannot place on this layer here.` means the owner restricted that layer. `Only its author can change it.` means it is someone else's item; a curator sees `As a curator you can remove it.`

Design your profile, and set the theme

Where

The community's member list, then your own name. Themes are on the community screen under `Community theme`.

What you will see

`Design my profile` and `Use one of my other designs`. For themes: `Use community theme`, `Use my theme`, `High contrast`, and `Adopt this theme`.

What to click or type

Build the profile the same way as a page. For a palette of your own: `Me > Appearance > Create custom theme`, then edit colors or `Generate from a color`.

What happens next

A copied design is a draft signed under this community's keys and creates no link to the community you copied it from. `High contrast` always wins over any theme.

Done when

The community renders as you chose. `Share` hands a theme over as a code or QR; the other person uses `Import a theme`, then `Paste a theme code` or `Scan QR`.

If it fails: a malformed theme or template code is refused and the previous state stays. Nothing imported can change the app's own trust indicators, and a community theme never reaches the tab bar, Feed, Messages, or direct message threads.
Declared is not available. An owner can declare `Video`, `Live stage`, `Short videos`, `Storefront`, `Membership tiers`, `Link embeds`, and `Offline downloads` under `Capabilities`. If this build cannot deliver one, the block says so plainly, for example `The storefront is enabled here, but this build has no billing service configured.` Meerkat never renders a capability it cannot actually run, and the creator commerce rails behind the storefront and tier blocks are a documented plan rather than a shipped feature.

6 · Pair and sync

Move data only through real sessions.

The session ledger is the truth. Counts shown there come from the sync engine tables.

Pair devices, then sync through a connection server

Where

On both devices: `Me > Advanced connection > Sync`.

What you will see

Controls to pair, a five-emoji check, the connection server address, a box for a shared phrase, and a list of syncs that really happened.

What to click or type

Complete pairing and compare emoji. Then enter the same phrase. On one device tap `Listen`; on the other tap `Sync now`.

What happens next

The devices pin trust, then a pairwise encrypted engine session runs over the phrase-derived rendezvous.

Done when

The paired device is safety checked and a completed session shows real sent, received, and applied counts.

If it fails: never approve mismatched emoji. Check `Connection status` and correct `No connection server` or `Unreachable` before retrying.

11 · Public layer

One community, two doors.

Meerkat has a public side as well as a private one, and they are two doors into the same product rather than two products. Whoever owns a group can publish things out of it; anyone can read those things with no account, no profile, and nothing following them around; and the owner can hand out an open invitation that turns a reader into a member. The wall between them is structural, not a setting: the verification account never becomes the Meerkat identity, public writes carry an anonymous blind-signed credential, and private messages, communities, and sync never call the account layer. A group can stay entirely dark, publish a reading page, or open its door, and none of those choices moves private content anywhere.

Where

`Meerkat > Public`.

What you will see

`Public` and `Explore`, with real published content, or a message naming exactly what is missing.

What to click or type

Read whatever is there, without signing in. To post yourself, sign in separately, and only when those servers are switched on.

What happens next

When you post, you carry a pass that proves you are allowed to post without revealing who you signed in as.

Done when

Fetched content appears, or the app honestly states which service is unavailable.

If it fails: do not alter the private mesh identity. Ask the operator to verify account, persona, humanity, and directory service configuration.
