## Key Takeaways

### Revenue Concentration
- The top 3 revenue generators across all 29 categories are **Angi ($1.19B)**, **Reddit ($1.3B)**, and **Mercari ($1.21B)** -- all marketplace/platform businesses.
- In pure consumer app categories, **Duolingo ($748M)**, **MyFitnessPal ($310M)**, and **Calm ($300M est.)** lead.

### Valuation Leaders
- **Discord ($15B)**, **Notion ($10-11B)**, **Duolingo ($9.5B)**, **Thumbtack ($3.2B)**, and **Headspace ($3B)** represent the highest valuations.

### Category Patterns
- **Winner-take-most**: Budget, Notes, Words, Trails, Workouts, and Mood categories each have 1-2 dominant players with 10x+ revenue vs. the rest.
- **Fragmented**: Closet, Pets, Stars, Meds, and Subs categories lack a clear dominant player, presenting opportunity for a well-executed hub module.
- **Acquisitions accelerating**: Bending Spoons (Evernote, Komoot, Eventbrite), Bumble (Geneva), Naver (Poshmark), Samsung (Whisk), and Automattic (Day One) are active acquirers in these spaces.

### Profitability
- Bootstrapped indie apps (YNAB, Obsidian, Bear, Paprika, Penzu, Copilot) tend to be profitable at modest scale.
- VC-backed consumer apps often remain unprofitable even at $100M+ revenue (Reddit, Discord, Headspace).
- B2B-pivoted consumer apps (Medisafe, MyTherapy, PetDesk) find profitability through enterprise/pharma contracts.

### MyLife Opportunity
- Many categories MyLife covers have dominant paid apps charging $50-$100/yr per module. A $99/yr bundle covering 28+ modules would represent extreme value vs. buying individual competitors.
- The most defensible moat would be data integration across modules (e.g., nutrition <> workouts <> mood <> health correlations) -- something no single-category competitor can offer.
