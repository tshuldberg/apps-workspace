# MyBudget Investor Mockup Prompts

**Module one-liner:** Know where every dollar went, and why.
**Accent color:** #22C55E (green)
**Marquee features to showcase:** Envelope allocation grid with real-time left-to-spend, Plaid bank sync with subscription detection on a 215-entry catalog, debt payoff and net-worth tracker with age-of-money.
**Wedge vs competitors:** YNAB-grade rigor without a SaaS subscription or forced cloud, on-device by default.
