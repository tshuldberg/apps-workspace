# MySleep Investor Mockup Prompts

**Module one-liner:** Track your sleep, remember your dreams.
**Accent color:** #A78BFA (lavender)
**Marquee features to showcase:** Nightly sleep journal with wake-feeling scoring, dream capture with pattern analysis, factor tracking correlated to quality.
**Wedge vs competitors:** A private dream and sleep log, not a wearable-subscription silo.

## Prompt 1 - Hero screen (mobile 390x844)

```
Design MySleep log home, 390x844, iOS, #131318. Top: last night summary "7h 32m, feeling 8/10" lavender #A78BFA accent. Below: factor chips logged (caffeine, screens, stress). Dream journal preview card. Bottom tab: Log, Dreams, Factors, Trends. Nighttime quiet, muted.
```
