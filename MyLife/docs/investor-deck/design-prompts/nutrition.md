# MyNutrition Investor Mockup Prompts

**Module one-liner:** Your food diary stays on your device.
**Accent color:** #F97316 (orange)
**Marquee features to showcase:** Barcode scan with Open Food Facts and AI photo logging, BMR and TDEE with HealthKit active-calorie sync, cross-module insight engine (fasting + workouts + mood).
**Wedge vs competitors:** MyFitnessPal's depth without the ad-load, private by default with cross-module intelligence.
