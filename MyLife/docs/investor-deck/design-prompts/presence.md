# MyPresence Investor Mockup Prompts

**Module one-liner:** Put your phone down. Pick your life up.
**Accent color:** #0891B2 (deep cyan)
**Marquee features to showcase:** Daily and per-app usage with 14-category taxonomy, focus sessions (solo/group/beast) with planned vs actual, intention prompts before opening an app.
**Wedge vs competitors:** Screen Time plus Opal plus Forest with XP and accountability, inside a privacy suite.
