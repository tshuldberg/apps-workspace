# MyHealth Investor Mockup Prompts

**Module one-liner:** Your health data, on your device, under your control.
**Accent color:** #EF4444 (red)
**Marquee features to showcase:** 0-100 daily health score across 6 domains, cross-domain Pearson correlation engine, doctor-visit report generator.
**Wedge vs competitors:** A unified health hub absorbing meds, fasting, and cycle data that stays on-device.
