# MyPets Investor Mockup Prompts

**Module one-liner:** Pet health records and care tracker.
**Accent color:** #F59E0B (amber)
**Marquee features to showcase:** Multi-pet profiles across 9 species with medical records, vaccinations with due reminders and core-vaccine schedules, weight trends and expense tracking.
**Wedge vs competitors:** A genuine pet medical record plus care planner, not a social-feed pet app.

## Prompt 1 - Hero screen (mobile 390x844)

```
Design MyPets home, 390x844, iOS, #131318. Top: pet picker carousel with circular photo avatars, selected ringed amber #F59E0B. Below: hero card showing next vaccine due, weight trend sparkline, med schedule chips. Bottom tab: Pets, Health, Weight, Costs. Vet-calm, not cute-overload.
```
