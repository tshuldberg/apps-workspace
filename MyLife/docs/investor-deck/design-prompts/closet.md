# MyCloset Investor Mockup Prompts

**Module one-liner:** Your wardrobe, fully private.
**Accent color:** #E879A8 (rose)
**Marquee features to showcase:** Digital wardrobe with cost-per-wear, outfit suggestions adapting to weather, laundry and packing lists from your own items.
**Wedge vs competitors:** Whering-grade wardrobe without surrendering your closet photos to a fashion ad network.

## Prompt 1 - Hero screen (mobile 390x844)

```
Design MyCloset wardrobe, 390x844, iOS, #131318. Top: header "Wardrobe" and filter chips Tops, Bottoms, Shoes, Outerwear. Main: 3-column masonry grid of clothing items on neutral cutouts, each tile a glass card with a small cost-per-wear badge in rose #E879A8. Bottom tab: Wardrobe, Outfits, Laundry, Packing. Editorial, minimalist magazine feel, not mall-app garish.
```
