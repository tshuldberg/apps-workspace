## Summary

Meerkat is raising a **$400,000 angel round** on the published **YC Post-Money SAFE (Cap Only)**, with a **$10M post-money valuation cap** and **no discount**. Minimum individual check is **$25,000**. Rolling close, targeting final close by end of Q2 2026.

Proceeds fund the public launch of **BestChef**, Meerkat's wedge product: a social recipe app where home cooks compete on leaderboards and macros, pantry, and grocery lists sync automatically. BestChef is the first of 39 modules already built on the Meerkat hub.

The round is intentionally sized to preserve founder control, keep governance simple, and bring on strategic angels whose networks accelerate launch distribution.

## Ownership Math

At the $10M post-money cap, a $400K round represents approximately **4% target dilution** for new investors, assuming full allocation is taken up.

Representative individual outcomes, assuming the SAFE converts at the cap in the next priced round:

| Check size | Approx. ownership at cap |
|------------|---------------------------|
| $25,000 | ~0.25% |
| $50,000 | ~0.50% |
| $100,000 | ~1.00% |
| $250,000 | ~2.50% |

Actual ownership at conversion depends on the terms of the next priced round (pre-money, new-money raised, option pool expansion). If the next round prices **below** the $10M cap, the SAFE converts at that lower price. If it prices **above** the cap, the SAFE converts at the cap.

## Why This Round, Why Now

This is not a "we need capital or we die" raise. The Meerkat hub (39 modules, 4,000+ tests, mesh sync shipped) and BestChef (590 tests, App Store-ready) exist today because founder built them unfunded.

This raise exists because **speed compounds** in consumer software. Every month between now and launch is a month competitors do not have AI-native architecture, local-first storage, or a $12 price point. Getting to 50K paid users in Q4 2026 instead of Q2 2027 compounds meaningfully across the next 3 years.

What this money buys that we cannot get any other way:
1. **Paid creator slots alongside free ones**. 15 committed free creators is a strong floor; 3 paid top-tier creators is the launch spike that drives App Store Featured placement.
2. **Founder full-time commitment immediately** rather than after BestChef revenue covers it (~6 months delay).
3. **Quality bar for public launch**. Designer contract ensures app-store polish that matches App Store Editorial standards.

The right angel for this round has either shipped consumer software at scale, runs a creator business that can amplify launch, or has strategic network value that compounds on top of the check.

## Investor Requirements

We are selective about who participates in this round. The wrong angel is worse than no angel at this size.

**Looking for:**
- Operators who have shipped consumer software at scale (100K+ paid users)
- Creators with operator chops who understand launch mechanics
- Industry operators in food tech, fitness, or privacy-aligned consumer products
- Former founders who have raised their own seed and can coach on the Series A if it happens

**Not a fit:**
- Institutional venture funds (fund math does not work at this size and dilution)
- Investors who require board seats at sub-$2M checks
- Investors who want revenue-share or profit-share overrides
- Investors who expect a forced exit path

The founder will remain principal through at least Series A. Any future priced round will adhere to the anti-enshittification pledge (no ads, no data sales, no bait-and-switch tier changes).
