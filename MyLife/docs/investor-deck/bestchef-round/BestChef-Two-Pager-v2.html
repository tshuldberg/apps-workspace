Why $2.99/month, on purpose

The pricing is the wedge, not an oversight

Cheapest sub in the category

Yummly $5/mo, Mealime $5.99/mo, premium subs $8 to $10. $2.99 is the most credible "we are not them" signal in a category poisoned by ad farms and aggressive paywalls.

Recurring revenue compounds with the graph

Kitchen utility supplies retention (pantry, lists, OCR). Leaderboards supply frequency. Revenue and engagement compound on the same surface.

Revenue layers come after traction

Creator tipping, premium creator subs, sponsored leaderboard slots, ingredient affiliate. All on the roadmap, none launch-critical. We earn them by first proving weekly opens.

Trust & safety plan

20% of the round · the non-negotiable line

UGC photo and video plus a public voting layer means moderation is the operation, not a footnote. The $100K T&S allocation funds tooling, an on-call moderator rotation, and the policy work that keeps the leaderboards credible. Treated as core infrastructure, budgeted accordingly.

Pre-publish

  • Automated image + video classification (CSAM, nudity, violence, hate)
  • Recipe text scan for unsafe medical or allergen claims
  • New-account rate limits and progressive trust scoring
  • Age gating on submissions and creator program enrollment

Post-publish

  • In-app report flow with category triage and SLA
  • Vote integrity: device + behavior signals against brigading and rings
  • Human moderator queue with on-call rotation across the team of three
  • Appeals path, transparency log, and policy versioning from day one

Vendor stack: Hive or Sightengine for media classification, OpenAI moderation for text, in-house dashboards for queue and appeals. CSAM reporting via NCMEC. Policies and enforcement bands documented before launch and reviewed quarterly.
