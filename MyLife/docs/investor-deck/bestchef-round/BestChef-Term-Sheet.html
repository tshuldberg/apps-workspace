Summary

BestChef is raising a $500,000 seed round on the published YC Post-Money SAFE (Cap Only), with a $10M valuation cap and no discount. Target ownership at the cap is 5%. Minimum check is $25,000. Rolling close, final close target Q2 2026.

Proceeds fund the public launch of BestChef, a social leaderboard recipe app paired with a private kitchen suite (saved recipes, grocery lists, pantry, receipt OCR, automatic macros). Product is built. The round is intentionally weighted toward acquisition spend.

Use of funds is marketing-heavy by design. 55% of the raise goes directly to paid creator partnerships, App Store ads, and content production. Product is not the risk. Reach is. Six months of focused acquisition.

Why This Round, Why Now

BestChef is built. The risk is reach, not engineering. Recipe content is one of the most viral content categories on TikTok and Instagram. BestChef gives creators something every other recipe app does not: a leaderboard that ranks their recipe against everyone else's. That mechanic produces shareable evidence on every submission.

What this money buys:

  • Three paid top-tier creator slots layered on top of the free creator commitments already in place.
  • Apple Search Ads on competitor keywords. Highest-intent install traffic available on iOS.
  • App Store editorial polish. Designer pass on screenshots, icon, and onboarding video for Apple Featured submission.
  • Founder full-time immediately rather than after revenue covers it.

Investor Fit

This round is sized and structured for strategic operator angels and small consumer-focused funds. The wrong investor at this size is worse than no investor.

Looking for:

  • Operators who shipped consumer software at scale (100K+ paid users)
  • Food, fitness, or creator economy operators with relevant network access
  • Former founders post-seed who can coach on Series A if pursued
  • Funds whose check size and ownership target match this round

Not a fit:

  • Investors who require board seats at this check size
  • Investors who want revenue-share or profit-share overrides
  • Funds whose math requires more than 5% ownership for $500K
  • Investors who expect a forced exit path
