Execution Steps

  1. Investor reviews the YC template (SAFE-C.pdf), this cover sheet, and the term sheet at BestChef-Term-Sheet.html.
  2. Investor confirms accredited status (self-certification per Reg D Rule 506(b)).
  3. Investor and Company agree on the Investor's Purchase Amount in writing.
  4. Counsel (or Clerky or Carta) generates the executable SAFE by filling the YC template fields with the values from this sheet and the Investor-specific fields.
  5. Both parties sign. Wire is sent on or about the execution date.
  6. Company delivers a counter-signed SAFE PDF to the Investor and updates the cap table.
