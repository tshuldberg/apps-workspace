BestChef

The social leaderboard for cooking

Every dish has rankings. Every dish has a chef. Submissions earn votes. Votes earn rank. Competition around the world will help the best dishes and chefs rise to the top.

Underneath the social layer sits a private kitchen for saved recipes, grocery, pantry, and receipt OCR. This makes BestChef a daily cooking and planning companion, helping all of us preserve and share food around the world.
