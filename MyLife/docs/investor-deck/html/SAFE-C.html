SAFE-C structure

YC post-money valuation-cap-only SAFE + separate pro rata side letter

Meerkat is raising up to $1.5M on the standard YC post-money valuation-cap-only SAFE. In an equity financing, the SAFE converts using the better of the round price or the cap price. In a liquidity event, the holder receives the better of the cash-out amount or the as-converted amount. Any pro rata right is documented separately in the companion YC side letter.
