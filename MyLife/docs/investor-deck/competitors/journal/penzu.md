# Penzu — Deep Dive (vs MyJournal)

**Module:** journal
**Tier:** direct (privacy-forward, web-first personal diary)
**Founded:** 2008
**HQ:** New York, NY
**Status:** private (bootstrapped, profitable since inception)

## One-line
The oldest online journal still shipping, known for "military-grade" AES-256 encryption and a bootstrapped 8-person team serving 1M+ diarists at $20-60/yr.

## Product
- Multiple private journals, entries, tags, reminders
- AES-256 double-encryption (Penzu's long-standing marketing)
- Custom covers/themes; share by secure link
- Pricing: free; Pro $4.99/mo or $19.99/yr; Pro+ $49.99/yr
- Platforms: Web (primary), iOS, Android

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Pro subscription** ($4.99/mo or $19.99/yr): unlocks additional journals, custom covers, email reminders.
2. **Pro+ subscription** ($49.99/yr): AES-256 encryption, shared journals, unlimited photos.
3. **Legacy annual plans**: grandfathered pricing tiers.
No ads, no B2B enterprise. Church/therapist bulk deals exist informally.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Web-first, mobile second.** Penzu's iOS/Android apps trail the web experience. MyJournal's mobile is 21 routes (though web is only 5 — gap to close).
- **Dated UX.** Penzu's design hasn't been refreshed meaningfully in 5+ years; MyJournal uses Cool Obsidian design system.
- **Encryption as sole differentiator.** Penzu sells "military-grade AES" — but data still hits their servers. MyJournal is local-first SQLite, arguably stronger privacy guarantee.
- **No CBT, therapy prep, affirmations, vision boards, philosophy quotes.** Penzu is a plain diary; MyJournal ships all five with tests.
- **No cross-module correlation, no voice, no location, no weather metadata.** MyJournal captures all four automatically.
