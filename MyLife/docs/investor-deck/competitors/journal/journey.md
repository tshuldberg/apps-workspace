## How they make money (precise)
1. **Journey Membership subscription** ($6.99/mo or $49.99/yr): unlocks cloud sync, Premium templates, coach, publishing.
2. **Lifetime membership** (one-time): assistive-tech and bulk licensing (e.g., Australian AssistiveTech channels).
3. **Desktop app upgrades**: paid tiers on Windows/macOS app stores.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Sync depends on user's own Google Drive / Dropbox.** Journey doesn't host; this is elegant but brittle. MyJournal is SQLite on device with explicit CSV/markdown export.
- **No CBT / therapy / affirmations / philosophy stack.** Journey is journaling UX only. MyJournal has 15 cognitive distortions, 4 therapy templates, 8 affirmation categories, 5 philosophy traditions, vision boards, book builder — all built-in.
- **Single-category pricing.** $49.99/yr standalone vs $99/yr MyLife suite covering 31 modules.
- **No cross-module correlation.** Journey is isolated; MyJournal's `writing-insights.ts`, `therapeutic-progress.ts`, `habit-intelligence.ts` engines consume data from MyMood + MyHabits.
