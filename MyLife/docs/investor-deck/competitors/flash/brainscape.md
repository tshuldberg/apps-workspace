# Brainscape — Deep Dive (vs MyFlash)

**Module:** flash
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2010
**HQ:** New York, USA (Bold Learning Solutions, Inc.)
**Status:** private (lightly VC-backed — ~$1.7M raised)

## One-line
Confidence-based repetition flashcard platform with a curated expert-content marketplace (USMLE, bar exam, real estate licensing) and a slow-growing B2B certification tier.

## Product
- Flashcards rated 1-5 by user-reported confidence → algorithm surfaces low-confidence cards more often
- "Certified" decks made by subject experts (premium, often sold per-deck or per-domain)
- Free user-generated decks (similar to Quizlet)
- Corporate/classroom tier for compliance training
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: free / Pro $9.99/mo or $47.88/yr; Certified decks $4.99-49.99 à la carte; Enterprise custom
- Distinguishing UX: self-rated confidence is the single algorithmic input — simpler than FSRS, more intuitive than SM-2

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Pro subscription** $47.88/yr — ad-free, offline, advanced stats.
2. **Certified deck marketplace** — expert-created decks, one-time $4.99-49.99; revenue-share with creators.
3. **Brainscape for Business / Classroom** — compliance and training (SMB/edu).

Unit economics: two-sided marketplace dynamics for Certified decks (creators + buyers) but nowhere near Quizlet's UGC scale. B2B growing but small.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Subpar algorithm:** confidence-based repetition is less retention-optimized than FSRS. MyFlash ships FSRS-inspired scheduling.
- **No image occlusion, no cloze beyond basic:** Brainscape missed the visual-learner wave. MyFlash has rect/ellipse occlusion + Anki-style cloze.
- **No cross-module learning loop:** Brainscape is a silo. MyFlash pulls study triggers from MyBooks reading and MyNotes.
- **B2B/edu distraction:** product roadmap pulled toward enterprise LMS, consumer features stagnated. MyFlash is consumer-first.
- **Niche exam lock-in isn't applicable:** Certified decks are paid-course alternatives, not a competitive feature to replicate — MyFlash wins the personal-study layer without needing a marketplace.
