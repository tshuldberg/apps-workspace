# Snapchat — Deep Dive (vs MyFriends)

**Module:** friends
**Tier:** adjacent (friend-list-first social network)
**Founded:** 2011
**HQ:** Santa Monica, USA (Snap Inc.)
**Status:** public (NYSE: SNAP, IPO 2017)

## One-line
The youth-dominant friend-graph network whose core product remains ephemeral messaging + Stories + Snap Map, monetized via ads and the nascent Snapchat+ subscription, with a friend list MyLife users treat as a social utility — not a CRM.

## Product
- Snaps (disappearing photos/videos), Stories, Chat, Memories
- Snap Map (friend locations, Places)
- Snapchat+ (custom AI chatbots, extra filters, app icons)
- AR lenses (Spectacles 2025 hardware relaunched)
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web (limited)
- Pricing: free ad-supported / Snapchat+ $3.99/mo or $29.99/yr
- Distinguishing UX: ephemerality + best-friends metrics (streaks, Best Friends emojis)

## Acquisition / Exit
Independent as of 2026-04. Rejected $3B Facebook acquisition offer in 2013 (famously). IPO in 2017. Evan Spiegel and Bobby Murphy retain dual-class supervoting stock — acquisition effectively impossible without their consent. No public acquisition talks since IPO. Ongoing competitive pressure from Meta, TikTok, YouTube Shorts.

## Users
- DAU: 453M (Q3 2024 earnings)
- MAU: 800M+ (company claim)
- Snapchat+ subscribers: 12M+ (Q3 2024 disclosure)
- Geography: global; US ~25%, Europe ~25%, RoW growing (India)

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Advertising** — Snap Ads (video), Collection Ads, Lenses, Filters; $5B+/yr. Heavy DR (direct-response) focus post-Apple ATT.
2. **Snapchat+ subscription** — $3.99/mo, 12M+ subs = ~$575M ARR.
3. **Spectacles** — AR hardware (low volume).

Unit economics: post-ATT ad targeting recovery lifted 2024 revenue; platform fragility (TikTok competition + ad measurement) keeps margins compressed.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Friend graph is a stream, not a CRM:** Snapchat tells you who you snapped yesterday; it does not help you remember a friend's birthday, the last time you hung out, or a gift idea. MyFriends does all three.
- **No relationship quality metrics:** streaks measure engagement, not friendship. MyFriends computes hangout quality and social-energy scores locally.
- **Ephemerality = no memory:** Snaps disappear; Memories is a backup, not a diary. MyFriends preserves photos + context per person permanently on-device.
- **Ad surveillance baseline:** Snap profiles users for ad targeting. MyFriends does zero cloud profiling.
- **Cross-module enrichment:** MyFriends hangouts link to MyDining visits, MyRecipes shared meals, MyRSVP events — Snapchat has none of that.
