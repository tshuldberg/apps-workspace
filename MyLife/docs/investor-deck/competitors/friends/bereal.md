# BeReal — Deep Dive (vs MyFriends)

**Module:** friends
**Tier:** adjacent (friend-graph social network)
**Founded:** 2020
**HQ:** Paris, France
**Status:** acquired (Voodoo, June 2024, reported ~$500M)

## One-line
The "authentic moment" daily-prompt friend network that went viral in 2022, ran out of growth in 2023, and was acquired by mobile-game giant Voodoo mid-2024 — now navigating a quiet decline while Voodoo tries to monetize the friend graph.

## Product
- Once-daily prompt: take a photo from both cameras in 2 minutes
- Friends-only feed of BeReals; no feed algorithm, purely chronological
- RealMojis (photo reactions), comments
- Added late: BeReal Pro (creator accounts), music attachment, direct-message integration
- Platforms: iOS, Android
- Pricing: free, no subscription (post-acquisition testing ad experiments 2024-2025)
- Distinguishing UX: the enforced spontaneity window is the entire product

## Acquisition / Exit
- Acquired by: Voodoo (private, Paris, mobile-games giant known for hypercasual like Helix Jump)
- Date: 2024-06
- Price: ~$500M (~20% below Series B peak of $630M)
- Current status inside parent: standalone brand, experimenting with monetization (ads, creator tier, partnership tests)
- Strategic rationale: Voodoo wanted a viral consumer social property to apply its ad-monetization and game-publisher distribution muscle; BeReal's friend graph plus Voodoo's ad network was the thesis
- Founder outcome: Alexis Barreyat and Kevin Perreau both cashed out; Barreyat departed day-to-day operations by late 2024; Voodoo leadership installed

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Pre-acquisition**: nothing. Venture-funded pure user growth.
2. **Post-acquisition (Voodoo, 2024-2025)**: pilot ad formats, limited external API partnerships, creator verification tier tests.

Unit economics: the product mechanic (one post per day) fundamentally caps engagement density — this is both the brand's power and the reason ads didn't work pre-acquisition.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Single-photo social, no relationship memory:** BeReal gives you a daily photo of friends, nothing else. MyFriends logs hangouts, gifts, life events, memories.
- **Ephemeral engagement, no archive:** past BeReals are a Memories feed, not a queryable relationship record. MyFriends is queryable on-device forever.
- **Declining engagement:** BeReal's DAU collapsed post-2022 hype. Users are looking for durable friend tooling. MyFriends is the tool that fits the post-BeReal moment.
- **Voodoo ownership skepticism:** a mobile-game studio now owns your friend graph. MyFriends is local-first SQLite, no cloud profile to sell.
- **No life-event awareness:** BeReal does not surface birthdays, anniversaries, or nudges when a friendship is drifting. MyFriends' frequency + social-energy + nudges engines do.
