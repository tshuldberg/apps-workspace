# Surfline — Deep Dive (vs MySurf)

**Module:** surf
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 1985 (print) / 2001 (digital)
**HQ:** Huntington Beach, California, USA
**Status:** private (Wavetrak Inc.)

## One-line
The global default surf forecast platform, built on a moat of 700+ proprietary HD cams and Chernin Group capital, monetized via a $99.99/yr Premium paywall plus B2B coastal data.

## Product
- 15-day forecasts with swell, wind, tide, and buoy overlays
- 700+ live HD cameras at major breaks (exclusive moat)
- "Rewind" DVR to clip your own waves from cam feed
- Session logging via cam-match (auto-clip a wave when you surf in front of a cam)
- Reports and travel guides by regional editorial team
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web, Apple Watch, Apple TV
- Pricing: Free (limited) / Premium $9.99/mo or $99.99/yr; Team/Pro tiers exist for B2B
- Distinguishing UX choice: the live cam is the product, forecasts are the ornament

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Premium subscriptions** ($9.99/mo or $99.99/yr): unlocks 15-day forecast, cam rewind, HD cams, ad-free.
2. **B2B coastal data (Surfline Coastal Monitoring)**: municipalities, lifeguards, and Navy pay for cam + wave analytics.
3. **Advertising + sponsorships**: WSL, Red Bull, surf brand campaigns on forecast pages.

Unit economics: cams are capex but depreciate over 5+ years. Subscriptions are high-margin (>70% gross) once cam install is paid down.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Price**: $99.99/yr single-category vs MyLife $99/yr for 30+ modules.
- **Privacy**: Surfline tracks spot visits, sells aggregated coastal data. MySurf stores sessions locally, no ad network, no data broker.
- **Cam dependency**: Surfline's moat (cams) is also its trap — they are a media company with OPEX. MySurf intentionally omits cams (V5 migration deletes the table) and wins on pure forecast math + privacy.
- **Editorial inertia**: Surfline's regional editors are expensive; MySurf uses AI narrative generation per spot/region at near-zero marginal cost.
