# The Infatuation — Deep Dive (vs MyDining)

**Module:** dining
**Tier:** direct (discovery side)
**Founded:** 2009
**HQ:** New York, USA
**Status:** acquired (JPMorgan Chase, 2021) — subsidiary of Chase Media

## One-line
Editorially curated restaurant reviews by a full-time critic staff, acquired by JPMorgan to turn Chase Sapphire dining benefits into a media product.

## Product
- Editor-written reviews of ~8,000 restaurants across 30+ cities
- Guides ("Where to eat in Williamsburg on a Tuesday"), lists, Perfect For tags
- EEEEEATSCON food festival (live events)
- Reservation deep-links via SevenRooms/Resy/OpenTable
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: free; no consumer subscription
- Distinguishing UX: branded editorial voice that feels like a magazine, not Yelp

## Acquisition / Exit
- Acquired by: JPMorgan Chase & Co. (NYSE: JPM)
- Date: 2021-06
- Price: undisclosed (WSJ reported est. $50-80M)
- Current status inside parent: standalone brand inside Chase's Consumer & Community Banking media operations; key input into Chase Sapphire Reserve / Preferred dining benefits and Chase Media Solutions ad business
- Strategic rationale: JPMorgan wanted editorial dining content to justify the premium fee on Sapphire Reserve ($550+/yr card); also an input for Chase's nascent first-party retail-media business
- Founder outcome: Andrew Steinthal and Chris Stang both signed earn-out terms; Stang departed by 2023, Steinthal shortly after; current Infatuation editorial team runs under Chase Media

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Internal Chase marketing budget**: editorial content drives Sapphire Reserve dining perks, justifying the card's annual fee.
2. **Sponsored content / brand partnerships** on lists and guides (food brands, travel, spirits).
3. **Events (EEEEEATSCON)** ticket revenue + sponsor revenue.
4. **Reservation affiliate** (minor).

Unit economics not applicable in the same way — they are a cost center optimized for Chase card retention, not standalone P&L.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **No personal memory:** The Infatuation tells you where to go, never tracks where you went. MyDining is the diary layer they deliberately won't build (off-brand for media).
- **Editorial bias:** curated for sponsor/advertiser alignment; can't be honest about a Chase-partner restaurant that got worse.
- **No structured data:** reviews are prose; MyDining has structured dishes, wines, companions, photos, reservations.
- **US/English only:** global dining is a gap. MyDining is platform-agnostic.
- **No cross-module context:** MyDining pulls from email confirmations and links to MyRSVP events.
