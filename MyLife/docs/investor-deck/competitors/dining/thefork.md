# TheFork — Deep Dive (vs MyDining)

**Module:** dining
**Tier:** adjacent (reservations + dining log)
**Founded:** 2007 (as LaFourchette)
**HQ:** Paris, France
**Status:** acquired (Tripadvisor, 2014) — Tripadvisor owns; TRIP is public on NASDAQ

## One-line
Europe's dominant restaurant reservation platform (55K+ restaurants), owned by Tripadvisor, monetizing through per-cover fees and yield-pricing ("TheFork Pay").

## Product
- Reservation search and booking, discount deals ("50% off" promos)
- User reviews, photo menus, loyalty program ("Yums" points redeemable for discounts)
- TheFork Pay (in-app bill pay)
- TheFork Manager (B2B CRM/yield tool for restaurants)
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: free for diners; restaurants pay commission + software fees
- Distinguishing UX: discount-led discovery in Europe (OpenTable equivalent for EU)

## Acquisition / Exit
- Acquired by: Tripadvisor (NASDAQ: TRIP)
- Date: 2014-05
- Price: $140M cash
- Current status inside parent: standalone brand, now Tripadvisor's primary "Experiences & Dining" segment growth driver; rebranded LaFourchette -> TheFork in 2017
- Strategic rationale: Tripadvisor wanted a reservation + booking vertical to attach to its review platform; European restaurant density made TheFork the logical OpenTable alternative
- Founder outcome: Bertrand Jelensperger stayed on as TheFork CEO through 2019, then transitioned out; Denis Fayolle departed earlier to start other ventures

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Per-cover commission** ($1-3 per diner seated via TheFork) — the OpenTable model.
2. **TheFork Manager** SaaS subscription ($100-300/mo per restaurant) for yield, CRM, POS integration.
3. **TheFork Pay** bill-pay takes a percentage on each transaction.
4. **Featured listings / promo placement** for restaurants willing to pay for visibility.

Unit economics: restaurants pay ~€3/cover net; EU dining market volume × take rate drives revenue. Stable, mature.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Reservation-first, not memory-first:** TheFork optimizes booking funnels, not memories. MyDining is the post-meal diary that reservations apps structurally can't become (conflicts with B2B incentives).
- **No companion/wine data:** reservations don't care who you ate with or what you drank. MyDining treats both as first-class entities.
- **Data-sharing with restaurants:** TheFork shares diner profiles with partner restaurants for CRM. MyDining keeps diner data on-device.
- **Promo-heavy bias:** TheFork steers you to restaurants running discounts, not the best restaurants for you.
- **Email-import interop:** MyDining parses TheFork/OpenTable/Resy confirmations, so we sit on top of the user's booking stack, not against it.
