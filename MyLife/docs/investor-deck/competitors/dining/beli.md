# Beli — Deep Dive (vs MyDining)

**Module:** dining
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2021
**HQ:** New York, USA
**Status:** private (VC-backed)

## One-line
A friends-ranked restaurant list app built on head-to-head comparisons, positioned as the Gen-Z replacement for Yelp and Google reviews.

## Product
- Pairwise restaurant ranking (A vs B vs C) produces a personal 1-10 score per spot
- Social feed of friends' visits and scores; "want to try" wishlist
- City leaderboards, trending lists, restaurant recommendations
- Reservation deep-links (OpenTable, Resy)
- Platforms: iOS, Android (limited), no web app of substance
- Pricing: free, ad-free; "Beli Plus" $49.99/yr (2025) for advanced filters, unlimited comparisons
- Distinguishing UX: the forced comparison loop that makes your score genuinely reflect preference

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Affiliate/referral** on reservations booked via OpenTable and Resy deep-links (small per-cover kickback).
2. **Beli Plus** subscription $49.99/yr launched 2025 (filters, unlimited comparisons, advanced recs).
3. **B2B restaurant marketing** hinted in 2025 press but not yet monetized.

Unit economics dependent on subscription conversion; without ads, Beli needs 5-10% Plus conversion to hit meaningful ARR.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Social graph dependency:** Beli is useless without friends on it. MyDining works solo as a private diary first, social later.
- **No private mode:** every visit feeds the friend feed. MyDining defaults to private with optional sharing.
- **No dish-level memory:** Beli ranks restaurants, not what you actually ate. MyDining tracks dishes, wines, companions.
- **No email import:** Beli requires manual entry. MyDining parses OpenTable/Resy confirmation emails automatically.
- **Suite bundle:** Beli is a standalone subscription eating into a user's discretionary app budget.
