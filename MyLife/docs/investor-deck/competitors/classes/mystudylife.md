# My Study Life — Deep Dive (vs MyClasses)

**Module:** classes
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2013
**HQ:** UK
**Status:** private (indie, My Study Life Ltd)

## One-line
A cloud-synced student planner for schedules, homework, and exams; one of the largest free student planners by install base.

## Product
- Class schedule (rotation-aware, supports flip weeks)
- Homework, exam, revision task tracking
- Cloud sync across iOS, Android, Web
- Grade tracking (added 2023)
- Pomodoro timer (added 2024)
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web (PWA)
- Pricing: free (ad-supported on mobile), Premium ~$3.99/mo or ~$24.99/yr
- Distinguishing UX choice: rotation-schedule support (UK/Commonwealth schools with alternating-week timetables)

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Premium subscription** (monthly/annual): removes ads, adds themes, adds widgets, unlimited grade history.
2. **In-app ads** on free mobile tier.
3. No data monetization reported; privacy policy basic.

Unit economics: low ARPU but very low COGS on cloud-synced JSON docs.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Single feature scope**: schedule + homework + exams only. MyClasses adds degree planner, application tracker (college/internship), lifelong learning, Pomodoro, time tracker, office hours, commute — all free tier.
- **Cloud-dependent**: My Study Life requires an account and server sync. MyClasses is local-first.
- **No grade projection or degree-requirement satisfaction**: MyClasses models requirements and tracks satisfaction toward a diploma, not just GPA.
- **No application pipeline**: MyClasses tracks college + internship + grad-school applications with tasks — My Study Life has nothing for seniors.
