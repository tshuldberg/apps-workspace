# Chegg — Deep Dive (vs MyClasses)

**Module:** classes
**Tier:** adjacent (study tools rather than planner)
**Founded:** 2005
**HQ:** Santa Clara, CA, USA
**Status:** public (NYSE: CHGG)

## One-line
The public ed-tech giant — textbook rentals, homework help, tutors, and a study-app franchise under siege from ChatGPT.

## Product
- Chegg Study (homework Q&A library, expert answers)
- Chegg Study Pack (includes grammar, tutoring, flashcards)
- Textbook rental + eTextbooks
- Tutoring, CheggMate (GPT-4 integration launched 2023)
- Limited scheduling / to-do features
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: Chegg Study $19.95/mo; Study Pack $24.95/mo
- Distinguishing UX choice: massive expert-answer library (~100M questions) tied to textbook ISBNs

## Acquisition / Exit
Independent as of 2026-04 but actively exploring strategic alternatives including a possible take-private or acquisition (publicly disclosed 2025). Most recent fundraise: $1B+ in convertible notes (2019, 2021). Market cap ~$120M vs peak ~$12B — a 99% drawdown driven by ChatGPT commoditizing homework help.

- Geography: US ~80%, International ~20%

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Chegg Services subscriptions** ($19.95-$24.95/mo): homework Q&A, expert answers, grammar, tutoring credits.
2. **Textbook rentals + eTextbooks** (declining).
3. **Skills content** (coding bootcamps, Busuu language acquired 2022).

Unit economics: subscription ARPU ~$180/yr declining as discount promos spike to stem churn; CAC rising as ChatGPT hollows out homework-help TAM.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Not a planner**: Chegg tracks purchased help, not a student's schedule. MyClasses is the planner layer Chegg has never built.
- **ChatGPT decimation**: Chegg shed 43% of subs in 18 months. Students are migrating away; MyClasses becomes the planner in a post-Chegg world.
- **Public-company pressure**: aggressive monetization likely as CHGG fights to hit guidance. MyClasses stays flat-priced in the suite.
- **Academic-integrity risk**: schools are blocking/auditing Chegg use. MyClasses does not host answer banks — zero academic-integrity exposure.
