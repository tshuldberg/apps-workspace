# Sofascore — Deep Dive (vs MySports)

**Module:** sports
**Tier:** direct (live scores, global sports coverage)
**Founded:** 2010
**HQ:** Zagreb, Croatia
**Status:** private (bootstrapped / quiet)

## One-line
Europe's global-sports live-score app of choice — soccer-first, with 25M+ MAU, deep stats on 20+ sports, a reputation for speed, and an independent bootstrapped trajectory rare in the category.

## Product
- Live scores across soccer (primary), tennis, basketball, cricket, ice hockey, handball, volleyball, MMA, F1, baseball, NFL
- Lineups, player heatmaps, Sofascore Rating per player (proprietary 0-10 scale)
- Detailed stats, h2h, form analysis, league tables
- Push notifications per team/match
- Sofascore Editor (paid community contributor program)
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web
- **Pricing:** Free (ad-supported); Sofascore Premium $2.99/mo or $19.99/yr (ad removal + extras)
- **Distinguishing UX:** the most comprehensive soccer coverage globally; lightning-fast live updates

## Acquisition / Exit
Independent as of 2026-04. Most recent round: none (bootstrapped since 2010). No known acquisition talks in public record; at ~$40M revenue and strong profitability, acquirers like Stats Perform, Genius Sports, or Opta could find it strategic. Founders have stated no interest in VC to date.

## Users
- 25M+ MAU globally (company-reported)
- ~1M paid subscribers estimated
- Geography: strong in EU (esp. Balkans, Southern Europe, UK), growing LatAm, MENA, APAC
- Conversion: ~4-5% free → paid

## How they make money (precise)
Primary: advertising — heavy use of betting operator banners (bet365, Betway, Unibet), especially in soccer coverage where users are high-intent. Secondary: Premium subscription at $20/yr × ~1M subs = ~$20M ARR. Tertiary: Sofascore Data API licensing to sportsbooks and media partners (Opta/Stats-Perform-adjacent product). Unit economics favorable — low server costs per user, bootstrapped discipline.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Heavy gambling ad presence:** Sofascore screens are full of bet365 / Betway banners. MyLife MySports explicitly rejects gambling funnels.
- **US coverage is thin:** Sofascore is soccer-first; NFL/NBA/MLB depth lags ESPN. MyLife MySports covers all four US majors equally.
- **Privacy:** Sofascore user tracking feeds ad targeting via exchanges. MyLife team-following is local-only.
- **Single-purpose:** Sofascore has no social or personal-sports play layer. MyLife connects to Friends for group watch and logs your own pickup games.
- **Subscription value:** $20/yr buys ad removal; MyLife suite at comparable price gives scores + 29 modules ad-free by default.
