# ESPN — Deep Dive (vs MySports)

**Module:** sports
**Tier:** direct (scoreboard + news dominant player)
**Founded:** 1979
**HQ:** Bristol, CT
**Status:** subsidiary (The Walt Disney Company, NYSE: DIS)

## One-line
The default US sports app — 30M+ monthly app users, scores across every major league, plus ESPN+ streaming, Fantasy, and now ESPN Bet (via Penn Entertainment JV).

## Product
- Live scores, stats, standings, schedules across NFL/NBA/MLB/NHL/MLS/F1/college
- News, podcasts, video highlights, breaking alerts
- ESPN Fantasy (football, baseball, basketball, hockey)
- ESPN+ streaming subscription integrated in-app
- ESPN Bet sportsbook (where legal) via Penn partnership
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web, Apple TV, Roku, consoles, Apple Watch
- **Pricing:** Free (ad-supported) core; ESPN+ $11.99/mo or $119.99/yr; bundles with Disney+
- **Distinguishing UX:** deepest US coverage, official league data, Fantasy integration is sticky

## Acquisition / Exit
- Already acquired: ESPN has been 80% Disney / 20% Hearst since 1995 (following Disney's $19B acquisition of Capital Cities/ABC, which had owned ESPN since 1984).
- Current status: Flagship of Disney's Sports segment; Hearst retains 20% minority interest
- No known plans to spin off or divest; 2023 rumors of Disney selling stake to strategic (Apple, Amazon) did not materialize; ESPN Bet JV (Penn) signed instead.
- Founder outcome: Bill Rasmussen exited 1984, later advised various sports-media startups

## Users
- App: 30M+ MAU in US
- ESPN+ subscribers: 26M+ (Q4 FY2024)
- ESPN.com: 100M+ unique monthly visitors
- Geography: US ~85%, international minor
- Conversion: ~12-15% of app MAU convert to ESPN+ subscribers

## How they make money (precise)
Primary: cable affiliate fees (~$9-10 per subscriber per month × 70M+ pay-TV homes) — single largest revenue line at ~$8B+ annually, but declining. Secondary: advertising across linear TV, digital, and app inventory. Tertiary: ESPN+ D2C subscriptions at $120/yr × 26M = ~$3B ARR. Emerging: ESPN Bet licensing fees, sponsorship, commerce/betting affiliate. Unit economics are protected by long-term league rights deals (NFL, NBA, MLB — $10B+/yr committed).

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Ads everywhere:** ESPN app shows pre-roll video ads, banner ads, ESPN Bet prompts across every screen. MyLife MySports has zero ads.
- **Paywall pressure:** ESPN increasingly locks content behind ESPN+ ($120/yr). MyLife keeps scores free, no upsell.
- **Gambling + fantasy upselling:** ESPN leverages dashboard real estate for ESPN Bet and Fantasy lead-gen. Uncomfortable for many users — MyLife is conscientiously non-gambling.
- **League-data coupling:** ESPN can be pressured by league partners (NFL, NBA) re: content displayed. MyLife aggregates from free public sources, no content politics.
- **No personal play logging:** ESPN does not track your own pickup hoops games, kids' little-league, or intramural leagues. MyLife does.
- **Bundle fatigue:** ESPN+ $120/yr + Disney+ + Hulu = $30+/mo. MyLife suite at $7-10/mo includes sports + 29 modules.
