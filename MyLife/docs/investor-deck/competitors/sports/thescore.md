# theScore — Deep Dive (vs MySports)

**Module:** sports
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 1994 (Score Media), mobile pivot ~2012
**HQ:** Toronto, Canada
**Status:** subsidiary (acquired by Penn Entertainment, 2021)

## One-line
Canada's favorite mobile-first sports scoreboard app — clean UX, followed-team dashboards, now bundled into Penn's gambling ecosystem as the top-funnel for theScore Bet.

## Product
- Real-time scores, stats, standings across NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, NCAA, MLS, soccer, golf, UFC, F1
- Personalized teams + alert rules (close game, scoring, OT, injury)
- News feed, player stats, box scores
- theScore Bet (sportsbook, adults 19+/21+ only where legal)
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web, Apple Watch
- **Pricing:** Free (ad-supported). theScore Bet monetizes separately.
- **Distinguishing UX:** cleanest "just scores" app; no forced video, no paywall

## Acquisition / Exit
- Acquired by: Penn Entertainment (NASDAQ: PENN)
- Date: 2021-10
- Price: $2B ($500M cash + 17M Penn shares; mark-to-market peaked near $3B briefly post-deal)
- Current status inside parent: rebranded funnel (app) + Penn Interactive sportsbook segment
- Strategic rationale: Canadian market entry + US sportsbook user funnel + media/content moat
- Founder outcome: John Levy continued post-acquisition as Chairman of theScore; brother Aubrey Levy (CMO) retained; largely cashed out

- Conversion: N/A for core app; theScore Bet conversion est. 2-4% of app users in legal states

## How they make money (precise)
Direct app: display and video advertising from sportsbooks (DraftKings, FanDuel), beer/liquor, auto, telecom. Post-Penn acquisition, the app's strategic purpose is driving theScore Bet sign-ups — a legal-state user funnel worth $500-1000 LTV per bettor. Penn reports consolidated, so app-level revenue is not broken out.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Gambling-adjacent everywhere:** theScore's post-acquisition UX has expanding bet integrations; many users uncomfortable with gambling prompts. MyLife MySports is explicitly "no ads, no sponsored gambling funnels."
- **Ads in your dashboard:** theScore shows multiple ad placements per screen. MyLife has zero ads.
- **Single-purpose:** theScore is sports-only. MyLife embeds sports into suite (with Friends for group-watching, RSVP for tailgates, Budget for ticket spend).
- **Canada-centric:** US users have ESPN/Yahoo as stronger defaults; theScore's wedge narrows. MyLife is neutral on region.
- **Parent company conflict:** Penn's business depends on gambling conversion; if that fails, app product investment may suffer. MyLife aligns with user, not with a sportsbook.
