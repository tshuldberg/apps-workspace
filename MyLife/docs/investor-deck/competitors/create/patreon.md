# Patreon — Deep Dive (vs MyCreate)

**Module:** create
**Tier:** adjacent (creator monetization + CRM)
**Founded:** 2013
**HQ:** San Francisco, USA
**Status:** private (VC-backed, late-stage)

## One-line
The original creator-subscription platform — monthly memberships for patrons, with posts, tiers, and community tools for creators across every craft.

## Product
- Creator pages with tiers and subscriptions
- Posts (text, audio, video, images, polls)
- Direct messaging, community, chats
- Shop (digital + physical goods)
- Creator CRM and analytics
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing (to creators): 8% Pro / 12% Premium of earnings + payment processing; Free tier basic
- Distinguishing UX choice: recurring monthly or per-creation patronage, tier unlocking

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Take-rate** on creator earnings: Pro 8%, Premium 12%, Free tier has restrictions.
2. **Payment processing** passthrough (2.9% + $0.30 per pledge, mostly pass-through to Stripe).
3. **Commerce** (Shop) — take rate on digital/physical goods.

Unit economics: marketplace gross margin ~25-35% after payments + support + fraud; volatility driven by top creators leaving for alternatives (Substack, Ko-fi, OnlyFans).

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Monetization-only**: Patreon is a billing platform, not a craft-tracking tool. MyCreate tracks projects, progress, practice, equipment — data Patreon never holds.
- **Public-first identity**: a Patreon page requires a public persona. MyCreate works private-first.
- **Take-rate cost**: 8-12% forever. MyCreate is a flat $99/yr suite line item.
- **Integration opportunity**: MyCreate can export portfolio pieces to Patreon/Shopify/Ghost — cooperative rather than competitive.
