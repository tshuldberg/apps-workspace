# Streaks — Deep Dive (vs MyHabits)

**Module:** habits
**Tier:** direct (iOS habit tracker)
**Founded:** 2015 (app launch)
**HQ:** Adelaide, Australia
**Status:** private (indie studio: Crunchy Bagel, solo founder Quentin Zervaas)

## One-line
Apple-native habit tracker built around a 12-habit grid and streak-or-die discipline; an Apple Design Award winner with a one-time purchase model.

## Product
- Track up to 24 habits (12 active) in a tile grid; complete or reset streak
- Apple Watch + Shortcuts + Siri + Health auto-complete (steps, exercise mins)
- Negative habits ("at most X times"), task habits, timed habits
- Pricing: $5.99 one-time IAP (no subscription)
- Platforms: iOS + iPadOS + watchOS + macOS (Apple-only)

## How they make money (precise)
1. **App Store one-time purchase** ($5.99): 100% of revenue. Apple takes 15-30%; rest is net.
2. **Sibling app "Little Streaks"** (kids habits): separate $4.99 purchase.
No recurring revenue. No ads. Streaks is a case study in sustainable indie — Apple Design Award + word-of-mouth = steady trickle with near-zero CAC.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **iOS-only.** No Android, no web. MyHabits ships on both and web.
- **12-habit ceiling.** Streaks is deliberately minimal; cannot track sobriety, cravings, Pomodoro, billable time, pet companion, or badges. MyHabits has 26 mobile routes across RPG + focus + time-tracking.
- **No challenges, no stacking, no areas.** Streaks is a lone tile grid. MyHabits has habit stacks, streak freezes, grace periods, 8 built-in programs.
- **No export, no cross-module correlation.** Streaks data is siloed; MyHabits participates in the hub's cross-module Pearson engine.
