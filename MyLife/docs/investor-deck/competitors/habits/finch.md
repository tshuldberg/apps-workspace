# Finch — Deep Dive (vs MyHabits)

**Module:** habits
**Tier:** direct (gamified habits + self-care)
**Founded:** 2021
**HQ:** Santa Clara, CA
**Status:** private (bootstrapped, Public Benefit Corporation)

## One-line
Self-care pet app where daily habits feed a bird character; best-in-class D30 retention beating Duolingo/Calm/Headspace.

## Product
- Daily self-care goals, mood check-ins, reflections, breathing exercises
- Bird pet evolves by completing tasks; unlock outfits, adventures, insights
- Soft-UI pastel aesthetic; beloved by Gen Z + mental health community
- Pricing: free core; Finch Plus $39.99/yr (premium goals, insights, customization)
- Platforms: iOS, Android (no web)

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Finch Plus subscription** ($39.99/yr or ~$7/mo): ~100% of revenue. Unlocks premium goals, new outfits, deeper insights, adventure chapters.
2. **No IAP gem packs** (rare for a pet-game): prices skin customization via subscription only.
Unit economics: modest ARPPU (~$40/yr); massive install base + organic word-of-mouth keeps CAC near zero.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Android + iOS only.** No web, no desktop, no export. MyHabits has web + mobile + CSV export.
- **Mental-health-only framing.** Finch cannot credibly onboard a user tracking sobriety, time-billable projects, or gym PRs. MyHabits ships sobriety clock + Pomodoro + billable time + 37 badges.
- **No sobriety depth, no HealthKit, no Siri.** Finch is onboarding a mental-health user; MyHabits serves the full habit/productivity/recovery stack in one module.
- **Bundle price.** $99/yr MyLife suite vs $39.99/yr Finch plus buying Toggl, I Am Sober, and a Pomodoro app separately ($180+/yr).
