## One-line
The minimalist, Apple Watch-first pill reminder that set the design benchmark for the category — now quietly sunsetting but still referenced as the "clean UX" standard.

## Product
- Simple pill reminders with flexible "window" semantics (take within a 1-2 hour band, not a specific minute)
- Apple Watch companion (first-class, not secondary)
- Skip/snooze/taken on-watch
- Pricing: free, no subscription, no ads (as designed)
- Platforms: iOS + Apple Watch

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Single-purpose, single-platform, possibly defunct.** Round Health never grew into meds + vitals + symptoms + doctor report. MyMeds already spans all four.
- **No data model beyond reminders.** Round Health has no interaction checker, no refill tracker burn-rate, no caregiver alerts. MyMeds ships all three.
- **No cross-module value.** Round Health is iOS stand-alone. MyMeds participates in the hub alongside journal, mood, health, habits.
- **Strategic note:** Round Health is more a reminder (no pun intended) to investors that minimal UX is a table-stakes requirement — MyMeds must not be "feature-rich but ugly." Use the Cool Obsidian design system to match Round Health's polish while shipping 10x the functionality.
- **MyMeds roadmap gap:** RxNorm integration is scoped but not yet shipped. That gap is minor relative to Round Health's total feature set, but noted honestly for investor transparency.
