# Forest — Deep Dive (vs MyPresence)

**Module:** presence
**Tier:** direct (focus + gamified phone avoidance)
**Founded:** 2014
**HQ:** Taichung, Taiwan
**Status:** private (bootstrapped, Seekrtech)

## One-line
The OG gamified focus app: plant a virtual tree, don't touch your phone until it grows, and the company donates to Trees for the Future when you buy coins — a decade-old brand with persistent #1 productivity rankings.

## Product
- Focus timer: plant a tree, leave the app = tree dies
- Coins earned for completed sessions, spent on tree species + real-tree donations
- Forest (multiplayer collaborative sessions)
- Daily achievements, friends, leaderboards
- Chrome/Firefox extension (websites blocker as tree-growing sessions)
- Apple Watch app
- Platforms: iOS ($3.99 one-time), Android (free with Pro IAP $1.99), Chrome, Firefox
- Pricing: iOS $3.99 one-time (primary model); Android free with $1.99 Pro IAP; real-tree donations $0.99/tree via coin packs
- Distinguishing UX choice: paid-once on iOS (counter-cyclical to subscription) + real-world environmental impact hook

## How they make money (precise)
1. **iOS paid app** ($3.99 one-time): single biggest line; unusual in 2025 to not be subscription.
2. **Android IAP coins** (various prices $0.99-$9.99): unlock tree species, extensions.
3. **Charitable plant-a-real-tree coins** ($0.99 = 1 real tree): ~20% of coin revenue; Seekrtech keeps margin, forwards rest to Trees for the Future.
4. **White-label / B2B partnerships** (occasional educational deals).
5. No ads, no data licensing.

Unit economics: a $3.99 one-time on iOS is classic pre-subscription pricing — LTV capped but CAC ~0 from App Store ranking + virality.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **No sessions analytics depth:** Forest counts trees; it doesn't surface weekend pattern, morning-pickup trend, time-of-day optimization, or goal regression the way MyPresence insight engine does.
- **No accountability or commitment contracts:** Forest has social planting but no stakes, contracts, or shared goals with revocation. MyPresence ships full accountability + rewards + commitment.
- **Lean team, slow iteration:** Forest's core mechanic is unchanged since 2015. MyPresence ships schema V2 + 5 new engines in one year.
- **Hasn't modernized pricing:** $3.99 one-time caps per-user revenue; no upgrade path. MyLife is a flat suite subscription.
- **No cross-module intelligence:** Forest sessions don't inform MyHabits or MyMood. MyPresence passes session data to MyHabits (focus-streak habit) and MyMood (focus-and-mood correlation).
- **Single category, sentimental moat:** users love the virtual tree but cannot migrate their years of trees elsewhere. MyPresence ships CSV export from day one.
