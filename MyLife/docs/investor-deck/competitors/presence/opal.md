# Opal — Deep Dive (vs MyPresence)

**Module:** presence
**Tier:** direct (digital-wellbeing / focus-sessions leader)
**Founded:** 2020
**HQ:** San Francisco + Paris
**Status:** private (seed-funded, $10M ARR)

## One-line
The breakout screen-time app that turned iOS Screen Time APIs into a premium focus-session product; proves consumers will pay $70-100/yr to block their own apps with design polish.

## Product
- App blocking via Screen Time + Family Controls entitlements
- Deep Focus sessions (hard-locked, requires breaking-glass friction to unlock)
- Scheduled blocks, bedtime mode, work/school profiles
- Time tracking by app, weekly reports, streaks, badges
- Leaderboards + Groups (accountability)
- Opal Body mode (movement prompts during long sits)
- Platforms: iOS only (required: Family Controls entitlement)
- Pricing: free 7-day trial / Opal Pro $69.99/yr or $9.99/mo; Opal Premium $99.99/yr (new tier)
- Distinguishing UX choice: Deep Focus — intentional friction designed so you cannot casually unblock

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Opal Pro** ($69.99/yr or $9.99/mo): all blocking features, unlimited sessions, Leaderboards, Insights.
2. **Opal Premium** ($99.99/yr, newer tier): advanced sessions + additional features.
3. **Deep Focus unlock friction**: not a revenue line directly, but behavioral design that drives retention and therefore LTV.

Unit economics: iOS App Store takes 15-30%; gross margin ~75% after Apple cut; CAC mostly organic via TikTok/Instagram virality.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **iOS only:** Opal requires Family Controls entitlement and has no Android app. MyPresence is cross-platform.
- **No accountability-partner stakes:** Opal has groups + leaderboards but no commitment contracts with financial or social stakes. MyPresence ships commitment contracts + share codes + revocation + rewards.
- **Single-purpose silo:** Opal is focus-only. MyPresence integrates with MyHabits (streak counts as habit), MyWorkouts (sessions scheduled around gym), MyMood (phone-usage correlates with mood).
- **Paywall-aggressive:** Opal moved multiple basic features behind Premium in 2024-2025. MyPresence suite-includes everything.
- **No local AI:** Opal uses no on-device ML. MyPresence insight engine runs weekend-pattern + morning-pickup + goal-regression detectors locally.
- **Pricing:** Opal Pro = $70/yr for one focus app; MyLife = $99/yr for 30 modules.
