# 11pets — Deep Dive (vs MyPets)

**Module:** pets
**Tier:** direct (consumer pet-health diary)
**Founded:** 2015
**HQ:** Nicosia, Cyprus
**Status:** private (indie, bootstrapped)

## One-line
The reference consumer pet-health diary: vaccinations, medications, weight, vet visits, grooming — beautifully organized, cross-platform, and cheap; MyPets's closest feature-for-feature competitor.

## Product
- Multi-pet profiles (dogs, cats, exotics)
- Vaccination tracker + reminders (pre-seeded core schedules)
- Medications, flea/tick treatments, deworming with due-date engine
- Weight log, body condition, feeding logs, expenses
- Vet visit history, documents storage, photo gallery
- Sharing with vet / family / sitter
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: free (core) / Premium ~$1.99/mo or ~$19.99/yr
- Distinguishing UX choice: an insanely low price point for a very complete pet-health dataset — built by ex-SAP engineers in Cyprus

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Standalone app vs suite:** 11pets is pets only. MyPets ties expense tracking into MyBudget, vet reminders into MyHub notifications, photos into MyNotes.
- **No insurance, no budget:** 11pets tracks expenses per entry but has no budget categories or insurance claim tracking. MyPets ships 8 expense categories + insurance policies + claims + expense budgets (V4).
- **No body-condition scoring:** 11pets logs weight but not 1-9 BCS (veterinary standard). MyPets ships BCS.
- **Cross-module intelligence:** nothing in 11pets connects pet walks to MyWorkouts or pet food cost to MyBudget. MyPets is the suite's pet surface.
- **Suite bundle:** $20/yr for 11pets Premium vs $99/yr for 30 modules.
- **Lean team means slow iteration:** 11pets has been on V9 of its data model for 8 years. MyPets shipped schema V1-V4 within the first year.
