# Tractive — Deep Dive (vs MyPets)

**Module:** pets
**Tier:** adjacent (hardware + subscription GPS, pending Bending Spoons acquisition)
**Founded:** 2012
**HQ:** Pasching, Austria
**Status:** private (Bending Spoons acquisition announced March 2026, closing Q2 2026)

## One-line
The dominant European GPS pet tracker — hardware collar + monthly subscription — now being rolled up by Bending Spoons in a EUR 300-500M deal, the most lucrative pet-tech exit to date.

## Product
- GPS collar hardware (LTE + Bluetooth) for dogs and cats
- Real-time location, activity tracking, sleep tracking, wellness alerts
- Virtual fence, escape alerts, location history, family sharing
- Tractive Vet Chat (24/7 vet access) in premium tiers
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web (hardware is the moat)
- Pricing: hardware ~$50-$99 one-time + subscription $5-$13/mo depending on tier (Premium / Premium+)
- Distinguishing UX choice: collar hardware + ARR subscription yields $100+/yr/pet ARPU vs pure-software pet apps at $2/mo

## Acquisition / Exit
- Acquired by: Bending Spoons (private, Milan, Italy)
- Date: 2026-03 (announced), closing expected Q2 2026 pending regulatory approval
- Price: EUR 300-500M (reported range; cash + mixed consideration)
- Current status inside parent: not yet closed; Bending Spoons typically maintains standalone brand while consolidating operations
- Strategic rationale: Bending Spoons' first major hardware + subscription asset; subscription-heavy recurring-revenue business fits their Evernote/Meetup/WeTransfer rollup playbook; EUR 105M+ ARR at acquisition is cash-flow anchor
- Founder outcome: Michael Hurnaus expected to stay through closing per Mainsights; post-acquisition role N/D (Bending Spoons historically replaces leadership within 12-18 months)

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Monthly/annual subscription** ($5-$13/mo depending on tier and contract length): the real revenue engine.
2. **Hardware sales** (collars ~$50-99 + accessories): near-cost unit sales because subscription is the LTV driver.
3. **Vet Chat premium** and wellness features in top tier.
4. **Battery / charger / band replacements** (accessories margin).

Unit economics: hardware near break-even; 3-year subscription LTV est. $250-400/pet at 70%+ gross margin on the sub line.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Hardware lock-in:** Tractive requires their collar — you can't bring your own GPS. MyPets is software-only and integrates with any connected collar or none at all (multi-hardware optional).
- **Tracking-only product:** Tractive does not track vaccinations, medications, weight trends, expenses, or insurance. MyPets ships all of that plus imports location data (future integration).
- **Subscription required forever:** if you cancel, the hardware is a brick. MyPets data survives without subscription; suite includes pets for flat $99/yr.
- **Under new ownership (Bending Spoons):** pricing + feature cuts likely (Evernote pattern). MyPets is consumer-aligned, not PE-aligned.
- **Suite integration:** Tractive walk data is a silo. MyPets feeds exercise logs to MyWorkouts and MyHealth activity.
