# Rocket Money — Deep Dive (vs MyBudget)

**Module:** budget
**Tier:** direct (subscription-management subset)
**Founded:** 2015 (as Truebill)
**HQ:** Silver Spring, MD, USA

## One-line
Subscription-cancellation concierge plus budgeting and bill negotiation, rebranded from Truebill into Rocket Companies' fintech bundle.

## Product
- Subscription detection and one-tap cancellation
- Bill negotiation ("we will negotiate your cable bill")
- Smart Savings (automated transfers to a partner-bank account)
- Budgeting + net worth + credit score
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: Free tier + Premium ($6-$12/mo "pay what you want", gates concierge cancellation)
- Distinguishing UX choice: "we'll cancel it for you" concierge UX — Rocket handles the phone/email hassle

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Premium subscription** ($6-$12/mo "pay what you want"): unlocks concierge cancellation, autopilot savings, unlimited budgets.
2. **Bill negotiation fees**: 40% of first-year savings on any negotiated bill (cable, internet, cell).
3. **Smart Savings float** (minor): interest earned on pooled user savings held at partner banks.
4. **Rocket Mortgage cross-sell**: mortgage refinance referral to parent.

Unit economics: ~$50-80 ARPU blended across free + paid; CAC subsidized by Rocket parent's massive Super Bowl-scale brand spend.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Financial ecosystem lock-in**: Rocket Money funnels to Rocket Mortgage — you are a lead, not a customer. MyBudget has no parent with a mortgage book.
- **Concierge is their moat**: MyBudget doesn't cancel subs for you — but it ships a 215-entry catalog, cancellation action log, and deep links. For users comfortable cancelling themselves (90% of subs), this is equivalent.
- **Data exposure**: Rocket Money reads every bank transaction and routes through Rocket Companies infrastructure, a public company obligated to maximize shareholder value via data + cross-sell. MyBudget is local-first.
- **No investment tracking**: Rocket Money ignores investment accounts; MyBudget tracks holdings + price history.
