# YNAB — Deep Dive (vs MyBudget)

**Module:** budget
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2004
**HQ:** Lehi, Utah, USA
**Status:** private (bootstrapped, no VC)

## One-line
The gold standard for zero-based envelope budgeting; rabid community, premium pricing, no investors to answer to.

## Product
- Zero-based budgeting with envelope "categories"
- Bank sync (US + Canada), Target Category goals, monthly rollover, "Age of Money" metric
- Reports (Spending, Income vs Expense, Net Worth)
- Loan planner (2024), goal tracking
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web (responsive)
- Pricing: $14.99/mo or $109/yr; 34-day free trial; free for students
- Distinguishing UX choice: the Four Rules methodology ("Give Every Dollar a Job") enforced throughout UX

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Annual/monthly subscriptions** ($109/yr or $14.99/mo): unlimited accounts, unlimited devices, teaching content library.
2. **Corporate/workshop program** (minor): employer-sponsored seats.
3. No ads. No referral fees. No data monetization. Aggressive privacy stance.

Unit economics: very high — subscription business with low hosting cost per user (~$5-8/yr inferred) and no CAC subsidy from VC money; heavy dependence on content marketing + word of mouth.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Price**: $109/yr for one budgeting app vs $99/yr for the whole MyLife suite. For a household, MyBudget delivers 85-90% feature parity at a fraction of the cost.
- **Cloud lock-in**: YNAB data lives on YNAB's servers; export is CSV-only. MyBudget is a single local SQLite file the user owns.
- **Investment + net worth + loans + debt payoff**: YNAB added loan planner in 2024 but still doesn't ship net-worth snapshots or investment holdings natively. MyBudget does.
- **Subscription catalog**: YNAB surfaces recurring transactions but has no Rocket-Money-style subscription catalog with cancellation actions. MyBudget has a 215-entry catalog.
