# Sanctuary — Deep Dive (vs MyStars)

**Module:** stars
**Tier:** direct (astrology + live-reader marketplace)
**Founded:** 2019
**HQ:** New York, NY
**Status:** private (VC-backed)

## One-line
Sanctuary pairs AI astrology content with a human psychic/astrologer marketplace — on-demand paid readings via chat with vetted readers, blending tech and two-sided marketplace dynamics.

## Product
- Daily horoscope, natal chart, compatibility
- Live Readings: pay-per-minute chat with vetted astrologers, tarot readers, psychic mediums
- Guided courses, podcasts, astro-content library
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web
- **Pricing:** Free content; Sanctuary Monthly $19.99/mo or $199/yr; Live Readings ~$4-10/min separately
- **Distinguishing UX:** human expert as a service — closest to Keen.com / California Psychics for Gen Z mobile audience

## How they make money (precise)
Primary: marketplace take-rate on Live Readings — readers charge $4-10/minute, Sanctuary keeps ~30%. Average session 15-30 min = $60-300 gross per session, $20-100 to Sanctuary. Secondary: monthly subscription for AI content + discounts on readings. Tertiary: astrology course bundles (like MasterClass for woo). Unit economics depend heavily on reader supply and repeat-reading conversion.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Marketplace economics:** Sanctuary's live-reading cost can exceed $200/session — aimed at high-spend believers. MyLife stars is a flat sub for all cosmic content.
- **Reader quality variance:** marketplace vetting is imperfect; bad readings damage brand. MyLife computes on-device — no variable-quality human in the loop.
- **Anxiety monetization:** pay-per-minute during relationship crises is ethically fraught. MyLife doesn't exploit users in emotional states.
- **No journal integration:** Sanctuary sessions evaporate after the call. MyLife owns an astrology journal persistent locally.
- **Privacy:** Sanctuary logs every reading conversation; users sharing deep personal context. MyLife keeps all chart + journal data local.
- **Two-sided marketplace brittleness:** if readers leave for competing platforms (Keen, Psychic Source), app experience collapses.
