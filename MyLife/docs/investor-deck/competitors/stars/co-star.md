# Co–Star — Deep Dive (vs MyStars)

**Module:** stars
**Tier:** direct (category leader)
**Founded:** 2017
**HQ:** New York, NY
**Status:** private (VC-backed)

## One-line
The Gen Z astrology juggernaut — AI-generated daily horoscopes with a distinct typographic aesthetic and NASA orbital-data calculation that turned astrology into a social messaging medium for 30M+ users.

## Product
- Birth chart with sun/moon/rising + full natal chart
- Daily push horoscope ("the void wants your 8pm")
- Friend compatibility, synastry, group charts
- Transits, planetary movements, retrograde alerts
- Chat feature for sending horoscopes to friends
- **Platforms:** iOS (primary), Android (2023+ launch)
- **Pricing:** Free core; premium "Co-Star Plus" $5.99/mo for advanced chart readings and extras
- **Distinguishing UX:** brutalist monochrome design, AI-generated text with specific sardonic voice

## How they make money (precise)
Primary: Co-Star Plus subscriptions at ~$70/yr × ~100-200K subs = ~$7-15M. Secondary: experimental commerce (in-app birthday reading, gift chart readings, merch). Tertiary: occasional brand partnerships for horoscope content (e.g., limited-time campaigns with Nike, Vogue). Unit economics challenged — free tier is product-complete, so conversion depends on engagement loops.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **AI-voiced ≠ personal:** Co-Star's "voice" is a creative brand, not your private journal. MyLife stars pairs chart with user's own astrology journal.
- **Push-notification addiction:** Co-Star spams to drive DAU; users cite it as both charming and manipulative. MyLife is calm and user-initiated.
- **Anxiety vectoring:** Co-Star notifications have prompted viral anxiety ("the void wants..."). MyLife can offer neutral or optimistic reading toggles.
- **Social graph requirement:** Co-Star's compatibility features push friend invites, harvesting social graph. MyLife keeps saved profiles local.
- **No tarot, no solar returns depth:** Co-Star is chart + horoscope. MyLife ships tarot-of-the-day, tarot spreads, solar returns, progressions, transit calendar.
- **Privacy for a sensitive category:** birth time is extremely personal data. Co-Star servers hold millions of charts; MyLife computes on-device.
