# The Pattern — Deep Dive (vs MyStars)

**Module:** stars
**Tier:** direct (relationship-focused astrology)
**Founded:** 2017
**HQ:** Venice, CA
**Status:** private (founder-funded, low-profile)

## One-line
Astrology-adjacent personality and relationship analysis — famously mentioned by Channing Tatum on a date in 2019, surged to millions of downloads, positions itself as "eerily accurate" personal insight rather than horoscope.

## Product
- Natal chart interpretation framed as personality "patterns"
- Relationship compatibility deep-dives (the core hook)
- Truth Messaging — chat other users with shared relationship interpretations
- Daily guidance / connected cycles framing
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android
- **Pricing:** Free; premium "Go Deeper" subscription ~$9.99/mo or ~$60/yr
- **Distinguishing UX:** less astrology jargon, more pop-psychology framing; pastel gradient aesthetic

## How they make money (precise)
Primary: "Go Deeper" monthly / yearly subs at ~$60-100/yr. Secondary: premium chart bundles, relationship readings as one-time IAP. Tertiary: none disclosed. Very small revenue base — likely 10-30K paying subs = ~$600K-$3M ARR.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Celebrity-driven growth is volatile:** The Pattern's 2019 Channing Tatum spike is a cautionary tale. MyLife acquires via suite bundle, not celebrity.
- **Relationship-focus is narrow:** The Pattern struggles to retain solo users. MyLife stars includes tarot, solar returns, progressions — broader daily surface area.
- **Proprietary "pattern" language is gate-kept:** users invest in learning Pattern vocabulary; switching cost is low once users realize it's astrology with new names. MyLife uses standard astrology terms.
- **No journal, no tarot:** The Pattern is read-only interpretation. MyLife stars owns an astrology journal with attachments + tarot spreads.
- **Privacy:** birth time + relationship details are extremely sensitive. The Pattern's servers hold all this; MyLife is local-first.
