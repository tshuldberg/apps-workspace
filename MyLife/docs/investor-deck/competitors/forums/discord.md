# Discord — Deep Dive (vs MyForums)

**Module:** forums
**Tier:** direct (community platform)
**Founded:** 2015
**HQ:** San Francisco, USA
**Status:** private (late-stage, pre-IPO, filed confidentially 2025)

## One-line
Real-time voice/text community platform built for gaming that expanded into general-purpose communities, monetized through Nitro subscriptions and a growing ads/games marketplace pivot as IPO approaches.

## Product
- Servers with channels (text, voice, stage, forum channels added 2022)
- Voice chat, video chat, screen share, streaming
- Roles/permissions, webhooks, bot ecosystem
- Nitro tier: HD video, bigger uploads, custom emojis, animated avatars, server boosting
- Platforms: Desktop (Win/Mac/Linux), iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: free / Nitro Basic $2.99/mo / Nitro $9.99/mo / Nitro Yearly $99.99
- Distinguishing UX: real-time voice + gamer-native UI made it the default for youth communities

## Acquisition / Exit
Independent as of 2026-04. Most recent round: 2021 Series G at $15B. Confidential S-1 IPO filing reported in late 2025. Co-founders Jason Citron (CEO) and Stan Vishnevskiy still leading; Citron announced transition plan in 2025 with new CEO Humam Sakhnini (ex-Activision Blizzard King).

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Nitro subscriptions** $9.99/mo — cosmetics + uploads + HD + server boosts.
2. **Server Boosts** — users pay to upgrade specific servers (emojis, better audio quality).
3. **Advertising** — launched 2023 with in-app Quests (sponsored engagement tasks for game publishers); expanding.
4. **Game marketplace** — small revenue share on games sold via Discord.

Unit economics: low marginal server cost; heavy infra cost for real-time voice. Ads are the growth lever IPO narrative depends on.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Bot infestation:** Discord's bot ecosystem is both its strength and a bot/scam flood. MyForums does device-attested human verification — structural bot ban.
- **Ad monetization coming:** Discord is pivoting to ads to hit IPO numbers; MyForums is subscription-funded with no ad pressure.
- **No threaded text for long-form:** forum channels were bolted on 2022 and remain second-class. MyForums is threaded-first with Reddit-style voting.
- **Opaque moderation:** Discord mod actions are private. MyForums has a transparent public mod log in every community.
- **Gaming/youth brand:** Discord is off-brand for health, finance, cycle, mental health communities. MyForums lives inside a health/life-tracking suite — adjacency matters.
