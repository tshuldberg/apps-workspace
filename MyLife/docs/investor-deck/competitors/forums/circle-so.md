# Circle (circle.so) — Deep Dive (vs MyForums)

**Module:** forums
**Tier:** adjacent (creator-community SaaS, B2B-priced)
**Founded:** 2020
**HQ:** New York, USA (CircleCo Inc)
**Status:** private (VC-backed)

## One-line
The Slack/Facebook-Groups replacement for creator and course communities — white-label community SaaS that creators rent to host paid memberships, moving into the gap Geneva (acquired 2024 by Bumble) and Mighty Networks occupy.

## Product
- Private branded communities with spaces (discussions, live, events, course, members)
- Paid memberships (Stripe integration), gated content, events with RSVP
- Course hosting ("Circle Courses"), livestreaming (native + integrations)
- White-label mobile app for larger plans ("Branded App")
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: Professional $89/mo, Business $199/mo, Enterprise $399+/mo; all tiered on seats + features
- Distinguishing UX: best-in-class onboarding + creator brand customization

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Seat-tiered SaaS subscriptions** $89-399+/mo — plan gating drives upgrade path.
2. **Branded App add-on** (Enterprise) — meaningful ARPU lift.
3. **Payment processing fees** — Circle takes a small cut on Stripe-routed memberships sold through Circle.
4. **Courses add-on** — bundles with Business/Enterprise.

Unit economics: Typical SaaS — net retention >100% driven by community growth; CAC paid back ~10-14 months per founder commentary.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **B2B-priced, creator-only:** Circle is unaffordable for small communities ($89/mo minimum); MyForums is free to every MyLife user, no seat pricing.
- **Siloed per-creator:** each Circle community is its own login. MyForums is federated under one user identity across the suite.
- **No bot verification layer:** Circle relies on Stripe gate for bot-free, but non-paid communities are vulnerable. MyForums has device-attested human verification native.
- **Topic orientation differs:** Circle is optimized for creator monetization. MyForums is optimized for health/life-tracking topical adjacency to modules.
- **No cross-module previews:** Circle cannot render a MyBooks book card or MyRecipes recipe card inline. MyForums can.
