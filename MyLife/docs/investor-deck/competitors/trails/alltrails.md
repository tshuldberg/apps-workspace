# AllTrails — Deep Dive (vs MyTrails)

**Module:** trails
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2010
**HQ:** San Francisco, California, USA
**Status:** private (majority Permira, 2024)

## One-line
The world's largest community-driven trail database (450K+ curated trails, 60M+ users), monetized via a $35.99/yr Plus subscription and increasingly AI-assisted trail recommendations.

## Product
- 450K+ curated trails worldwide with photos, reviews, and stats
- GPS recording, route-matching, wrong-turn alerts (Plus)
- Offline maps (Plus) and 3D preview (Plus)
- Community reviews and trail photos
- Lifeline (share live location with contact) (Plus)
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web, Apple Watch, Wear OS
- Pricing: Free / Plus $35.99/yr (some markets $29.99/yr) / Peak (AI Explorer) bundled with Plus from 2024
- Distinguishing UX choice: community-sourced "Suggest Edits" keep trail data fresh at scale

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Plus subscription** ($35.99/yr): offline maps, wrong-turn alerts, Lifeline, 3D previews, Peak AI.
2. **Affiliate commerce**: REI gear, National Geographic maps embedded in trail pages.
3. **Land-manager partnerships**: some national/state parks pay for AllTrails-hosted data and trailhead signage.

Unit economics: pure SaaS margins on subs (>70% gross). Main cost: map tile licensing (Mapbox/OSM) + moderation staff for UGC.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Cloud lock-in**: AllTrails requires account creation; all trail recordings upload. MyTrails is local-first, zero-cloud for core use.
- **Bundle economics**: $35.99/yr for one category is a trade MyLife disrupts with $99/yr for 30 modules.
- **Data-exhaust model**: AllTrails sells aggregated hike data to land managers. MyTrails keeps GPS recordings encrypted on device.
- **Stale Lifeline UX**: Lifeline is limited to a single emergency contact. MyLife's Presence + hub crews extend safety to opt-in networks across the suite.
- **No trip planning layer**: AllTrails is trail-centric, not trip-centric. MyTrails ships Trips + Packing (V6/V7 schemas) that link directly to MyTravel.
