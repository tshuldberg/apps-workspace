# Gaia GPS — Deep Dive (vs MyTrails)

**Module:** trails
**Tier:** direct (backcountry navigation)
**Founded:** 2008
**HQ:** Boulder, Colorado, USA
**Status:** acquired (Outside Inc., 2021)

## One-line
The backcountry navigation specialist — topo maps, layered overlays (slope, satellite, NatGeo), and offline-first routing — absorbed into Outside Inc.'s $29.99/yr Outside+ media bundle.

## Product
- 250+ map layers (USGS topo, NatGeo Trails Illustrated, slope-angle shading, wildfire)
- Offline map downloads by area (unlimited on Premium)
- GPS recording with waypoints and tracks
- Public trails database + crowdsourced routes
- Route-planning with elevation profile
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web, Garmin inReach integration
- Pricing: Free (limited) / Premium ~$39.99/yr standalone; bundled into Outside+ $99.99/yr
- Distinguishing UX choice: layered map stack — let power users pick the right map for the terrain (avalanche, fire, NatGeo)

## Acquisition / Exit
**Acquired by:** Outside Inc. (formerly Pocket Outdoor Media, controlled by Robin Thurston).
**Date:** March 2021.
**Price:** Not disclosed (~$20-40M est. based on revenue multiples).
**Current status inside parent:** core backcountry asset inside Outside+ bundle; standalone product still maintained but bundle-first go-to-market.
**Strategic rationale:** Outside Inc. assembled a vertically integrated outdoor-media empire (magazines + Peloton Trailforks + Gaia + CyclingTips + Warren Miller); Gaia supplies the navigation utility layer.
**Founder outcome:** Andrew Johnson and Anna Nesterova departed per LinkedIn; no earn-out publicly disclosed.

## Users
- MAU: 500K-1M est.
- Paying subs: est. 80-120K standalone + larger share via Outside+
- Geography: US 70%+ (backcountry use heavy in western states), Canada
- Conversion rate: ~10-15% est. (power-user category converts higher)

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Gaia Premium standalone** (~$39.99/yr): layers, offline maps, print, route planning.
2. **Outside+ bundle** ($99.99/yr): Gaia Premium + Outside magazine + Peloton Trailforks + CyclingTips + Warren Miller + more.
3. **NatGeo / USGS layer licensing** built into tier pricing.

Unit economics: map licensing is real COGS (NatGeo, Mapbox). Standalone sub margin est. 55-65%; bundled subs dilute per-product revenue.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Bundle math**: Outside+ at $99.99/yr gives you trail maps + magazines. MyLife $99/yr gives you 30 modules across your whole life.
- **Backcountry-only framing**: Gaia is niche and intimidating for casual hikers. MyTrails scales from casual walk to serious backcountry with same UX.
- **Parent distress**: Outside Inc. has had layoffs and restructuring through 2023-2024; product roadmap uncertain. MyTrails sits inside a 30-module suite with diversified revenue.
- **No trip/packing layer**: Gaia is pure navigation. MyTrails adds packing templates (V6), trip itineraries (V7), reviews (V10) — the full hike lifecycle.
