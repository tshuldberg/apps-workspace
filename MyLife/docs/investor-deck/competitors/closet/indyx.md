# Indyx — Deep Dive (vs MyCloset)

**Module:** closet
**Tier:** direct (styling-first)
**Founded:** 2021
**HQ:** USA
**Status:** private (seed-funded startup)

## One-line
A hybrid consumer + professional-stylist wardrobe app; cost-per-wear charts, 1:1 styling marketplace ($60+), and resell integrations.

## Product
- Digital wardrobe with AI background removal + auto-tagging
- Outfit planner with drag-and-drop boards
- Calendar and packing lists
- Cost-per-wear with interactive charts
- Open Closets (public browsable wardrobes of influencers)
- 1:1 styling services ($60+)
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: free core + Indyx Insider (premium features, price N/D) + paid styling services
- Distinguishing UX choice: professional styling marketplace layered onto closet app

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Indyx Insider** subscription (price tiered): unlocks advanced analytics, premium features.
2. **Styling marketplace fees**: take-rate on 1:1 styling sessions ($60+ per session).
3. **Affiliate** on featured retailer purchases.

Unit economics: marketplace gross margin high (~70%+ on take rate) but constrained by low stylist and client liquidity; Insider sub drives recurring but attach rate is modest.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Stylist marketplace is the value prop**: Indyx monetizes human styling. MyCloset monetizes nothing — it ships AI outfit suggestions that learn from feedback, free inside the suite.
- **Public Open Closets**: many wardrobes are meant to be shared. MyCloset is private by default.
- **Weaker analytics**: no capsule versatility, no color palette analysis, no weather scoring. MyCloset ships all three.
- **Cloud-only with account requirement**: MyCloset is local-first.
