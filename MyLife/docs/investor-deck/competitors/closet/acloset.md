# Acloset — Deep Dive (vs MyCloset)

**Module:** closet
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2020
**HQ:** Seoul, South Korea (Looko Inc.)
**Status:** private (venture-backed, Korean ecosystem)

## One-line
Korean AI-first wardrobe app; strongest in AI outfit suggestions and Asia-Pacific growth; Google Play Editor's Pick.

## Product
- Photo-to-wardrobe with automatic background removal + category/color tagging
- AI outfit suggestions based on occasion, weather, style
- Calendar for worn outfits
- Packing list planner
- Style challenges and community
- Platforms: iOS, Android (no web)
- Pricing: free / Premium ($3.99/mo or $23.99/yr, regional pricing varies)
- Distinguishing UX choice: AI-first; the outfit recommendations engine is the product's headline feature

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Premium subscription** ($3.99/mo or $23.99/yr): unlocks unlimited AI suggestions, advanced calendar, more styles.
2. **Brand partnerships** (Korean fashion labels featured).
3. **Affiliate links** to shopping destinations.

Unit economics: ARPU ~$25-40 blended; CAC via Instagram + TikTok in US ~$12-20.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **AI models run on their servers**: every photo is uploaded and analyzed remotely. MyCloset runs suggestions locally.
- **Korean-first UX**: translation quality inconsistent for western markets.
- **No cost-per-wear engine**: Acloset emphasizes styling, not analytics. MyCloset ships CPW with leaderboards and category medians.
- **Shopping affiliation**: the AI can nudge toward purchases. MyCloset has no affiliate revenue line.
