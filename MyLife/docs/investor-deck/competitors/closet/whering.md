# Whering — Deep Dive (vs MyCloset)

**Module:** closet
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2020
**HQ:** London, UK
**Status:** private (VC-backed)

## One-line
A social digital wardrobe app with a 9M+ user claim, strong press coverage, and a UK-incubated sustainability narrative.

## Product
- Photo-to-wardrobe with background removal
- Outfit planner with drag-and-drop
- Lookbooks, packing lists, calendar
- Cost-per-wear analytics
- Share wardrobes with friends ("social wardrobe")
- 100M+ item catalog you can pull from
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: free / VIP subscription (price tiered; ~$4.99/mo)
- Distinguishing UX choice: social sharing + sustainability framing ("wear more of what you own")

## How they make money (precise)
1. **VIP subscription** (~$4.99/mo): unlocks advanced insights, outfit suggestions, no ads.
2. **Affiliate / brand partnerships** (sustainable-fashion brands featured in-app).
3. **Community / social layer** drives organic growth (free tier).

Unit economics: blended ARPU very low given free-dominant base; CAC subsidized by PR-led growth in the UK.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Social layer dependency**: Whering's model requires you to share your wardrobe. MyCloset is private by default — perfect for users who want analytics without publishing.
- **Affiliate monetization**: suggests items for purchase; a conflict of interest. MyCloset has zero affiliate revenue.
- **Cloud-only**: photos and metadata live on servers. MyCloset stores locally.
- **No capsule + versatility math**: MyCloset computes capsule versatility scores, an analytical feature Whering doesn't ship.
