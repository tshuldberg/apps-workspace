# Merriam-Webster — Deep Dive (vs MyWords)

**Module:** words
**Tier:** direct (dictionary + thesaurus)
**Founded:** 1831 (print) / 1996 (digital)
**HQ:** Springfield, Massachusetts, USA
**Status:** private (Encyclopaedia Britannica subsidiary)

## One-line
The iconic American English dictionary — authoritative, ad-supported free tier, with a Premium subscription for unlimited lookups and no ads, owned by Encyclopaedia Britannica since 1964.

## Product
- Dictionary + thesaurus lookups
- Word of the Day, Time Traveler (first-known-use year), Word History
- Quizzes, word games (Blossom Word, Missing Letter)
- Spanish/English and medical dictionaries
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web (primary), Alexa skill
- Pricing: Free (ad-supported) / Premium Dictionary $3.99/mo or $34.95/yr / Premium Pro $5.99/mo (plus unabridged)
- Distinguishing UX choice: editorial prestige + Word of the Day email (millions of subscribers)

## Acquisition / Exit
**Acquired by:** Encyclopaedia Britannica Inc. (since 1964).

**Current status inside parent:** anchor digital property of Britannica Inc.; IPO filing September 2024 targeted a ~$1B enterprise value, paused pending market conditions.

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Display advertising** on free dictionary lookups and Word of the Day pages.
2. **Premium Dictionary** ($34.95/yr): ad-free + unabridged access.
3. **API + licensing**: OEM device dictionaries (Kindle, iOS preinstalls historically), publisher API deals.
4. **Print publishing**: physical dictionaries still generate steady revenue.

Unit economics: ad RPM on dictionary pages is low (~$2-5 per 1000 pageviews); Premium is higher margin but low conversion; licensing is lumpy but high margin.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **English only**: Merriam-Webster is American English authoritative. MyWords covers 270+ languages via Free Dictionary API + Wiktionary + Datamuse.
- **Ad-heavy free UX**: M-W's free web is aggressively monetized with display ads. MyWords has zero ads, flat suite pricing.
- **No cross-module**: M-W doesn't integrate with flashcards or notes. MyWords has native Flash card cross-link (flash_card_id column in V2 migration).
- **No FTS on saved lookups**: M-W's "favorites" is a flat list. MyWords ships FTS5 full-text search on saved words (V4 migration + triggers).
- **Print-era brand inertia**: M-W earns on brand + SEO, not UX innovation. MyWords can rev its product faster as part of a 30-module suite.
