# Dictionary.com — Deep Dive (vs MyWords)

**Module:** words
**Tier:** direct (dictionary + thesaurus)
**Founded:** 1995
**HQ:** Oakland, California, USA
**Status:** private (IXL Learning subsidiary)

## One-line
The most-trafficked consumer dictionary on the open web — thesaurus, slang.com, and Spanish English built in, monetized primarily through display advertising and an ad-free Premium tier.

## Product
- Dictionary + thesaurus (Thesaurus.com integrated)
- Spanish/English dictionary
- Slang dictionary (Dictionary.com acquired Slang.com content)
- Word of the Day, quizzes, crossword solver
- Pronunciation audio (select languages)
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web (primary), browser extensions
- Pricing: Free (ad-supported) / Ad-Free Premium ~$1.99/mo or $19.99/yr
- Distinguishing UX choice: integrates thesaurus + dictionary + slang + games into single consumer surface

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Display advertising** (largest revenue line): Google AdSense + programmatic, huge SEO traffic volume.
2. **Ad-Free Premium** ($19.99/yr): removes ads from web and app.
3. **API licensing** for developers (partnership with publishers).
4. **Cross-sell** within IXL ecosystem (Vocabulary.com, Rosetta Stone, IXL for schools).

Unit economics: SEO-driven page view model is highest-volume in category; ad RPM low but volume compensates. Premium subs are small but high-margin.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Ad clutter**: Dictionary.com free UX is dominated by display ads, affiliate widgets, and pop-overs. MyWords has zero ads.
- **English-centric**: Dictionary.com's core is American English + Spanish. MyWords handles 270+ languages.
- **No saved words FTS**: a user can favorite a word but there is no full-text search across saved lookups. MyWords ships FTS5 on saved words (V4 migration).
- **No cross-module**: Dictionary.com doesn't integrate with flashcards, notes, or journal. MyWords → MyFlash → MyJournal wiring is native.
- **Ownership complexity**: Dictionary.com has changed owners 3 times in 15 years. IXL Learning focus is K-12, not consumer. MyLife owns the whole stack consistently.
