# Otter.ai — Deep Dive (vs MyVoice)

**Module:** voice
**Tier:** direct (AI transcription) + adjacent (meeting intelligence)
**Founded:** 2016
**HQ:** Mountain View, California, USA
**Status:** private ($100M ARR, profitable)

## One-line
The B2B meeting-intelligence leader — joins your Zoom/Meet/Teams, transcribes, summarizes, and extracts action items — now reaching $100M ARR and ~200 employees via aggressive OtterPilot bot deployment.

## Product
- Live meeting transcription (Zoom/Meet/Teams bot: OtterPilot)
- Speaker diarization and multi-speaker meetings
- Automatic summaries + action items (OtterAI Chat)
- Calendar integration to auto-join meetings
- Shared workspaces (Business tier)
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web (primary), Chrome extension, Zoom App
- Pricing: Free (300 min/mo) / Pro $16.99/mo or $8.33/mo annual / Business $30/user/mo
- Distinguishing UX choice: the bot joins your meetings for you — Otter is the product layer on top of Zoom

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Pro subscription** ($8.33/mo annual): unlimited meetings, bot, summaries.
2. **Business** ($30/user/mo): team workspaces, admin, shared channels.
3. **Enterprise**: SSO, SOC 2, custom pricing.
4. Minor API revenue.

Unit economics: GPU-heavy STT (Whisper-era) drives COGS up, but enterprise ACV lifts gross margin to ~70%. Net retention >120% (land-and-expand play).

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Cloud upload of audio**: Otter sends your meeting audio to servers. MyVoice transcribes on-device — critical for therapy sessions, medical dictation, confidential calls.
- **B2B meeting focus**: Otter is not built for personal voice notes or single-user dictation; UX is meeting-centric. MyVoice is personal dictation first.
- **Enterprise pricing pressure**: Otter's $30/user/mo doesn't serve consumers well; Pro at $100/yr for one category vs MyLife $99/yr for 30 modules (and voice is a free-tier module).
- **No ecosystem**: Otter is single-purpose. MyVoice → MyJournal → MyMeds voice log → MyNotes cross-wire is impossible with Otter.
