# Rev — Deep Dive (vs MyVoice)

**Module:** voice
**Tier:** direct (transcription, recording) + adjacent (human-graded transcription)
**Founded:** 2010
**HQ:** Austin, Texas, USA
**Status:** private

## One-line
The original human + AI transcription marketplace for journalists, podcasters, and legal pros — now pivoting from transcription services to an AI "VoiceHub" content-generation platform with unit-pricing at $0.25/min AI or $1.99/min human.

## Product
- AI transcription ($0.25/min) and human transcription ($1.99/min)
- Rev voice recorder app (free) with cloud upload to services
- Captions, subtitles, translated captions
- VoiceHub (new 2024-2025): AI summaries, clip-gen, podcast chaptering
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: per-minute transactional; no required subscription for recording app
- Distinguishing UX choice: choice of AI speed or human accuracy at the same UI — trade cost for quality per job

## How they make money (precise)
1. **AI transcription** ($0.25/min): margin ~60-70%.
2. **Human transcription** ($1.99/min): margin ~40-50% after Revver pay.
3. **Captions + translation**: premium per-minute services.
4. **VoiceHub** (2024+): AI content generation, podcast tooling.

Unit economics: mixed — AI is high margin, human is lower margin but higher ACV/perceived accuracy. 99%+ accuracy claim for human tier.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Transactional cost**: Rev bills per minute; a heavy user pays thousands/yr. MyVoice is a flat-fee free-tier hub module — no per-minute bills, no audio upload.
- **Cloud-required**: Rev requires audio upload to servers (critical for journalists, but fatal for private notes, therapy, medical dictation). MyVoice transcribes on-device.
- **Pivot risk**: Rev is mid-pivot from transcription-services to VoiceHub AI content platform; product identity is in flux. MyVoice has stable scope.
- **No personal use case**: Rev is built for pro content creators, not daily voice notes. MyVoice is personal dictation first.
