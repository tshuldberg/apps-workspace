# Just Press Record — Deep Dive (vs MyVoice)

**Module:** voice
**Tier:** direct (on-device voice memo + transcription)
**Founded:** 2015
**HQ:** Edinburgh, Scotland, UK
**Status:** private (indie, Open Planet Software)

## One-line
The Apple Watch-first voice recorder with on-device transcription in 30+ languages — an Apple Design Award-adjacent indie darling that proved you don't need the cloud to transcribe speech.

## Product
- One-tap recording from iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac
- On-device transcription (30+ languages, Apple Speech framework)
- iCloud sync across devices (end-to-end encrypted via Apple)
- Share as audio file or transcript
- Platforms: iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, macOS, visionOS (Apple-only)
- Pricing: $4.99 (iOS) / $9.99 (Mac) / $29.99 Pro subscription for premium AI features (launched 2024)
- Distinguishing UX choice: the watch button is the product — zero friction from impulse to recording

## How they make money (precise)
1. **One-time app purchase** ($4.99 iOS, $9.99 Mac): primary historical revenue.
2. **JPR Pro subscription** (~$29.99/yr, launched 2024): AI summaries, formatting, export.
3. No ads, no data sold.

Unit economics: near-zero marginal cost; pure margin on App Store sales minus Apple's cut (15-30%).

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Apple-only**: JPR doesn't exist on Android or web. MyVoice is iOS + Android + web with equivalent on-device STT.
- **No history/library depth**: JPR is a recorder with a transcript panel; no tagging, no FTS, no cross-module links. MyVoice ships favorites, tags, and schema-ready speaker diarization + language profiles.
- **One-category product**: even a Pro upgrade is $30/yr for voice-memo transcription. MyLife suite at $99/yr bundles voice + 29 modules.
- **No cross-wiring**: JPR voice notes do not flow into notes/journal/meds apps. MyVoice is architected for cross-module wiring (MyJournal audio entries, MyMeds dosing dictation).
