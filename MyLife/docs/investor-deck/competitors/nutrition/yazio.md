# Yazio — Deep Dive (vs MyNutrition)

**Module:** nutrition
**Tier:** direct (European leader)
**Founded:** 2013
**HQ:** Erfurt, Germany
**Status:** acquired (Groupe SEB, late 2023)

## One-line
Europe's #1 calorie counter and weight-loss app; beat MFP in DACH via localization, meal plans, and fasting integration, now owned by a French kitchen-appliance conglomerate.

## Product
- Food logging with barcode scanner, custom food, recipe creator
- 20+ fasting plans (16:8, 5:2, Warrior, etc.) integrated with diet tracking
- Meal plans (low-carb, Mediterranean, vegan, keto) with shopping list export
- Buddy system, streak tracking, body-stats trends (weight, waist, body-fat)
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web (companion)
- Pricing: Free (limited) / PRO $7.99/mo, $47.99/yr, or ~$99.99 lifetime (varies by market)
- Distinguishing UX choice: the meal-plan-first experience — most MFP users track what they ate; Yazio users follow a plan Yazio gave them

## Acquisition / Exit
- Acquired by: Groupe SEB (Euronext Paris: SK; owner of Tefal, Moulinex, Krups, Rowenta, WMF)
- Date: 2023 (late Q3/Q4 2023)
- Price: undisclosed
- Current status inside parent: standalone brand inside SEB's Digital / Consumer app portfolio
- Strategic rationale: SEB wants a digital/consumer subscription platform to anchor its connected-kitchen hardware (smart cookers, blenders); Yazio's recipe + meal-plan DB cross-sells into appliance upsell funnel; first major app acquisition for SEB
- Founder outcome: Florian Weissenstein stayed on as CEO post-acquisition per public reporting

## How they make money (precise)
1. **PRO subscription** ($7.99/mo or $47.99/yr): meal plans, fasting timer, advanced stats, macro goals, recipe library.
2. **Lifetime IAP** (~$99.99): large share of first-month new users; front-loads revenue.
3. Zero ads, zero affiliate, no data licensing.

Unit economics: gross margin ~85%+; SensorTower US iOS shows ~$150-200K/mo which extrapolates ~1/3 of revenue to US.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Parent company is a kitchen-appliance maker:** strategic intent is likely to push Yazio users to Tefal/Krups hardware. MyLife has no hardware dependency.
- **Meal plans are prescriptive, not adaptive:** Yazio users pick from a static library. MyNutrition correlates live diet with MyWorkouts + MyMood to generate context-aware insights.
- **No community integration:** Yazio added buddies in 2023 but there's no feed, challenges, or leaderboards. MyNutrition ships full community (profiles + challenges + cheers + leaderboards).
- **Europe-first data gaps:** Yazio database is strong in European foods, weaker in US brand catalog. MyNutrition unifies USDA + OFF + FatSecret globally.
- **$48-100/yr for one app vs $99/yr for 30 modules.**
