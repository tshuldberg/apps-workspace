# Cronometer — Deep Dive (vs MyNutrition)

**Module:** nutrition
**Tier:** direct (micronutrient accuracy niche, bootstrapped)
**Founded:** 2011
**HQ:** Revelstoke, BC, Canada
**Status:** private (bootstrapped, no VC)

## One-line
The gold standard for micronutrient accuracy — curated food database, no crowdsourced garbage entries, serious quantified-self and clinical following.

## Product
- Curated food database (no crowdsourcing; every entry verified by Cronometer staff)
- 80+ micronutrients tracked (not just macros)
- Biometrics, blood glucose (Stelo/Dexcom integrations), sleep, weight, mood
- Meal planning, recipe importer, custom foods, label scanner
- Cronometer Pro for dietitians ($29.99/mo): client dashboards
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: Free (core tracking, unlimited history) / Gold $8.99/mo or $49.99/yr / Gold+ with AI photo-logging
- Distinguishing UX choice: shows nutrient gaps as a heat map — vitamin D shortfall stares back at you

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Gold ($8.99/mo or $49.99/yr)**: custom biometrics, target nutrients, recipe importer, oracle (nutrient match suggestions), charts.
2. **Cronometer Pro** ($29.99/mo per dietitian): client roster, remote diary access, food-plan delivery — a real B2B line.
3. **CSV exports + custom foods**: no ads, no affiliate, no data licensing.

Unit economics: gross margin ~90% (nutrition data licensing is one-time); team size <50; profitable every year.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Micronutrient UX is clinical, not consumer-friendly:** Cronometer looks like a medical tool. MyNutrition inherits the same 80+ micronutrient rigor but wraps it in the Cool Obsidian design system and AI photo logging.
- **No adaptive TDEE:** Cronometer uses fixed activity multipliers. MyNutrition's energy-balance engine pulls HealthKit active calories for real-time TDEE.
- **No cross-module context:** Cronometer is food + biometrics only. MyNutrition correlates diet with MyFast, MyWorkouts, MyMood.
- **Lean team / slow UI evolution:** 2011-era UX; MyNutrition ships modern mobile-first UI.
- **Suite bundle:** Gold is $50/yr; MyLife is $99/yr for 30 modules including matched micronutrient accuracy.
