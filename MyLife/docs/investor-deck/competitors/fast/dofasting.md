# DoFasting — Deep Dive (vs MyFast)

**Module:** fast
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2019
**HQ:** Vilnius, Lithuania (Fasting Solutions UAB, part of Kilo Health portfolio)
**Status:** private (Kilo Health subsidiary)

## One-line
Lithuanian aggressive-acquisition fasting tracker with meal plans and a heavy quiz-funnel onboarding flow, part of Kilo Health's portfolio of 30+ health-adjacent subscription apps.

## Product
- Fasting timer with 6 preset plans
- Workouts library, meal plans with grocery lists, calorie counter
- Weight + water + body measurements tracking
- Habit tracker, progress journal
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web (paywall-first)
- Pricing: no free tier of substance — web quiz funnels to $39.99-$99.99 per 3/6-month plans; auto-renew
- Distinguishing UX: quiz funnel ("answer 20 questions, get your plan") driving conversion before any app experience

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Quiz-funnel paywall**: user completes 20-Q onboarding, gets an "individualized plan" gated by a $39.99-99.99 charge before app install is usable.
2. **Subscription auto-renewal** on 3/6-month cycles — aggressive retention tactics (friction to cancel documented in BBB/consumer complaints).
3. **Upsells**: workout plans, recipes, eBooks inside-app.

Unit economics: LTV / CAC management at scale via paid social. Heavy legal exposure on refund/auto-renew practices in US and EU (dark-pattern class actions against similar Kilo Health sibling products).

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **No free tier of substance:** DoFasting is paywall-first. MyFast is fully free with no feature gating.
- **No physiological depth:** DoFasting tracks the timer; MyFast models 6 zones in real time with caffeine half-life and hydration coefficients.
- **Auto-renew / consumer-trust issues:** negative App Store reviews flag cancellation friction. MyLife publishes cancellation policy and has no aggressive retention flow.
- **Weight-loss positioning:** DoFasting targets weight loss with diet-culture marketing. MyFast is protocol-neutral (autophagy, circadian, religious, etc).
- **Cross-module data hygiene:** MyFast shares data only with user-enabled modules locally; DoFasting profile is a cloud asset sold to affiliated brands.
