# Zero (Big Sky Health) — Deep Dive (vs MyFast)

**Module:** fast
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2016 (Zero app); Big Sky Health parent founded 2019
**HQ:** Santa Monica, USA
**Status:** private (Big Sky Health Inc., VC-backed)

## One-line
The category-defining fasting tracker with Jack Dorsey endorsement, a paid "Zero Plus" subscription, and a portfolio parent (Big Sky Health) that also runs Less (alcohol) and Oak (meditation).

## Product
- Fasting timer with preset protocols (13/11, 16/8, 18/6, 20/4, OMAD, circadian, 5:2, custom)
- Journal, mood, weight, glucose (HealthKit) tracking
- "Plus" tier: advanced stats, custom stages, expert content from Peter Attia + Mark Mattson
- Apple Watch app, shareable stats
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Apple Watch, Web (limited)
- Pricing: free / Zero Plus $69.99/yr (or $9.99/mo)
- Distinguishing UX: clean minimalist timer + celebrity/expert positioning

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Zero Plus subscription** $69.99/yr — gates stats history, custom stages, content library.
2. Previously tested a one-time purchase ($39.99 lifetime) before retreating to subscription.

Unit economics: low marginal cost per user; revenue gated by conversion rate, which has stagnated. Portfolio strategy (Less + Oak) exists to cross-sell health subs under one umbrella.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **No caffeine half-life modeling:** Zero tracks weight and mood, not caffeine decay. MyFast ships a caffeine engine inside the same module.
- **No hydration granularity:** Zero has basic water logging. MyFast has 12 beverage types with hydration coefficients.
- **No fast quality score:** Zero shows completion %, not a composite 0-100 quality score (adherence + hydration + protocol fit).
- **Walled garden for stats:** Zero gates 6-month history behind Plus. MyFast is free and unrestricted.
- **Suite anchor advantage:** MyFast is free as the top-of-funnel hook. Zero's entire business model is defending the $69.99/yr paywall.
