## One-line
A clean, design-forward intermittent fasting tracker from an indie Italian dev shop, popular in the Apple Design Award-adjacent aesthetic tier.

## Product
- Circular timer with 8 preset protocols
- Weight/mood/water logs, journal
- HealthKit integration, Apple Watch, widgets
- Social share cards, streaks
- Platforms: iOS, Apple Watch (no Android, no Web)
- Pricing: free / Pro $4.99/mo, $24.99/yr, or $49.99 lifetime
- Distinguishing UX: best-in-class iOS aesthetic (Human Interface Guidelines compliant, rich haptics)

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Pro subscription** $4.99/mo or $24.99/yr — gates advanced stats, custom protocols, themes.
2. **Lifetime purchase** $49.99 — one-time.
3. No ads, no data sales, no B2B.

Unit economics: indie-friendly. Small team, Apple-only stack, low infra cost. Revenue scales with aesthetic-forward iOS users who will pay for design quality.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **iOS-only:** Window has no Android, no Web, no cross-device sync beyond iCloud. MyFast spans iOS/Android/Web with a unified SQLite-backed store.
- **No protocol progression engine:** Window lets you pick a protocol; it does not suggest the next one based on sustained adherence. MyFast does.
- **No cross-module data:** Window is single-purpose. MyFast connects to MyWorkouts, MyMeds, MyNutrition, MySleep within the same suite.
- **No caffeine engine:** Window does not track caffeine half-life.
- **Price gap closed by bundle:** a user paying $24.99/yr for Window alone could get MyFast (free) plus 29 other modules in the MyLife suite.
