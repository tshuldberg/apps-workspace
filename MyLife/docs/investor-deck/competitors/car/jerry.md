# Jerry — Deep Dive (vs MyCar)

**Module:** car
**Tier:** adjacent (insurance-led, now garage-tracking)
**Founded:** 2017
**HQ:** Palo Alto, CA, USA
**Status:** private (VC-backed)

## One-line
An AI car-ownership "super-app" that started as insurance comparison, now adds maintenance reminders and vehicle profiles.

## Product
- Insurance shopping (compares 50+ carriers)
- Car-loan refinance
- Garage / maintenance reminders (added 2022)
- Driving behavior scoring
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: free to users; Jerry takes broker commissions from insurance carriers
- Distinguishing UX choice: chat-style insurance quote flow ("Hi, I'm Jerry")

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Insurance broker commissions** from 50+ carrier partners (~10-15% of first-year premium).
2. **Auto loan refinance fees** via lender partners.
3. **Lead sales** (implicit) to participating carriers even when users don't bind.

Unit economics: commission avg ~$100-150 per bound policy, CAC via Instagram/TikTok ads ~$30-60; 20-40% gross margin after ad spend.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Jerry is a lead-gen machine**: every interaction ends in a quote request. MyCar never monetizes user data — maintenance, fuel, diagnostics stay local.
- **Garage feature is an add-on**: maintenance reminders in Jerry are a retention hook, not a full service history. MyCar ships OBD-II diagnostics, DTC lookup, tire measurements, GPS trip log, parking saver.
- **No document vault, no fuel economy, no trip log**: Jerry doesn't store insurance documents or track cost-per-mile. MyCar does.
- **Insurance comparison can remain a feature MyLife links out to**: we don't need to compete on insurance brokerage, we complement it.
