# Drivvo — Deep Dive (vs MyCar)

**Module:** car
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2013
**HQ:** Minas Gerais, Brazil
**Status:** private (bootstrapped, solo founder)

## One-line
The Brazilian indie vehicle log app with the largest user base in Latin America; fuel, maintenance, expenses, reminders, fleet plan.

## Product
- Vehicle log: fuel, maintenance, services, expenses
- Fuel economy (km/L, km/mi) reports
- Reminders by km or date
- Fleet plan (business)
- Supports car, motorcycle, truck, plane, boat, tractor, generator
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: free (ad-supported) / Pro $1.99/mo or $11.99/yr; Fleet custom
- Distinguishing UX choice: supports non-car vehicles (tractors, boats) not covered by US competitors

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Pro subscription** ($1.99/mo or $11.99/yr): ad-free, unlimited vehicles, CSV export, more reports.
2. **In-app ads** (free tier).
3. **Fleet B2B** (custom pricing per vehicle) for small businesses.

Unit economics: low ARPU (~$5-8 blended) but very low COGS; bootstrapped opex supports marginal growth.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Ads in the free tier**: MyCar has zero ads; a suite subscription replaces ad revenue with predictable recurring.
- **No OBD-II, no DTC database, no document vault**: Drivvo is a log, not a diagnostics suite. MyCar has OBD + DTC + live-data logs.
- **No trip log with GPS, no parking saver**: Drivvo lacks modern iPhone-native features.
- **Weak English-language support**: Drivvo is Portuguese-first. MyCar ships en-US-native UX.
