# Bring! — Deep Dive (vs MyMarket)

**Module:** market
**Tier:** direct (grocery/shopping-list, European leader)
**Founded:** 2015
**HQ:** Zurich, Switzerland
**Status:** acquired (Swiss Post, 2021)

## One-line
Europe's #1 shopping-list app, now owned by Swiss Post and monetized primarily through retail-media partnerships and sponsored brand tiles inside the catalog.

## Product
- Icon-first shopping catalog (1000+ illustrated items, 20+ languages)
- Real-time shared lists with instant sync; QR-code list joining
- Offers/prospectus (digital flyer) from partner grocers (Migros, Coop, Edeka, Lidl, Rewe)
- Inspirations (recipes from brand partners), meal planner
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web, Apple Watch, WearOS, Alexa/Google
- Pricing: fully free; Bring! Premium rolled out 2023 (CHF 2.49/mo, ad-removal + themes)
- Distinguishing UX choice: beautiful illustrated icons replace typed product names, so lists are shareable across language barriers

## Acquisition / Exit
- Acquired by: Swiss Post (state-owned, Switzerland)

- Price: undisclosed (terms not public; estimated EUR 30-50M based on comparable retail-media deals)

- Founder outcome: Marco Cerqui and Dominic Mehr stayed on post-acquisition; leadership handed over by 2024

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Retailer partnerships** (Migros, Coop, Edeka, Rewe, Lidl): paid inclusion in the digital flyer surface and sponsored product tiles.
2. **Brand sponsorship** (CPG giants — Nestle, Unilever): sponsored items in the illustrated catalog trigger "add to list" events for attribution.
3. **Bring! Premium** (CHF 2.49/mo): ad removal, dark themes, aesthetic customization.
4. **Recipe/inspiration placements** from brand partners.

Unit economics: sponsored-tile CPM est. >$10 because intent is near-purchase (grocery list right before store trip).

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Ad-supported UX:** every surface is a sponsored tile. MyMarket has zero ads and zero affiliate — privacy as product.
- **Retailer lock-in:** Bring!'s monetization depends on European grocery chains; no US penetration.
- **No peer-to-peer sell surface:** Bring! is a list-only. MyMarket ships full listings + Stripe Connect escrow + Signal-grade encrypted chat.
- **Data-exhaust model:** every item add feeds Swiss Post's retail-media business. MyMarket keeps lists local (SQLite cache) and suite-private.
