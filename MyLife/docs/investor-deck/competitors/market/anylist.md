# AnyList — Deep Dive (vs MyMarket)

**Module:** market
**Tier:** direct (grocery/shopping-list)
**Founded:** 2010
**HQ:** Sunnyvale / Claymont, DE, USA
**Status:** private (indie, Y Combinator alum)

## One-line
The iOS-power-user grocery and recipe list app; shared family lists, recipe clipping, and meal planning, all as a flat-rate subscription.

## Product
- Shared grocery lists with real-time sync across iOS/Android/Web
- Recipe web clipper, ingredient parsing, scale recipes, add to list in one tap
- Meal planning calendar with drag-to-plan UX
- Pantry inventory, favorite/recent items, aisle categorization (auto-learned)
- Siri + Apple Watch + CarPlay + HomePod + Alexa/Google integrations
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: free (basic) / AnyList Complete $9.99/yr or Family $11.99/yr
- Distinguishing UX choice: recipe-first meal planning that pushes to the grocery list, not a list app bolted onto recipes

## How they make money (precise)
1. **AnyList Complete** $9.99/yr per account, or $11.99/yr per family (up to 6).
2. No ads, no affiliate, no data licensing. Pure paid-feature unlock.

Unit economics: a 3-person indie team at ~$5M revenue is ~$1.5M per head gross, which explains the anti-growth posture. No CAC, organic growth via App Store + word of mouth.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Isolated silo:** AnyList has no connection to a pantry-scanner, nutrition tracker, recipe library, or receipt scanner. MyMarket lives inside a suite where grocery lists can be populated from MyNutrition meal plans, MyRecipes weekly menus, and MyBudget grocery envelope.
- **No C2C marketplace layer:** MyMarket ships Stripe Connect escrow, E2E-encrypted chat, and 5-tier progressive verification. AnyList is read-only shopping.
- **iOS-heavy polish:** Android + Web are functional but a step behind. MyMarket is native-parity across iOS, Android, and Web from day one.
- **Suite pricing:** $9.99/yr for one app vs $99/yr for 30 modules.
