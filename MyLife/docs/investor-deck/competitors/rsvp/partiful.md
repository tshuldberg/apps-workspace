# Partiful — Deep Dive (vs MyRSVP)

**Module:** rsvp
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2020
**HQ:** New York, NY
**Status:** private (VC-backed growth stage)

## One-line
Gen Z's party invitation app — mobile-first, free, chat-enabled RSVP with expense splitting and event discovery that earned Google's "Best App 2024" award.

## Product
- Create invites in <60 seconds with GIF/video hero images
- Group chat per event, polls, playlists, photo stream, recap
- Expense splitting, add-to-calendar, location with map
- Guest list with +1s, dietary notes, custom questions
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web
- **Pricing:** Free (core product), commerce layer emerging (paid boosts, event add-ons)
- **Distinguishing UX:** chat-first social layer — invites feel like group threads, not forms

## How they make money (precise)
As of 2025: minimal monetization. Primary experiments include sponsored event templates (brand partnerships like liquor/food), paid event boosts, commerce via affiliate gifting. Long-term bet is a commerce-layered events graph — Partiful knows what event you're hosting and can intermediate catering/invitations/gifts. Investor pitch is distribution first, monetization later (a16z consumer-social playbook).

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Mandatory account + social graph:** Partiful requires phone number, maps your contacts, builds social graph. MyLife keeps events local, no social graph harvested.
- **Gen Z party focus:** Partiful is party-coded; weddings, work events, book club feel off-brand. MyLife templates span 12 event types.
- **No suite integration:** Partiful is standalone. MyLife RSVP connects to Recipes (menu), Budget (split expenses), Calendar (sync), Friends (guest history).
- **Pre-revenue fragility:** Partiful monetization is unproven — a pivot to ads or data could alienate users; MyLife's subscription model aligns incentives.
