# Mealime — Deep Dive (vs MyRecipes)

**Module:** recipes
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2014
**HQ:** Vancouver, Canada
**Status:** private (indie, bootstrapped)

## One-line
Mealime generates personalized weekly meal plans with 30-minute recipes and auto-compiled grocery lists filtered by dietary preferences, focused on simplicity over organization.

## Product
- Auto-generated weekly meal plans (5-7 meals/week) based on dietary prefs
- Step-by-step cook mode with timers
- Smart grocery lists that dedupe and aisle-sort
- Integration with Instacart, Amazon Fresh, Walmart for one-tap grocery ordering
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web (companion)
- **Pricing:** Free tier (limited) / Pro $2.99/mo or $19.99/yr
- **Distinguishing UX:** zero-effort meal planning — no import, just pick preferences and get the week

## How they make money (precise)
Primary: Pro subscriptions at $19.99/yr × ~250K subs ≈ $5M ARR. Secondary: grocery-delivery affiliate cuts from Instacart (~2-5% rev share per order). Tertiary: sponsored ingredient placements (dormant). Unit economics: CAC is low due to App Store ranking + word-of-mouth; LTV ~$40-60 based on estimated 2-3yr subscriber lifetime.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Recipe library is curated, not user-owned:** Mealime users cannot import their grandmother's recipe. MyLife imports URL/photo/video/voice.
- **No pantry cross-check:** Mealime shopping lists do not subtract what you already have. MyRecipes does via pantry module.
- **No cross-module life integration:** Mealime is meal-plan-only. MyLife recipes plug into Garden (harvest → meal), RSVP (event menus), and Budget (food spend).
- **Privacy:** Mealime sends grocery preferences to affiliate partners for revenue; MyLife keeps intent local.
