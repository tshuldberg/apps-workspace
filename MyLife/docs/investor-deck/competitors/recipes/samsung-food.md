# Samsung Food (formerly Whisk) — Deep Dive (vs MyRecipes)

**Module:** recipes
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2014 (as Whisk), acquired by Samsung 2019
**HQ:** London, UK / Seoul, KR (Samsung subsidiary)
**Status:** subsidiary (Samsung Electronics)

## One-line
AI-powered recipe discovery and smart shopping tied to Samsung smart appliances; rebranded from Whisk to Samsung Food in Sept 2023 to anchor the SmartThings kitchen ecosystem.

## Product
- AI recipe recommendations tailored to dietary goals and pantry
- Smart shopping lists with grocery partner integrations (Walmart, Kroger, Tesco)
- Meal planning with calendar
- Recipe import from any URL
- Samsung Family Hub fridge + SmartThings oven integration (send recipe → appliance)
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web, Samsung Smart TVs, Family Hub fridges
- **Pricing:** Free (core), Samsung Food+ premium $4.99/mo or $35/yr (launched 2024)
- **Distinguishing UX:** AI personalization + direct-to-appliance cooking instructions

## How they make money (precise)
Direct product revenue is negligible. Real business model: (1) drive Samsung appliance attach rates via software-hardware lock-in — Family Hub fridges ($3-5K) and Bespoke ovens justify software investment; (2) Food+ subs at $35/yr layer on modest recurring revenue; (3) retailer referral fees from grocery partners. Economics are subsidized by appliance margin.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Samsung ecosystem lock-in is the point:** Samsung Food is a loss-leader for appliances; users on Apple/Pixel have nothing to gain. MyLife is device-agnostic.
- **Intent data harvesting:** Samsung Food requires Samsung Account, harvests shopping/diet intent for analytics. MyLife is local-first, zero intent sharing.
- **No cross-module life integration:** Samsung Food is kitchen-only. MyLife spans 30 modules.
- **AI without vendor capture:** MyLife can ship Claude Vision recipe import without forcing Samsung login or appliance tie-in.
