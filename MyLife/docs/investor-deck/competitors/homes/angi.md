# Angi — Deep Dive (vs MyHomes)

**Module:** homes
**Tier:** adjacent (home-services marketplace, legacy)
**Founded:** 1995 (Angie's List); 2017 merged with HomeAdvisor; 2021 rebranded as Angi; 2025 spun off from IAC
**HQ:** Denver, CO / New York, NY
**Status:** public (NASDAQ: ANGI)

## One-line
The largest home-services revenue player in the US by TTM, now a post-IAC independent company running a declining lead-gen marketplace facing Thumbtack + Amazon Home Services pressure.

## Product
- Angi + HomeAdvisor brands merged into single platform
- Pro matching, ProFinder, fixed-price projects, Angi Services
- 17M projects/year, 168k+ service pros
- Pricing: free for consumers (ad-supported + booking fees); pros pay per lead + subscriptions
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Pro pay-per-lead**: legacy HomeAdvisor model. Pros pay $15-$100 per consumer contact.
2. **Pro subscriptions**: annual memberships for directory placement.
3. **Angi Services (fixed-price)**: Angi books the job, takes a cut.
4. **Ads**: display + affiliate revenue.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Declining engagement, shrinking revenue.** Angi 2024 revenue dropped 16% YoY; consumer trust has eroded (FTC complaints, pro churn). MyHomes is private and not a marketplace.
- **Marketplace, not management.** Angi does nothing for appliance warranties, inventory, maintenance schedules, insurance tracking — MyHomes' 7 domain engines.
- **Ad-supported.** Angi monetizes via ads + lead sales; MyHomes has zero analytics, zero telemetry.
- **Public company quarterly pressure**: Angi must keep lead prices rising, which alienates pros and consumers. MyHomes has no marketplace dependency.
- **Bundle.** $99/yr MyLife suite vs Angi "free but expensive by data" model.
