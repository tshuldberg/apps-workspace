# Thumbtack — Deep Dive (vs MyHomes)

**Module:** homes
**Tier:** adjacent (home services marketplace pivoting to management)
**Founded:** 2008
**HQ:** San Francisco, CA
**Status:** private (late-stage, Series G)

## One-line
The #2 US home-services marketplace, now layering an AI "home management" product on top of its 300k pro network to challenge Angi and HomeZada.

## Product
- Match homeowners with 300k+ local pros across home, events, lessons, wellness
- Instant quotes, messaging, scheduling, reviews
- Thumbtack Home Management: AI-driven maintenance plan + contractor matching
- Pricing: free for consumers; pros pay per lead ($5-$100+ per contact)
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Pay-per-lead from pros**: primary. $5-$100 per consumer contact, bid-based per category. "Billions of dollars" flowing to 300k pros through the platform (2024).
2. **Pro subscriptions (Thumbtack Pro)**: premium lead packages.
3. **Thumbtack Home Management**: still being monetized; likely premium consumer subscription or affiliate revenue on service bookings.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Marketplace economics misalign with homeowner privacy.** Thumbtack monetizes every contractor lead; MyHomes keeps contractor directory local-only.
- **No property data model.** Thumbtack knows projects but not appliances/warranties/inventory. MyHomes ships room + inventory + appliance registries with 191 passing engine tests.
- **No maintenance scheduling.** Thumbtack is reactive (user requests quote). MyHomes' reminder engine seeds 20+ default schedules on onboarding.
- **No document vault or insurance.** Thumbtack is pure marketplace; no vault for deeds, warranties, insurance gap analysis.
- **Privacy.** Thumbtack sells leads to pros; homeowners become a sales funnel. MyHomes is offline-first.
