# Calm — Deep Dive (vs MySleep)

**Module:** sleep
**Tier:** adjacent (sleep content + meditation; overlaps sleep journaling)
**Founded:** 2012
**HQ:** San Francisco, CA
**Status:** private (late-stage, profitable path)

## One-line
The #1 sleep and meditation app, known for celebrity-narrated Sleep Stories and 140M+ downloads — the content side of the sleep market.

## Product
- Sleep Stories (bedtime narration from LeBron, Harry Styles, Matthew McConaughey)
- Guided meditations, breathwork, mindfulness programs
- Music, soundscapes (rain, forest), sleep-timer audio
- Daily Calm 10-minute sessions, mood check-in (minimal journaling)
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web, Apple Watch, Alexa, Google Assistant
- **Pricing:** Free tier (very limited); Calm Premium $14.99/mo or $69.99/yr; $399.99 lifetime
- **Distinguishing UX:** celebrity-voiced content as the hook, premium brand positioning

- Geography: US ~65%, UK ~10%, remainder global

## How they make money (precise)
Primary: Calm Premium subscriptions at $70/yr × ~4M subs ≈ $280M ARR. Secondary: Calm Health + Calm Business partnerships with employers, insurers, airlines (per-seat enterprise pricing). Tertiary: content licensing (Sleep Stories licensed to airlines, hotels). Unit economics: CAC via celebrity partnerships and TV ads is high ($50-80 estimated); LTV ~$120-150 given 2yr avg sub life.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Content-heavy, tracking-light:** Calm plays sleep audio but does not journal your actual sleep. MyLife tracks it.
- **No cross-module correlation:** Calm cannot link sleep to mood, caffeine, or habits. MyLife does this natively.
- **Premium price ceiling:** $70/yr is hard to justify for a single function. MyLife delivers sleep + 29 others at similar total price.
- **Content gets stale:** Calm's Sleep Stories are recorded once; users churn when catalog runs out. MyLife's value is user-owned data that compounds.
- **Celebrity voices ≠ personal insight:** Matthew McConaughey reading cannot tell you that your Tuesday bad sleep correlated with screens past 10pm. MyLife can.
