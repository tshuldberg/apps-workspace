# Sleep Cycle — Deep Dive (vs MySleep)

**Module:** sleep
**Tier:** direct (dedicated sleep tracking + journaling)
**Founded:** 2009
**HQ:** Gothenburg, Sweden
**Status:** public (Nasdaq Stockholm: SLEEP)

## One-line
The original smart-alarm sleep tracker — uses microphone and accelerometer to analyze sleep cycles, wakes users during light sleep phase, and now the only publicly-traded pure-play sleep app.

## Product
- Smart alarm that wakes user in light sleep phase (30-min window)
- Sleep analysis via microphone (breathing, snoring, movement sounds)
- Sleep journal / mood + factor tracking
- Snore recording, heart rate via camera (premium), sleep aid audio
- Online sleep database for comparative stats
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Apple Watch, Wear OS
- **Pricing:** Free tier (basic); Premium $39.99/yr
- **Distinguishing UX:** mic-based sensing — no wearable required, works with phone on nightstand

## Acquisition / Exit
Independent public company as of 2026-04. Listed on Nasdaq Stockholm First North (ticker SLEEP). Most recent capital event: IPO 2021. At ~$130M market cap and declining category-cap, acquisition by a larger health-tech player (Withings, Oura, or insurer) plausible if growth stalls.

- Conversion: ~3-5% free → paid

## How they make money (precise)
Primary: Premium annual subscriptions at $39.99/yr × ~700K subs = ~$28M. Secondary: data licensing of anonymized aggregate sleep patterns to academic / healthcare partners (small but growing segment). Tertiary: Family plans, minor ad revenue on free tier. Unit economics: ARPU ~$40, CAC ~$8-12 via App Store search, LTV ~$80-120 at 2-3yr subscriber life.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Single-purpose:** Sleep Cycle tracks sleep and nothing else. MyLife integrates sleep with mood, habits, health, nutrition natively.
- **Microphone sensing raises privacy concerns:** Sleep Cycle listens all night, stores/analyzes audio. MyLife is manual journaling — user controls what is captured.
- **No dream capture:** Sleep Cycle is physical-metrics focused. MyLife has dedicated dream journal with pattern analysis.
- **Subscription fatigue:** $40/yr for one function vs MyLife suite ~$70-120/yr for 30 modules.
- **Public financials create pressure:** quarterly earnings may push Sleep Cycle toward ads or data monetization; MyLife privacy position is structurally durable.
