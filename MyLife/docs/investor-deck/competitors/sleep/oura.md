# Oura — Deep Dive (vs MySleep)

**Module:** sleep
**Tier:** adjacent (hardware-first sleep + readiness tracking)
**Founded:** 2013
**HQ:** Oulu, Finland / San Francisco, CA
**Status:** private (late-stage, $5B+ valuation)

## One-line
Oura Ring is the category-defining sleep + readiness wearable — a small titanium ring on the finger that outsells Fitbit in premium sleep tracking, now at 2.5M+ rings sold.

## Product
- Oura Ring (Gen 3, Gen 4) — finger-worn sensor measuring HRV, body temp, SpO2, sleep stages
- Sleep Score, Readiness Score, Activity Score daily
- Cycle tracking, stress management, daytime nap detection
- App with deep health insights, Apple Health/Google Fit integration
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android (companion); ring is hardware
- **Pricing:** Ring $299-549 one-time + Oura membership $5.99/mo ($70/yr) for full app features
- **Distinguishing UX:** all-day passive tracking in a discreet form factor; elite-tier sleep science

## How they make money (precise)
Primary: ring hardware at 40-50% gross margin ($299-549 retail). Secondary: membership subscription at $70/yr per user — pure-margin recurring revenue on top of hardware. Tertiary: B2B health-system partnerships (Dexcom CGM tie-in, insurer wellness programs). Unit economics: CAC ~$30-60 via paid media; lifetime value $300 hardware + $70/yr × ~4yr avg = ~$580 per customer.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **$300+ hardware barrier:** Oura is for affluent early adopters. MyLife MySleep works with zero hardware.
- **Hardware + sub double-charge:** $549 ring + $70/yr is a $619 year-one cost; MyLife suite at ~$100/yr includes sleep + 29 others.
- **Ring only (single surface):** Oura cannot integrate dreams, subjective mood, life context. MyLife journal captures the story behind the number.
- **Cloud dependency:** Oura data lives on Oura servers; account deletion loses history. MyLife keeps data local, under user control.
- **Not for kids/teens:** rings are expensive and adult-sized. MyLife appeals to full age range.
