# Bookly — Deep Dive (vs MyBooks)

**Module:** books
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2016
**HQ:** Cluj-Napoca, Romania
**Status:** private (indie)

## One-line
Reading-timer-first tracker with gamified stats and infographics; the runner-up to Goodreads/StoryGraph on mobile, strongest on iOS.

## Product
- Reading timer with ambient sounds
- Infographic per-book summaries ("Beautiful Reports")
- Quote and thought capture per book
- TBR list, reading goals (books/pages/minutes)
- Streaks and reading pace estimate
- Platforms: iOS, Android (no native web; limited sync)
- Pricing: free (1 active book) / Pro $4.99/mo or $29.99/yr; lifetime ~$79.99
- Distinguishing UX choice: timer + infographic shareable cards ("for Instagram stories")

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Pro subscription** ($4.99/mo or $29.99/yr): unlimited books, full stats, ambient sounds, custom themes.
2. **Lifetime IAP** (~$79.99): front-loaded revenue, popular with BookTok audience.
3. No ads, no affiliate. Pure paid-app model.

Unit economics: estimated >80% gross margin; high IAP-mix yields strong LTV even at low MAU.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Single-app silo**: Bookly is beautiful but isolated. MyBooks sits inside a suite with MyJournal, MyFlash, MyMood — reading data flows into reflection and vocab learning.
- **Cloud-account required for cross-device sync**: MyBooks syncs via local-first primitives without an account.
- **No ePub reader, no book clubs, no community challenges**: Bookly is a tracker; MyBooks is a full reading platform.
- **Per-module pricing**: $30/yr for one category vs $99/yr for 28 modules.
