# The StoryGraph — Deep Dive (vs MyBooks)

**Module:** books
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2019
**HQ:** London, UK
**Status:** private (bootstrapped)

## One-line
A Black-founded, indie Goodreads alternative that wins on mood/pace recommendations and charts; the post-Amazon reader migration darling.

## Product
- Shelves (To Read, Currently Reading, Read, DNF)
- Mood and pace tags (emotional, adventurous, reflective, fast, medium, slow)
- Reading Challenges (user-created, not just count goals)
- Stats dashboards (page counts, genre mix, mood mix, star curves)
- Goodreads CSV import; buddy reads
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: free (core) / Plus $4.99/mo or $49.99/yr
- Distinguishing UX choice: "Did you like it?" multi-axis review (mood, pace, character-driven vs plot-driven)

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Plus subscriptions** ($4.99/mo or $49.99/yr): unlocks additional stats, unlimited custom challenges, buddy reads with voice.
2. **Bookshop.org affiliate links**: book detail pages link to Bookshop.org (indie-bookstore-friendly) instead of Amazon.
3. No display ads. No data sold.

Unit economics: single-digit employees + Ruby/Rails + Heroku → low COGS, estimated >70% gross margin on Plus.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Still cloud-dependent**: StoryGraph requires an account, stores data on servers. MyBooks is local-first.
- **No ePub reader**: StoryGraph tracks books but doesn't let you read them. MyBooks has built-in reader with annotations.
- **Ecosystem gap**: no journal, no quotes FTS, no flashcards. MyBooks connects reading → MyJournal → MyFlash vocabulary.
- **Single price point**: $4.99/mo for one category; MyLife suite amortizes across 28 modules for $99/yr.
