## One-line
A subscription tracker that ironically monetizes via subscription — clean iOS/Android design, widget-forward, and a growing Pro tier positioning Subby as the modern upgrade path from Bobby.

## Product
- Manual entry of recurring subscriptions, categories, colors
- Home screen widgets + lock screen widgets (iOS 16+)
- Calendar view, cost breakdowns, upcoming renewals
- iCloud sync, Family Sharing
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android
- **Pricing:** Free (limited features); Subby Pro subscription ~$2.99/mo or ~$11.99/yr, lifetime ~$29.99
- **Distinguishing UX:** strong widget game, focus on glanceability and aesthetics

## How they make money (precise)
Primary: Subby Pro subscriptions at ~$12/yr. Secondary: lifetime purchase (~$30) for users who prefer one-time. Unit economics favorable because hosting costs are trivial (iCloud sync handled by Apple).

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Subscription-to-track-subscriptions irony:** users find it distasteful to pay $12/yr to track the $100/mo they already pay. MyLife includes subs tracking in suite — no standalone sub required.
- **Manual entry only:** same limitation as Bobby. MyLife plans opt-in bank-payee detection.
- **Thin feature set:** Subby is effectively Bobby with different aesthetics. MyLife adds price-comparison, renewal-calendar integration with MyBudget, and cross-module awareness.
- **Unverified indie:** app risks abandonment (single developer). MyLife is backed by suite revenue across 30 modules.
- **No Family Sharing optimization:** Subby has basic Family Share; MyLife handles multi-user via hub auth + shared subscription tier.
