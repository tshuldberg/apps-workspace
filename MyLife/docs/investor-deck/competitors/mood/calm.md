# Calm — Deep Dive (vs MyMood)

**Module:** mood
**Tier:** adjacent (meditation + sleep + mood wellness)
**Founded:** 2012
**HQ:** San Francisco, CA
**Status:** private (unicorn; $2B valuation 2020; ~$217M total raised)

## One-line
#1 meditation + sleep app globally with celebrity content, now converging on mood/mental health via Calm Health B2B — but still a guided-content business, not a mood engine.

## Product
- Guided meditations, sleep stories, soundscapes, masterclasses (LeBron, Harry Styles, Matthew McConaughey)
- Breathwork, daily mindfulness, Mood Check-Ins (added 2022-2023)
- Calm Health: B2B clinical programs for employers/insurers
- Pricing: Calm Premium $14.99/mo or $69.99/yr; Lifetime $399.99
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web, Apple TV

## Acquisition / Exit
Independent as of 2026-04. Most recent round: Series C Dec 2020 at $2B. No acquisition talks public. At $2B last-round valuation against estimated $300M revenue, market pressure may trigger (a) IPO window, (b) down-round growth financing, or (c) strategic acquisition by media/wellness platforms (Apple, Disney, Peloton) or healthcare (UnitedHealth, CVS Health).

## Users
- 140M+ lifetime downloads
- 3.5M subscribers (2025), down from 4M (2024) per Statista
- Geography: US-heavy, English-language content dominates
- Conversion free→paid: low single digits

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Calm Premium** ($69.99/yr): primary revenue. Unlocks full library.
2. **Calm Lifetime** ($399.99): one-time. Small share but high ARPU.
3. **Calm for Business**: employer wellness benefit ($8-15/employee/mo).
4. **Calm Health**: clinical mental-health programs for insurers.
5. **Partnerships**: co-branded content with hotels, airlines, health systems.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Content-first, not data-first.** Calm's business is producing and licensing guided content. MyMood's business is the user's own mood data + cross-module correlation. Different center of gravity.
- **No A/B lifestyle experiments.** MyMood ships Pearson-analyzed A/B experiments with conclusion generation; Calm has no per-user experimentation framework.
- **No Plutchik emotion taxonomy.** Calm tracks mood via 1-5 emoji; MyMood tracks 24 emotions with intensity 1-3.
- **No virtual pet, no soundscape mixer, no crisis flow with grounding + emergency contacts + affirmations.** MyMood's `src/engine/sos.ts` + `mo_emergency_contacts` is a category the meditation giants don't ship.
- **Pricing.** $69.99/yr Calm alone vs $99/yr MyLife suite — and MyMood is in the free tier.
