# Headspace — Deep Dive (vs MyMood)

**Module:** mood
**Tier:** adjacent (meditation + clinical behavioral health)
**Founded:** 2010
**HQ:** Santa Monica, CA
**Status:** private (Headspace Health; post-2021 Ginger merger, ~$3B combined valuation)

## One-line
The #2 mindfulness app, now a $3B digital behavioral health company pairing meditation content with Ginger's teletherapy — a B2B machine with a consumer app front-end.

## Product
- Guided meditations, sleepcasts, focus music, mindful moves
- Headspace XR (Meta Quest), Headspace for Kids
- Ginger: on-demand teletherapy + coaching (from 2022 merger)
- Clinical programs for Medicare/Medicaid/employer partners
- Pricing: Headspace $12.99/mo or $69.99/yr; enterprise custom
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web, Apple TV, Meta Quest

## Acquisition / Exit
Independent as of 2026-04. Notable structural event: Oct 2022 merger with Ginger formed Headspace Health at $3B. Blackstone Growth, Spectrum Equity, and Cigna Ventures became major cap-table holders via the merger. Most likely exit paths: (a) IPO when healthtech markets re-open, (b) strategic acquisition by a major insurer (UnitedHealth, Cigna — already an investor), or (c) private-equity recap.

## Users
- 100M+ downloads (Headspace app)
- 2.8M D2C subscribers + 100M consumer reach via B2B employer/plan coverage
- 2,700+ enterprise customers; 2,200+ companies via Headspace for Work
- Geography: US + UK primary

## How they make money (precise)
1. **D2C subscriptions** ($69.99/yr): ~60% of revenue.
2. **Headspace for Work** (employer benefit, $5-12/employee/mo): fast-growing.
3. **Health plan partnerships** (Cigna, Medicare Advantage deals): largest-value contracts.
4. **Ginger clinical services** (teletherapy, coaching): per-session or PEPM.
5. **Content licensing** (Netflix "Headspace Guide" specials): marketing + small revenue.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **B2B dependency.** Headspace's growth story is employer/health-plan sales; consumer app is becoming the funnel. MyMood is user-owned from day one.
- **No on-device intelligence engine.** Headspace tells you to meditate; it doesn't tell you "your mood has correlated with <6hr sleep in the last 14 days." MyMood ships 8 algorithmic insight detectors.
- **No SOS / crisis flow with grounding + emergency contacts** — Headspace has crisis articles, not an interactive grounding sequence with 5-senses + affirmations + custom contacts.
- **Clinical data goes to insurers.** Headspace Ginger sessions generate PHI; MyMood is SQLite + PIN-locked on device.
- **Pricing.** $69.99/yr Headspace vs free MyMood inside $99/yr MyLife suite.
