# Daylio — Deep Dive (vs MyMood)

**Module:** mood
**Tier:** direct (micro-journal / mood tracker category leader)
**Founded:** 2015
**HQ:** Bratislava, Slovakia
**Status:** private (bootstrapped, unfunded)

## One-line
The best-loved micro-journal — no typing, just mood + activity icons — built by a solo founder in Slovakia and still bootstrapped at ~$1.2M/yr revenue with 10M+ downloads.

## Product
- Tap-based mood entry (5 levels) + activities grid
- Stats, year-in-pixels, export, cloud backup
- Themes, goals (premium)
- Pricing: free; Premium $3.99/mo or $23.99/yr (varies by region)
- Platforms: iOS, Android (no web)

1. **Daylio Premium subscription** ($3.99/mo or $23.99/yr): 100% of revenue. Unlocks unlimited mood types, advanced stats, themes, goal sharing, PIN lock.
Unit economics: tiny opex, very high gross margins (solo founder, App Store rev shares).

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Micro-journal ceiling.** Daylio's simplicity is its gift and its cap: it will never ship CBT thought records, meditation timer + soundscape mixer, A/B experiments, SOS flow. MyMood already has all four.
- **No 24-emotion Plutchik taxonomy.** Daylio is 5 moods; MyMood is 24 emotions with 3 intensity levels.
- **No virtual pet, no focus sessions, no emergency contacts.** MyMood ships all three.
- **No cross-module.** Daylio data lives in Daylio; MyMood feeds the hub's Pearson engine with 9 other modules.
- **No web surface.** Daylio is mobile-only; MyMood is mobile + web.
- **Bundle.** $23.99/yr Daylio Premium vs MyMood shipping for free inside $99/yr MyLife suite.
