# Honey (PayPal) — Deep Dive (vs MyShop)

**Module:** shop
**Tier:** direct (wishlist + deal alerts overlap)
**Founded:** 2012
**HQ:** Los Angeles, CA
**Status:** subsidiary (PayPal Holdings, acquired Jan 2020)

## One-line
Browser extension and app that auto-applies coupons and tracks wishlisted items across 30,000+ retailers; PayPal's $4B bet to own the checkout moment — and the purchase intent that precedes it.

## Product
- Browser extension (Chrome/Safari/Edge) that auto-applies coupons at checkout
- Droplist: price drop alerts on saved items across any retailer
- PayPal Rewards (Honey Gold) cashback on partner merchants
- Price history graphs for items
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, browser extensions (Chrome/Safari/Firefox/Edge)
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Distinguishing UX:** friction-free coupon stacking at checkout — "found X coupons, saved $Y"

## Acquisition / Exit
- Acquired by: PayPal Holdings (NASDAQ: PYPL)
- Date: 2020-01
- Price: $4B cash (largest PayPal acquisition ever)
- Current status inside parent: integrated into PayPal's checkout surface and rewards program; separate brand maintained
- Strategic rationale: upstream intent capture (before checkout) to drive PayPal usage at checkout; merchant-services cross-sell
- Founder outcome: George Ruan and Ryan Hudson each netted ~$1-1.5B estimated; both stayed through brief integration period then departed by 2022

## How they make money (precise)
Primary: affiliate fees from retailers when Honey surfaces a coupon used at checkout (typical 2-10% of order value). PayPal integrates Honey-triggered discovery into its "pay with PayPal" flow to lift checkout conversion (PayPal's real business). Secondary: data insights for PayPal's advertising/merchant services. Tertiary: PayPal Honey Gold points redeemed against PayPal balance, creating PayPal engagement.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Surveillance business model:** Honey monitors every e-commerce site you visit, by design. MyLife keeps wishlists local.
- **Recent controversy:** 2024 YouTuber exposés (MegaLag) showed Honey "stealing" affiliate attribution from content creators, damaging brand trust.
- **No purchase/warranty tracking:** Honey is pre-checkout only. MyLife owns the post-purchase life: receipts, warranty, returns, satisfaction.
- **Retailer dependency:** Honey only works at integrated merchants. MyLife wishlists work for any URL, local store, thrift find, DIY.
- **Bundling:** MyLife suite at ~$7-10/mo vs Honey "free" — but free here means "we monetize your intent".
