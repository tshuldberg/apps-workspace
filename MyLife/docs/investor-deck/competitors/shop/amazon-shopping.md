# Amazon Shopping — Deep Dive (vs MyShop)

**Module:** shop
**Tier:** adjacent (dominates wishlist + purchase history as a by-product of commerce)
**Founded:** 1994 (Amazon); Shopping app ~2008
**HQ:** Seattle, WA
**Status:** public (NASDAQ: AMZN)

## One-line
Amazon Shopping is the default universal wishlist and purchase journal for 200M+ US Prime members — not by design, but because most household commerce flows through Amazon.

## Product
- Universal shopping cart + wishlists (public, private, gift registries, baby, wedding)
- Order history with return/warranty portal
- Price tracking on saved items, "deal alerts"
- Alexa voice shopping, AR try-on (some categories)
- **Platforms:** iOS, Android, Web, Fire OS, Echo
- **Pricing:** Free; Prime subscription $14.99/mo or $139/yr (shipping + video + deals)
- **Distinguishing UX:** end-to-end commerce pipeline — discover, buy, track, return in one flow

## How they make money (precise)
Shopping is not monetized as an app — it is the funnel. Primary: 1P retail margin on goods. Secondary: 3P seller fees (~15% referral + FBA fees). Tertiary: Prime subscription ($139/yr) upsell and advertising on product pages ($56B ads in 2024). Wishlist/purchase history is a strategic data asset that powers recommendations, retargeting, and Amazon's ad business.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Amazon-only:** Amazon wishlists cannot track non-Amazon items (Etsy, local stores, direct-to-consumer). MyLife shop is universal.
- **Surveillance-based:** Amazon monetizes shopping intent via ads and recommendations. MyLife keeps intent local.
- **No warranty/receipt vault:** Amazon tracks its own orders but not Apple Store, Best Buy, or in-person purchases. MyLife has universal receipt capture.
- **No per-person gift memory:** Amazon registries are event-scoped. MyLife's gifts-per-person view tracks lifetime giving without retargeting.
