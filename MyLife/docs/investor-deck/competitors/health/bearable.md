# Bearable — Deep Dive (vs MyHealth)

**Module:** health
**Tier:** direct (chronic-illness symptom + health correlator)
**Founded:** 2020
**HQ:** Europe (mainland, distributed)
**Status:** private (bootstrapped two-person team)

## One-line
The symptom + mood + med tracker beloved by the chronic-illness community, built around user-defined factors and correlation reports.

## Product
- Track mood, energy, symptoms, medications, vitals, sleep, diet, activity with custom factors
- Correlation reports (Pearson) across any two factors
- Custom experiments, unlimited goals (paid), historical data export
- Pricing: free core; Subscription $34.99/yr (often 40-76% discounted)
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Apple Watch (no web)

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Optional annual subscription** ($34.99/yr ≈ $0.10/day): unlocks unlimited Goals, custom Experiments, Correlation reports, full historical access.
2. **Frequent promotional discounts** (up to 76% off): keeps ARPPU lower but increases conversion.
Unit economics: ultra-low opex two-person team; churn-resilient because the user's multi-year data lives in the app.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **No web + no desktop.** Bearable is iOS/Android only. MyHealth and MyMeds ship web too.
- **No medication interaction checker.** Bearable tracks meds but has no 200+ pair interactions DB. MyMeds (re-exported via MyHealth) does.
- **No doctor report.** Bearable exports CSV; MyHealth generates markdown doctor reports that clinicians actually read.
- **No cross-module integration.** Bearable is a single silo; MyHealth correlates with MyMood, MyHabits, MyJournal, MyFast, MyCycle inside the suite.
- **Bundle price.** $35/yr Bearable alone vs $99/yr MyLife suite covering 31 modules.
