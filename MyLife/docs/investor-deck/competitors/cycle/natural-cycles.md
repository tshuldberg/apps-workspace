# Natural Cycles — Deep Dive (vs MyCycle)

**Module:** cycle
**Tier:** direct (FDA-cleared contraception niche)
**Founded:** 2013
**HQ:** Stockholm, Sweden
**Status:** private (VC-backed; IPO exploration reported)

## One-line
The only FDA-cleared digital contraceptive; basal body temperature + algorithm replaces hormonal birth control for millions of users; regulatory moat is the product.

## Product
- Daily BBT entry (via Bluetooth thermometer, Oura Ring, or Apple Watch integration)
- Algorithm delivers "green day" / "red day" recommendation
- Cycle log, pregnancy mode, post-pregnancy mode
- FDA-cleared (Aug 2018) Class II medical device
- Platforms: iOS, Android (thermometer hardware accessory)
- Pricing: $8.99/mo or $99.99/yr (often bundled with thermometer)
- Distinguishing UX choice: a medical device, not just a tracker — regulatory approval gives trust premium

## Users

- Demographic: heavily monogamous partnered women, 25-40

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Subscription** ($8.99/mo or $99.99/yr): core app; thermometer often included in annual plan.
2. **Hardware** (thermometer bundled or separate).
3. **Wearable integrations** (Oura, Apple Watch) — no direct revenue but retention hook.

Unit economics: high ARPU ($100-150 effective) but hardware COGS ~$15-20; CAC has spiked post-US expansion; churn elevated due to pregnancy-seeking users naturally leaving.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Regulatory focus narrows product**: Natural Cycles is a birth-control app, not a general tracker. MyCycle covers BBT + predictions + partner sync + pregnancy + community without the FDA submission burden (we are informational, not diagnostic).
- **Hardware dependency**: Natural Cycles' best mode requires a Bluetooth thermometer. MyCycle supports manual or any Bluetooth thermometer without vendor lock-in.
- **Cloud-based with account**: data lives on server; necessary for FDA audit, but limits privacy. MyCycle is local-first.
- **Narrow demographic appeal**: NC skews married-couples 25-40. MyCycle covers teens-to-menopause with no regulatory gatekeeping.
