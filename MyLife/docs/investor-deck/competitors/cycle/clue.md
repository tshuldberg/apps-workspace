# Clue — Deep Dive (vs MyCycle)

**Module:** cycle
**Tier:** direct
**Founded:** 2013
**HQ:** Berlin, Germany
**Status:** private (VC-backed)

## One-line
A science-first, design-driven period tracker positioned as the anti-pink alternative; strong in Europe, with FDA-cleared Clue Birth Control (digital contraception) launched 2021.

## Product
- Cycle log (period, symptoms, flow, mood, sex)
- Predictions and averages
- Pregnancy mode (+Pregnancy Tracking)
- Clue Birth Control (FDA-cleared digital contraceptive, 2021)
- Science-content heavy (medically reviewed articles)
- Platforms: iOS, Android, Web
- Pricing: Free (Clue Basic) / Clue Plus $9.99/mo or $49.99/yr / Birth Control add-on (additional)
- Distinguishing UX choice: gender-neutral, clinical design and strong science editorial standards

## Users

- Paid subscribers: ~500K-800K est.
- Geography: Europe heavy (Germany, France, UK); US present; strong in Latin America
- Strong brand equity in science/health community

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Clue Plus subscription** ($9.99/mo or $49.99/yr): advanced analysis, pattern insights.
2. **Clue Birth Control** (FDA-cleared subscription, additional fee): digital contraceptive tier.
3. **Content partnerships** with research institutions (UNICEF, Kinsey Institute).

Unit economics: low ARPU + high clinical content cost + heavy regulatory overhead (FDA); thin margins.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Cloud-first with account required**: MyCycle runs local-only; no account needed.
- **Clue Birth Control is regulated US-only**: adds complexity without revenue lift outside the US.
- **Revenue per user low**: Clue has more reach than monetization. MyLife suite spreads fixed costs across 28 modules, letting MyCycle ship depth without per-module P&L.
- **Partner sync thin**: Clue Connect is a toggle. MyCycle partner-sync has granular field-level privacy (share temperature, hide mood).
