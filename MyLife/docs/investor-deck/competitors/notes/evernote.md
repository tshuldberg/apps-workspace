# Evernote — Deep Dive (vs MyNotes)

**Module:** notes
**Tier:** direct (legacy market leader, now under Bending Spoons)
**Founded:** 2008
**HQ:** Redwood City, CA (originally); operations relocated to Milan/Italy post-acquisition
**Status:** acquired (Bending Spoons, 2022 announced / early 2023 closed)

## One-line
The once-dominant note-taking giant, bought out of decline by Bending Spoons and squeezed into profitability by aggressively paywalling features that used to be free.

## Product
- Rich notes with images, audio, PDFs, web clips
- Powerful OCR and search inside PDFs and images
- Templates, tasks, calendar sync, home dashboard
- Platforms: Web, macOS, Windows, iOS, Android
- Pricing: Free (limited to 50 notes, 1 notebook since 2023 paywall) / Personal $14.99/mo / Professional $17.99/mo / Teams $24.99/user/mo
- Distinguishing UX choice: still the strongest server-side OCR across handwritten notes and scanned PDFs

## Acquisition / Exit
- Acquired by: Bending Spoons (private, Milan, Italy)
- Date: 2022-11 (announced) / 2023-01 (closed)
- Price: undisclosed (~$200M est., well below peak $1B+ valuation)
- Current status inside parent: standalone brand, operations relocated to Italy, US/Chile offices closed
- Strategic rationale: classic Bending Spoons playbook — acquire tired consumer brand with recurring revenue, cut staff, aggressively paywall, extract cash flow (same pattern used for Meetup, Remini, WeTransfer)
- Founder outcome: Phil Libin (ex-CEO) long gone (left 2015); at-acquisition CEO Ian Small departed; most long-tenured staff laid off in 2023 waves

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Personal/Professional subscriptions** ($14.99-$17.99/mo): 10x price of Bear, 2x price of Notion Plus.
2. **Teams** ($24.99/user/mo): small B2B line.
3. **Aggressive free-tier restrictions** (50 notes, 1 notebook, 1 device for free) driving conversion since late 2023.

Unit economics: server costs are significant (Evernote stores large media files). Bending Spoons reportedly achieved profitability via infrastructure consolidation and paywall aggression.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Brand damage:** Bending Spoons' paywall-and-layoff playbook generated significant negative press and a user exodus to Obsidian, Notion, Bear, and Apple Notes. MyNotes captures the privacy-minded migrator.
- **Cloud-only with price escalation:** Evernote raised prices 3x in 2 years. MyNotes is SQLite-local, no account, included in the $99/yr suite.
- **Stagnant product investment:** under Bending Spoons, Evernote focuses on monetization not innovation. MyNotes ships infinite canvas + Notion-style databases + knowledge-graph intelligence in one module.
- **No on-device AI:** Evernote's AI (launched 2024) requires a Professional subscription. MyNotes ships local AI for free.
