# Obsidian — Deep Dive (vs MyNotes)

**Module:** notes
**Tier:** direct (local-first, knowledge-graph first)
**Founded:** 2020
**HQ:** Oakville, Ontario, Canada (parent: Dynalist Inc.)
**Status:** private (bootstrapped, no VC)

## One-line
The power-user, local-first markdown vault with [[wiki links]] and a graph view; MyNotes's closest architectural twin, shipping as a flat file system instead of a consumer app.

## Product
- Local markdown vault (folder-of-files, user-owned)
- [[Wiki links]], backlinks, graph view, tag co-occurrence
- 1000+ community plugins (Obsidian's real moat)
- Canvas (infinite whiteboard with nodes + embeds)
- Sync ($4-8/mo), Publish ($16/mo), Commercial license ($50/user/yr)
- Platforms: macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android
- Pricing: free (personal); Sync $48-$96/yr; Publish $192/yr; Commercial $50/user/yr; Catalyst one-time $25-$100 (early supporter)
- Distinguishing UX choice: your notes are plain .md files on disk — portable, git-able, bulletproof

## How they make money (precise)
1. **Obsidian Sync** ($4/mo personal, $8/mo standard): E2E encrypted cross-device vault sync — the primary revenue line.
2. **Obsidian Publish** ($16/mo): turn your vault into a public website; prosumer/researcher market.
3. **Commercial license** ($50/user/yr): required for employers; low-friction B2B revenue.
4. **Catalyst** ($25-$100 one-time): early-supporter perk; meaningful cumulative revenue.

Unit economics: Sync gross margin est. >85%; Publish CDN costs real but amortized; team of 18 runs lean.

## Wedge MyLife can exploit
- **Desktop-native, mobile-secondary:** Obsidian mobile is capable but second-class. MyNotes is mobile-first (Expo) with true iOS/Android parity.
- **No canvas intelligence:** Obsidian Canvas is visual but doesn't run graph analysis, hub detection, or articulation-point identification. MyNotes ships knowledge-discovery as built-in engines.
- **No OCR on attachments:** Obsidian indexes markdown only. MyNotes has FTS5 over OCR-extracted text from image/PDF attachments.
- **No AI assistant included:** every Obsidian AI experience is a community plugin that you wire to OpenAI yourself. MyNotes ships on-device AI (summarize/grammar/simplify) at zero cost.
- **Plugin ecosystem is its moat, not its product:** Obsidian's power comes from community plugins the average consumer never installs. MyLife targets that consumer with batteries-included defaults.
- **Suite pricing:** $99/yr for 30 modules vs $48-96/yr for Sync alone.
