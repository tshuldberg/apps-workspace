# MyLife Kickstarter Campaign Video Script

**Duration:** 75 seconds
**Format:** Founder talking head + product b-roll intercut
**Tone:** Calm, direct, no hype. Confident but not salesy.
**Music:** Minimal ambient. Fades under voice, never competes.

---

## SCRIPT

**[OPEN: Trey at a desk, phone in hand. Natural light. No studio.]**

TREY: Two years ago I added up what I was paying for apps. Calm, Strava, YNAB, MyFitnessPal, Flo, Day One, AllTrails, Planta. Eight hundred and fifty dollars a year. Eleven different apps. Eleven different companies sitting on my mood, my workouts, my finances, my body.

**[CUT TO: Phone screen showing competitor app icons with prices stacking up. Total: $755/yr.]**

Then Flo got caught sharing pregnancy data with Facebook. Then Mint shut down. Then Calm raised its price again.

**[CUT TO: Trey, closer.]**

So I quit all of them and built one app to replace all of them.

**[CUT TO: Screen recording scrolling through the MyLife dashboard. 30 module icons visible. Smooth, dark UI.]**

TREY (V/O): MyLife. Thirty modules. Mood, meds, cycle, habits, health, workouts, trails, recipes, nutrition, budget, books, journal, notes, voice, and sixteen more. One app. Every device.

**[CUT TO: Paywall screen showing "$12/year". Tap. Unlocked.]**

Twelve dollars a year. Locked at that price for life.

**[CUT TO: Trey, direct to camera.]**

Five of the modules are completely free. No account required, no asterisk. The other twenty-five unlock for a dollar a month.

Twenty-six of the thirty modules store everything on your phone. I can't read your data. I can't sell your data. I can't lose your data in a breach.

**[CUT TO: Brief montage of module screens: mood check-in, workout log, recipe import, budget envelope, journal entry. 2 seconds each.]**

TREY (V/O): I built this alone over eighteen months using AI tools. No venture capital. No investors telling me to raise the price. No ad network watching you use it.

**[CUT TO: Trey.]**

I'm incorporating as a Public Benefit Corporation and writing six promises into the corporate charter: never sell data, never raise prices on you, never run ads, never use dark patterns, never sell to private equity, never take money that requires breaking those promises.

**[CUT TO: The manifesto page scrolling slowly on a laptop screen.]**

Those aren't marketing lines. They're legally binding.

**[CUT TO: Trey, warm, final.]**

If you're tired of being the product, back us on Kickstarter. Twelve dollars. A year. Forever.

**[END CARD: MyLife logo on #131318 background. Tagline: "Thirty privacy-first apps. One subscription. One dollar a month. Forever." Kickstarter URL below.]**

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Length check:** Read-aloud timing at natural pace: ~73 seconds. Trim b-roll if over 80.
- **B-roll needed:** 4-5 screen recordings of the real app (dashboard scroll, mood check-in, paywall tap, recipe import, budget screen). Record on iPhone with screen recording, no simulator.
- **Audio:** Record voice in a quiet room with a lapel mic or AirPods Pro. Post-process: noise reduction, light compression, no reverb.
- **Editing:** iMovie or DaVinci Resolve (free). Jump cuts on the talking head are fine. No transitions. No text overlays except the end card.
- **Thumbnail:** Still frame of Trey holding phone + "$12/year" text overlay. Use Cool Obsidian colors.
- **Accessibility:** Add captions. Kickstarter supports SRT upload.
