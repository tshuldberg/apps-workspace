## Category 1: Kickstarter Software Campaigns

### 1.1 Successful Software/App Campaigns ($100K+)

Software and app campaigns on Kickstarter are rare successes. Only 4.2% of all successful projects raise over $100K, and software projects face an additional headwind: Kickstarter's backer network is not especially primed for software products compared to hardware, games, and physical goods. That said, several software campaigns have broken through.

#### Top Performing App Campaigns

| Campaign | Amount Raised | Backers | Year | Category |
|----------|---------------|---------|------|----------|
| **Reading Rainbow** (app expansion) | $5,408,916 | 105,857 | 2014 | Education/App |
| **memoryOS** | $642,014 | 2,862 | 2021 | Education/App |
| **Fluent Forever** (language app) | $587,000 | 1,853 | 2017 | Education/App |
| **Pillars of Eternity** (Obsidian Entertainment) | ~$4,000,000 | 73,986 | 2012 | Game/Software |
| **Proton Mail** (Indiegogo, not Kickstarter) | $550,377 | 10,576 | 2014 | Privacy/Email |
| **Pixelfed + Loops + Sup** (fediverse apps) | $81,000+ (ongoing) | 1,773+ | 2025 | Privacy/Social |
| **Krita** (open source painting) | Goal met (smaller) | Community | 2015 | Creative/Software |

#### Why These Campaigns Succeeded

**memoryOS (most funded app in Kickstarter history):**
- Became 6,400% funded (goal was only $10,000)
- Called their first couple thousand leads by phone for personal connection
- Used Facebook/Instagram conversion ads to landing page
- Built community army before launch
- Kept video under 3 minutes
- Received Kickstarter's "Project We Love" badge

**Fluent Forever ($587K):**
- Started with only 500-person mailing list, ended with 1,853 backers (3-4X multiplier)
- Worked with a Kickstarter consultant focused on maximizing Day 1 pledges
- Hit $100K within first 53 minutes of launch
- Subscription-based reward structure with different plans at different tiers

**Proton Mail ($550K on Indiegogo):**
- Hit $100K goal in 3 days, climbed to $200K two days later
- Privacy mission resonated deeply with post-Snowden audience
- "No one had ever built a technology company through crowdfunding" framing
- Grew from crowdfunding to 100 million users by 2024
- Lesson: PayPal froze their account mid-campaign ($251K locked). Have backup payment channels.

**Pixelfed/Loops/Sup ($81K+ in first week, 2025):**
- "No ads, no algorithms, no tracking" messaging
- Open source + fediverse values alignment
- Anti-Meta sentiment driving interest
- Stretch goal: register as nonprofit foundation

#### Key Patterns for Software Success

1. **Mission-driven framing beats feature lists.** Proton Mail and Pixelfed succeeded on values, not specs.
2. **Community before campaign.** Every six- and seven-figure campaign had an army of fans before launch.
3. **Low goal, high overfunding.** memoryOS set $10K goal and hit $642K. The 6,400% funded badge drives algorithmic visibility.
4. **Subscription rewards work.** Fluent Forever and memoryOS both used tiered subscription access as rewards.
5. **Education and privacy resonate.** The top app campaigns cluster around education and privacy/values themes.

### 1.2 Exact Steps to Launch a Kickstarter Campaign

#### Step 1: Create Account
- **URL:** https://www.kickstarter.com/signup
- Sign up with email or Apple ID
- No cost to create an account

#### Step 2: Start Your Project
- **URL:** https://www.kickstarter.com/start
- Select your project category (Technology > Software or Technology > Apps)
- Begin filling in the project editor

#### Step 3: Project Editor (Build Campaign Page)
Required elements:
- **Title and blurb** (elevator pitch, use searchable keywords)
- **Campaign video** (under 2 minutes performs best; explain why you're launching and benefits to backers)
- **Story section** (detailed description, images, GIFs)
- **Reward tiers** (5-7 tiers recommended; see Section 1.4)
- **Funding goal** (locked once launched; cover production + shipping + fees + buffer)
- **Campaign duration** (30 days is standard; maximum 60 days)
- **Risks and challenges** section (required by Kickstarter)

#### Step 4: Payment Setup
- Add a Visa, Mastercard, or American Express credit/debit card in the Payment tab
- Set up your bank account for receiving funds
- Verify identity

#### Step 5: Pre-Launch Page
- Available after project submission for review
- Access via Promotion tab in project editor
- Customize with text, images, links using the Pre-Launch Editor
- Followers click "Notify me on launch" and receive email when you go live
- **Recommendation:** Publish pre-launch page at least 1 month before launch (earlier is better)
- Send pre-launch updates to keep followers engaged

#### Step 6: Submit for Review
- Click "Continue" at least 2 business days before planned launch
- Kickstarter strives to respond within 24 hours but can take up to a week
- If flagged, contact Kickstarter support; you can still edit while waiting

#### Step 7: Launch
- **Best timing:** Tuesday mornings, 7-10 AM EST
- First 48 hours are critical for algorithmic boost
- Getting featured on "Popular Projects" page requires strong initial surge

#### Step 8: Campaign Management
- Post regular updates (backer engagement)
- Respond to comments and messages
- Monitor analytics dashboard

#### Step 9: Post-Campaign
- Funds collected (minus fees) after campaign ends
- Kickstarter's new Pledge Management tools help with fulfillment logistics
- Send backer surveys, fulfill rewards

### 1.3 Kickstarter vs Alternatives for Software Products

#### Platform Comparison Matrix

| Feature | Kickstarter | Indiegogo | Republic | Wefunder |
|---------|-------------|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Type** | Rewards-based | Rewards-based | Equity crowdfunding | Equity crowdfunding |
| **Funding Model** | All-or-nothing only | Fixed only (flexible retired Sept 2025) | All-or-nothing | Flexible (keep what you raise) |
| **Platform Fee** | 5% | 5% (8% for InDemand non-originating) | 6% cash + 2% securities | 7.5% (waived if angel invests $25K+) |
| **Payment Processing** | 3-5% | ~3% + $0.20/txn | 1.5-2.25% escrow/processing | 2% ACH/wire (investor pays) |
| **Total Take** | ~8-10% | ~8-9% | ~8-10% + $3-5K compliance | ~7.5-9.5% |
| **What Backers Get** | Rewards (product/perks) | Rewards (product/perks) | Equity in your company | Equity in your company |
| **Regulatory** | None | None | SEC Reg CF (Form C required) | SEC Reg CF (Form C required) |
| **Upfront Costs** | $0 | $0 | $3,000-$5,000 (Form C + escrow) | $0 (legal package optional) |
| **Max Raise** | No limit | No limit | $5M/year (Reg CF) | $5M/year (Reg CF) |
| **Acceptance Rate** | Most accepted | Most accepted | ~5% of applicants | More lenient |
| **Success Rate** | ~40% overall | Lower | ~90% | ~75% |
| **Audience Size** | 24.5M backers, 284K+ funded projects | Large, but smaller than Kickstarter | 3M members | 1.5M+ investors |

#### Recommendation for MyLife

**Use Kickstarter for the $150-500K rewards campaign.** Reasons:

1. **Largest backer audience.** 24.5M backers vs. Indiegogo's smaller pool.
2. **All-or-nothing creates urgency.** Drives Day 1 pledges and algorithmic visibility.
3. **Privacy/mission campaigns have precedent.** Proton Mail proved the model (though on Indiegogo). Pixelfed proved it again on Kickstarter in 2025.
4. **No equity given up.** Backers get subscription access, not ownership.
5. **Indiegogo killed flexible funding.** As of September 2025, Indiegogo is fixed-only anyway, removing its main differentiator.

**Consider Wefunder or Republic for a separate equity crowdfunding round** alongside or after the Kickstarter, if you want community investors who own a piece. This would be distinct from the angel SAFE round.

- Wefunder is faster to launch and has lower upfront costs
- Republic has more sophisticated investors and stronger vetting (which signals quality)
- Both require SEC Reg CF compliance ($3-5K in legal/audit costs)

### 1.4 Best Practices for a $200K+ Software Campaign

#### Pre-Launch List Size

The single most important predictor of campaign success is pre-launch audience size.

| Target Raise | Recommended List Size | Conversion Rate Assumption |
|-------------|----------------------|---------------------------|
| $100K | 3,000-5,000 emails | 1-3% email, 30-40% reservation |
| $200K | 5,000-10,000 emails | 1-3% email, 30-40% reservation |
| $500K | 10,000-20,000 emails | 1-3% email, 30-40% reservation |

**Critical insight:** A $1 reservation deposit funnel converts at 30-40%, while plain email signups convert at only 1-3%. A reservation funnel with 2,000 depositors is worth more than 20,000 email subscribers.

**Pre-launch timeline:** 6-12 weeks of focused pre-launch work. For a $200K+ software campaign, plan 8-12 weeks minimum.

#### Reward Tier Structure for a $12/yr Subscription App

Best-performing campaigns have 5-7 tiers. For MyLife specifically:

| Tier | Price | Reward | Limit |
|------|-------|--------|-------|
| **Supporter** | $5 | Name on supporters wall, project updates | Unlimited |
| **Early Bird Annual** | $10 | 1 year MyLife Pro (17% off $12) | 500 |
| **Founding Member** | $25 | 3 years MyLife Pro + Founding Member badge | 1,000 |
| **Lifetime Access** | $79 | Lifetime MyLife Pro + Founding Member badge + name in app credits | 500 |
| **Lifetime Family** | $149 | Lifetime Pro for up to 5 family members + all above | 200 |
| **Builder's Circle** | $499 | All above + quarterly founder video calls + vote on next module priority | 50 |
| **Founding Patron** | $1,000 | All above + private dinner/call with founder + permanent patron recognition | 20 |

**Pricing psychology notes:**
- VIP/Super Early Bird tiers (first 48 hours): 20-55% off MSRP
- Regular Early Bird tiers: 15-28% off MSRP
- Most popular Kickstarter pledge is $25
- Best core-package prices for highest raises: $150, $200, $250, $300
- Lifetime access creates urgency and high-value pledges

#### Video Requirements

- **Length:** Under 2 minutes tracks best. Maximum 3 minutes.
- **Content:** Explain WHY you're building this, not just WHAT it does
- **Tone:** Authentic, not overly polished. Kickstarter audiences respond to founder sincerity.
- **Structure:** Problem (30s) > Solution/demo (45s) > Why now/why me (30s) > Call to action (15s)
- **Production:** Work with a studio that understands Kickstarter audiences. Hit emotional notes and position product visually.

#### Update Cadence

- **Pre-launch:** 1-2 updates per week to followers
- **Week 1:** Daily updates showing momentum, press coverage, milestone celebrations
- **Weeks 2-3:** 2-3 updates per week with new features, behind-the-scenes
- **Final week:** Daily countdown updates, stretch goal announcements

#### Press Coordination

- Prepare a press kit (screenshots, video, founder bio, key stats) 2 weeks before launch
- Reach out to tech/privacy journalists 1 week before launch with embargo offer
- Target publications: TechCrunch, The Verge, Ars Technica, Wired, privacy-focused outlets
- Time press hits to coincide with launch day or day 2 for maximum algorithmic boost

## Category 2: Angel Investor Platforms and Networks

### 2.1 AngelList / AngelList Venture

#### How It Works Now

AngelList has evolved from a simple startup directory into a full-stack venture infrastructure platform. It supports $171 billion in assets and 13,000+ active startups. The platform now primarily serves as infrastructure for fund managers and syndicates rather than direct founder-to-angel matchmaking.

**For founders raising capital, the relevant product is AngelList Syndicates/SPVs:**

1. A lead investor (GP) creates a Syndicate profile on AngelList
2. The GP sources a deal (your startup) and creates an SPV
3. LPs (angel investors in the syndicate) commit capital into the SPV
4. The SPV makes one consolidated investment in your company
5. Your cap table shows one entity, not dozens of individual investors

#### Fees

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Standard SPV setup fee | $8,000 |
| State regulatory filing | $2,000 |
| Follow-on SPV (same syndicate) | $5,000 + $2,000 filing |
| Total fees cap | 10% of raised amount |
| Ongoing administration | Included (K-1s, distributions) |

#### How to Use It for MyLife's Angel Round

AngelList is best used when you have a lead angel investor willing to organize a syndicate. The founder doesn't create the SPV; the lead investor does. Your job is to:

1. Find a lead angel who believes in the privacy/mission thesis
2. That angel creates a Syndicate on AngelList and invites their LP network
3. The SPV consolidates all angel checks into one cap table entry

**Minimum SPV raise:** $80K ($50K for follow-ons)

**Key URL:** https://www.angellist.com/fund-administration/spvs

#### Software Companies That Used AngelList

AngelList's network has funded thousands of startups. 41.5% of AngelList deals went to AI/ML startups in H1 2025, but the platform serves all sectors. The syndicate model is sector-agnostic.

### 2.2 Republic (Equity Crowdfunding)

#### How It Works

Republic is an SEC-registered funding portal for equity crowdfunding under Regulation CF. Companies apply, go through Republic's vetting (only ~5% accepted), then list their raise for both accredited and non-accredited investors.

#### Application Process

1. **Apply at** https://republic.com/ (click "Raise" or "For Founders")
2. Submit company details: founders, mission, product, business model
3. Undergo Republic's vetting process (strict; ~5% acceptance)
4. If accepted, prepare legal documents:
   - File Form C with SEC ($1,500)
   - Set up escrow (~$1,500)
   - Attorney review ($0-$3,500)
5. Build campaign page with video, deck, financials
6. Launch campaign

#### Fees

| Fee Type | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Cash fee | 6% of amount raised |
| Securities fee | 2% of amount raised (in equity) |
| Form C filing | ~$1,500 upfront |
| Escrow | ~$1,500 (after campaign) |
| Attorney review | $0-$3,500 |
| **Total effective cost** | **~10-12% including upfront costs** |

#### Raise Limits

- Reg CF: Up to $5M per year
- Average company raises ~$500K on Republic
- Minimum investment for backers: $25 (each startup sets its own minimum)

#### Notable Companies Funded via Republic

- **Carta** (Republic Capital invested $16.7M across 3 rounds; $8.5B valuation)
- **Firefly Aerospace** ($25.8M; $112M NASA contract)
- **Axiom Space** ($11M; first private ISS mission)
- 250+ companies funded since 2016
- 3M community members across 150 countries

**Note:** The above are Republic Capital (their venture arm) investments, not community Reg CF rounds. For Reg CF specifically, Republic facilitated $15.6M in raises in 2024.

### 2.3 Wefunder

#### How It Works

Wefunder is an SEC-registered funding portal for Regulation Crowdfunding. More accessible than Republic (less strict vetting, no upfront fees), with 1.5M+ investors on the platform.

#### Application Process

1. **Create a free profile** at https://wefunder.com
2. Build your pitch page (video, deck, financials, terms)
3. Launch in "testing the waters" phase to collect investment reservations before completing legal work
4. Complete Form C and financial disclosures
5. Meet the $50K threshold (required before funds can be withdrawn)
6. Campaign goes live for up to $5M in raises

#### Fees

| Fee Type | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Platform fee | 7.5% of funds raised |
| Angel fee waiver | All fees waived if an angel invests $25K+ |
| Upfront costs | $0 (optional all-in-one legal package available) |
| Investor ACH/wire fee | 2% (paid by investor) |

**Critical advantage for MyLife:** If you secure one angel check of $25K+ through Wefunder, all platform fees are waived. This could save $37,500+ on a $500K raise.

#### Raise Limits and Requirements

- Max raise: $5M/year under Reg CF
- Min raise to withdraw: $50K
- No minimum revenue or valuation required
- Must be U.S.-incorporated (C-Corp, LLC, S-Corp, or Benefit Corp all accepted)
- Not in restricted industries (cannabis, porn, gambling, investment)

#### Financial Disclosure Requirements (SEC Reg CF)

### 2.6 LinkedIn for Angel Outreach

#### Practical Tactics

**Warm intros convert at 10-20x the rate of cold connection requests** (Hustle Fund data). Prioritize warm paths.

#### Step-by-Step LinkedIn Strategy

1. **Optimize your profile** for investor discovery:
   - Headline: Clear mission statement ("Building MyLife -- 30 privacy-first lifestyle modules, $12/yr, no data sales")
   - Summary: Traction, progress, product-market fit. Not cleverness.
   - Featured section: Link to deck, demo video, or manifesto

2. **Build a target list** of mission-aligned angels:
   - Search for investors who've funded privacy, consumer, or mission-driven companies
   - Filter by "angel investor" in title/bio
   - Look at investors in Proton, Signal, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Standard Notes portfolios
   - Check who follows #privacy #dataprivacy #consumertech hashtags

3. **Warm up before connecting (2-week engagement cycle):**
   - Comment thoughtfully on target investor's posts with genuine perspectives
   - Reply with founder-experience takes
   - Share relevant content they'd care about
   - By the time you send a connection request, you are no longer a stranger

4. **Connection request message (under 200 words):**
   - Reference their specific investment thesis or a post they wrote
   - One sentence on what you're building
   - One sentence on why you thought of them specifically
   - No pitch deck attached. Just open the door.

5. **Follow-up message after connection:**
   - Short (3-4 sentences max)
   - Share your BriefLink or 1-pager
   - Ask if they'd be open to a 15-minute call
   - No pressure, no urgency tactics

### 2.8 Y Combinator Post-Money SAFE Mechanics

This is the instrument MyLife plans to use for the $500-750K aligned angel round.

#### What Is a Post-Money SAFE?

A SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) is not a loan and not equity. It's a contract that gives the investor the right to future equity when a priced round happens (typically Series A). The "post-money" variant (introduced by YC in 2018) means the valuation cap includes all SAFE investments, giving investors immediate clarity on their ownership percentage.

#### How It Works

1. **You set a post-money valuation cap** (e.g., $8M)
2. **Investor writes a check** (e.g., $100K)
3. **Investor's ownership = investment / cap** ($100K / $8M = 1.25%)
4. **SAFE converts to equity** at the next priced round (Series A)
5. **No interest, no maturity date, no repayment obligation**

|----------|-----|
| All SAFE forms | https://www.ycombinator.com/documents |
| Post-Money SAFE (Valuation Cap Only) | https://www.ycombinator.com/assets/ycdc/Postmoney%20Safe%20-%20Valuation%20Cap%20Only%20v1.1.docx |
| SAFE Primer v1.1 (explanation) | https://www.ycombinator.com/assets/ycdc/Primer%20for%20post-money%20safe%20v1.1.pdf |
| Cooley SAFE Generator | https://www.cooleygo.com/generate-y-combinator-safe/ |

#### Valuation Cap Decision for MyLife ($6-9M)

Current market data for pre-seed/angel SAFE caps (2025):

| Round Size | Median Valuation Cap (Q2 2025) |
|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Under $250K | $7.5M |
| $250K-$500K | $10M |
| $500K-$1M | ~$10-12M |
| $1M-$2.5M | $15M |

**For MyLife's $500-750K angel round, a $6-9M post-money cap analysis:**

- **$6M cap:** Generous to investors. Gives up 8.3-12.5% ownership for $500-750K. Signals founder confidence is moderate, but attracts value-oriented angels. Easier to fill the round.
- **$8M cap:** Market-aligned for a pre-revenue consumer app with 30 modules built, TestFlight-verified, and strong product completeness. Gives up 6.25-9.4% for $500-750K. Sweet spot.
- **$9M cap:** Slightly above median for this raise size. Justified by product completeness (95% built), 30 modules, and privacy-first differentiation. Gives up 5.6-8.3%. May slow the round.

**Recommendation:** $8M post-money cap is the sweet spot. It's market-aligned, gives investors a meaningful stake (6.25-9.4%), and reflects the extraordinary product completeness for a pre-revenue company.

#### How Side Letters Work with SAFEs

A side letter is a separate agreement that modifies SAFE terms for specific investors without changing the SAFE itself. Common side letter provisions for angel investors:

1. **Information rights:** Quarterly/annual financial reports, board packages, investor updates. Standard SAFE has no info rights; angels often request them.
2. **Pro rata rights:** Right to invest in future rounds to maintain ownership percentage. Important for angels who want to follow on.
3. **MFN (Most Favored Nation):** If you issue a later SAFE with better terms, the earlier investor gets those terms too.

#### PBC Side Letter Mechanics

Since MyLife plans to convert from LLC to Delaware Public Benefit Corporation (PBC):

1. **PBC benefit report obligation:** PBCs must produce periodic benefit reports. A side letter can require these reports be shared with SAFE holders (reinforcing mission alignment).
2. **Mission lock provision:** A side letter can include language that SAFE holders support the PBC mission and agree not to pressure abandonment of benefit purpose.
3. **Conversion timing:** Side letter can specify that SAFE conversion happens after PBC conversion is complete, ensuring investors are buying into the PBC structure.

**Important note:** Standard YC SAFEs are designed for Delaware C-Corps. PBCs are a subtype of C-Corp in Delaware, so the standard SAFE works. But have a startup attorney review the PBC-specific implications before closing.

## Category 3: Revenue-Based Financing (RBF)

Revenue-based financing is non-dilutive capital repaid as a percentage of future revenue. It's ideal for Y2+ when MyLife has paying subscribers generating recurring revenue.

### 3.1 Clearco (formerly Clearbanc)

#### How It Works

Clearco provides non-dilutive revenue-based funding. You receive capital upfront and repay as a fixed percentage of future revenue. Payments flex with your sales.

#### Terms

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Funding range | $10K - $10M |
| Payback fee | 6-12.5% flat fee on advance |
| Revenue share | 6-12.5% of monthly revenue |
| Repayment | Flexes with revenue, no fixed schedule |
| Speed | As fast as 24 hours |
| Equity taken | None |
| Personal guarantee | None |

#### Eligibility Requirements

### 3.5 Stripe Capital

#### How It Works

Stripe Capital offers loans and cash advances to businesses that process payments through Stripe. It's invitation-only; you cannot apply directly.

#### Terms

| Parameter | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Advance range | Varies (based on processing volume) |
| Fee | 6-20% flat fee on advance amount |
| Repayment | % of daily Stripe sales (typically 5-15%) |
| Speed | Funds available quickly after accepting offer |
| Equity taken | None |
| Late/early fees | None |
| Credit check | None |

#### Example

$10,000 advance with $600 flat fee = $10,600 total repayment. Stripe deducts 5% of each day's sales until $10,600 is repaid.

#### Eligibility Requirements

- **U.S. business** with physical U.S. address
- **3+ months of Stripe processing history**
- **$5,000+ annual processing** through Stripe
- **$1,000+ average monthly processing** over last 3 months
- Must be opted in to Stripe Capital communications
- **Invitation-only:** Meeting minimums doesn't guarantee an offer

#### How to Improve Your Chances

Keep transaction volume through Stripe consistent and growing. Stripe's underwriting models evaluate processing history, so steady activity helps. Keep your Stripe account in good standing.

#### Fit for MyLife

**Potentially useful as a small supplement in Y2.** If MyLife processes subscription payments through Stripe (web subscriptions bypass Apple/Google fees), Stripe Capital could provide a small advance. The amounts are likely modest ($10-50K), but the terms are founder-friendly. The main limitation: mobile subscriptions go through App Store/Play Store, not Stripe.

| **Capchase** | $1M ARR | Flat fee | 70% of ARR | Enterprise SaaS | Y4+ |

### Critical Consideration: $12/yr Consumer Subscription and RBF

Most RBF providers are optimized for B2B SaaS with $5K-$50K annual contracts. A $12/yr consumer subscription is a fundamentally different revenue profile:

- **High volume, low ARPU.** You need ~11,000 subscribers just to hit Founderpath's $10K MRR minimum.
- **Consumer churn is higher.** RBF underwriters will discount your revenue more aggressively.
- **App Store intermediation.** 15-30% of revenue goes to Apple/Google before it reaches you, reducing the "recurring revenue" RBF providers can underwrite against.

**Mitigation strategies:**
1. Emphasize web/direct subscriptions (processed via Stripe, 100% of revenue counted)
2. Demonstrate strong retention metrics to offset consumer churn concerns
3. Target Founderpath first (lowest minimum, bootstrapped-founder-friendly)
4. Use Stripe Capital for web revenue specifically
