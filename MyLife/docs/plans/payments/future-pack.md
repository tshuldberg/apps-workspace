# MyPay Future Pack

Date: 2026-04-24

These tracks are intentionally separated from the first wallet launch.

## Product Scope

- Savings vaults and auto-round-up.
- Direct deposit with routing and account number UX.
- Bill pay and recurring payment management.

## Business And Regulatory Track

- BaaS vendor selection and contracting.
- FinCEN, MSB, and MTL strategy with counsel.
- Seller and merchant reporting, including Form 1099-K readiness.
- Trust, custodial accounting, and finance operations.

## Rule

Track these workstreams, but do not block the first regulated wallet launch on broader banking ambitions.
