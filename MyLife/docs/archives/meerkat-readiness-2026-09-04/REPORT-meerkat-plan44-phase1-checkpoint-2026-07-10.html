Major correctness fixes

  • Revoked and refenced legacy push and archive leases during migration.
  • Preserved legacy archive rows without invented signatures, hashes, identities, or byte counts.
  • Classified incompatible rows as legacy_unbound and excluded them from active paths.
  • Cancelled push attempts after registration, capability, or token invalidation in both backends.
  • Revalidated the complete push address graph at claim time using database time.
  • Preserved operations fencing history when leases and replay claims are released.
  • Added expiring persona reservations and recovery for durable humanity verification.
  • Mapped PostgreSQL outages to HTTP 503 at humanity and persona boundaries.
  • Shared one bounded HTTPS-by-default humanity client across persona and community processes.
  • Bounded NCMEC evidence to 256 hashes and 64 KiB.
  • Removed destructive hosted root-table grants.
  • Made directory health statistics authoritative across process restarts.
  • Replaced unsupported PostgreSQL regex quantifiers with explicit length checks.
