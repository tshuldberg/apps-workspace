# MyLife Module Expansion Opportunities

> Generated: 2026-03-26
> Input: Top 220 iOS apps analysis, competitor financials, existing 29-module inventory
> Purpose: Identify new modules that could draw users from popular iOS apps

---

## Methodology

Scored each potential new module on:
- **Simplicity to build** (5 = weekend project, 1 = months of work)
- **User draw potential** (5 = millions of frustrated users, 1 = niche)
- **Privacy wedge** (5 = competitor data practices are a scandal, 1 = no privacy angle)
- **Bundle fit** (5 = natural cross-module synergy, 1 = standalone silo)
- **Competitor weakness** (5 = market leader is hated/stale, 1 = competitor is beloved)

**Total score max = 25**

## Cross-Module Intelligence Unlocks

The real value of new modules isn't the module itself. It's the cross-module data correlation no single-purpose app can offer:

| New Module | Cross-Module Insight |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Sleep + Workouts + Mood | "You perform 23% better on workouts after 7+ hours of sleep" |
| Hydration + Nutrition + Fast | "Your fasting windows are easier when you drink 80oz+ the day before" |
| Screen Time + Mood + Sleep | "Your mood drops 1.2 points on days with 4+ hours of screen time" |
| Focus + Habits + Workouts | "You complete 40% more habits on days you exercise before 9am" |
| Password Vault + Budget | Auto-detect subscription services from saved logins |
| Expense Splitter + RSVP | "Your dinner party cost $127. Split 6 ways = $21.17 each" |
| Body Measurements + Nutrition + Workouts | "You gained 3 lbs of lean mass this month while averaging 2,400 cal/day" |
| Travel + Budget + Recipes | "Your trip to Japan cost $2,340. Here are the recipes you saved while there." |

This is the moat. Calm can't tell you how meditation affects your workouts. YNAB can't split your dinner bill. Fitbod can't correlate your sleep with your lifts. Only MyLife can.
