---

## Ranking Methodology

Each module is scored on four factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it measures |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **Market Size** | 3x | Total addressable users across all competitors in the category |
| **Competitor Revenue** | 2x | Proven willingness to pay in this category |
| **Module Readiness** | 2x | How close our module is to competitive parity (from Competitive Matrix) |
| **Privacy Wedge** | 1x | How much privacy concerns plague competitors (our differentiator) |

## Key Insight

The top 8 modules alone target **~1.6 billion combined competitor users** and compete against **~$2.3 billion in annual competitor revenue**. At $5/yr, even capturing 0.1% of that user base = 1.6M users and $8M ARR.

The privacy story is strongest for modules 1-3 (Budget, Cycle, Nutrition) where competitor data practices are genuinely harmful and publicly documented. These three modules should be the marketing leads.
