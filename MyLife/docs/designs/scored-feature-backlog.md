| MI Score | Range | Modules |
|----------|-------|---------|
| 5 | >100M users | Health, Notes, Cycle |
| 4 | 50-100M | Budget, Nutrition, Workouts, Flash |
| 3 | 10-50M | Mood, Habits, Journal, Fast, Voice, RSVP, Trails, Mail |
| 2 | 1-10M | Meds, Pets, Car, Homes, Closet, Garden, Market, Subs, Words, Forums, Books* |
| 1 | <1M | Surf, Stars |

*Books gets MI=5 for features because Goodreads has 150M+ users, making the book tracking market very large.
