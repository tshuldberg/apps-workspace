1. TLDR

The two reports contradict each other on what $4.99 means, and GPT is the accurate one. Fable presents a $4.99 per month hosted subscription as the launch SKU; GPT presents $4.99 as a one-time app unlock with hosted storage sold separately. The founder-locked model in Plan 22 and the launch orchestration doc both confirm GPT's reading. Everything else in both reports verified against the git record, with small caveats listed below.

Either correct it to the Plan 22 model or make an explicit founder decision to change the model, which would contradict Plan 22 and the orchestration doc.

6. The storage-ladder disagreement is an assumption, not an error

Fable recommends iCloud parity (2 TB at $10.99) assuming ~30% average fill (~$9.20 raw cost). GPT recommends pricing above iCloud from 200 GB up (2 TB at $14.99) using full-fill R2 math ($30.72). Both arithmetics check out under their stated assumptions. The choice between fill-rate assumptions and margin posture is a founder judgment call, not a factual dispute.

Related open decision Plan 22 Phase 0 explicitly flags a founder decision: keep meerkat:hosted-storage bundled in the hosted monthly SKU, or split it into the separate metered SKU Phase 6 describes. Whichever storage ladder is adopted should land as part of that decision.

7. Character of each report

  • Fable is a selling document: market sizing, competitive matrix, moats, the honesty boundary as a differentiator, why-now timing. Strong narrative, wrong pricing spine.
  • GPT is a diligence document: compliance standards (MASVS, NIST CSF, DSA, NCMEC, account deletion), a diligence proof pack, and a recommended launch position. Correct pricing spine, lighter narrative.
  • They are complementary. A corrected Fable narrative on top of GPT's pricing model and diligence checklist would be the strongest single artifact.
