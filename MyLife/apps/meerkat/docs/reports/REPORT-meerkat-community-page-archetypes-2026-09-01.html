Meerkat · community page archetypes

Six sample community front pages, each a different product shape a community owner can build: a creator's home, a newsroom, an art wall, a curated digest, a music scene, and a civic commons. Everything shown maps to primitives that already exist in the codebase; nothing is a fantasy widget.

The idea. A Meerkat community is not just channels: Plan 56 shipped the composition platform (Canvas blocks, Pages, layouts, milestone cards, page templates, themes, badges, the Plaza) and Plan 38 shipped sealed libraries. These archetypes are what page templates should ship in the create-community gallery, so "make a fan hub / newsroom / gallery / zine" is a one-tap starting point, the same move as Discord's server templates. Owner-signed theming restyles each page; the tab bar and the rest of the app never retheme.
The bigger claim: the Meerkat editor replaces creator platforms. Archetypes 7-10 push past community pages into the platforms creators currently rent: Patreon and OnlyFans-class membership (tier-gated content for any legal work, mature creators included behind the universal 18+ gate), personal websites, and webstores. The architectural difference from every incumbent: membership IS the read capability (a paying member holds keys on their device; no paywall server decides), payments run on the creator's OWN processor so Meerkat takes 0% and never touches the money, digital goods deliver as sealed library grants, and the page lives on the creator's own community server, so no platform can deplatform, reprice, or algorithm it. Archetype 10 is the editor itself.
