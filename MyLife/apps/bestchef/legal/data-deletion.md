# BestChef Data Deletion

You can delete your BestChef account and data at any time, from inside the
app, without contacting anyone.

## How to delete your account

1. Open BestChef.
2. Go to **Settings → Delete account**.
3. Confirm.

## What happens when you delete

**Immediately on your device:** local recipes, pantry, grocery lists,
submissions, comments, votes, cached media, settings, and sync secrets are
wiped.

**On our servers:** a deletion request is queued and processed by an
automated worker (normally within minutes, at most 24 hours). It removes
your profile, submissions, comments, votes and vote proofs, uploaded media,
follows, notifications, and blocks, and then deletes the account itself.
Deletion is irreversible.

**What can persist, and why:**

- Encrypted backups can contain deleted data briefly until they age out.
- Moderation records tied to legal obligations (for example child-safety
  reports) are retained where the law requires.
- Aggregated, non-identifying statistics (for example a dish's historical
  vote counts) are not tied to your identity after deletion.

## If you cannot access the app

Email **support@bestchef.app** from the address linked to your account (or
with your chef handle if your account is anonymous) and we will process the
deletion manually within 30 days.

## Checking it worked

Reinstalling and creating a new account will show none of your previous
content. If you believe something remains, contact support@bestchef.app.
