# BestChef Community Guidelines

BestChef exists to answer one question: what is the best recipe for every
dish? That only works if the kitchen stays honest and welcoming. These
rules apply to everything you publish: recipes, photos, videos, comments,
chef profiles, dish proposals, and vote proofs.

## Cook it for real

- **Vote proof means you cooked it.** Your proof photo must show the dish
  you actually made for that vote. Staged photos, stock images, other
  people's photos, and AI-generated images are vote fraud.
- **One photo, one vote.** Reusing a proof photo across votes, sharing
  photos within a group to farm votes, and deleting votes to recast them
  are all detected and enforced against: duplicate hashes are refused,
  reused photos across accounts go to moderators, deletions are throttled,
  and re-votes wait 24 hours and re-enter review.
- **Submit your own recipes.** Adapting a classic is cooking; copying
  someone's write-up and photos is not.

## Keep it welcoming

Zero tolerance for:

- Sexual content, and any content sexualizing minors (reported to the
  authorities where the law requires).
- Hate speech, harassment, bullying, or threats. Criticize the plating,
  never the person.
- Gore, violence, or animal abuse.
- Spam, scams, pyramid schemes, or undisclosed advertising.
- Doxxing or posting anyone's private information.

Food debates get spicy; keep them about the food.

## Keep it safe

- No content encouraging dangerous food handling (raw-chicken "hacks",
  botulism-risk canning shortcuts, and similar).
- Label common allergens honestly when your recipe involves them.
- No selling of regulated goods (alcohol, supplements, medicines).

## Reporting, enforcement, and appeals

Every piece of public content has a Report action. Reports go to human
moderators with the evidence attached. Depending on severity we remove
content, void votes, restrict features, or ban accounts. When we remove,
hide, or reject your content, we send you an in-app notification (your
notifications feed) that states what happened, the reason category, and
that you can appeal. If you think we got it wrong, appeal in-app from
My Reports or email support@bestchef.app; a human reviews every appeal and
you are notified of the outcome.

Blocking is always available: blocking a chef hides their content from you,
hides yours from them, and stops interactions between you server-side.
