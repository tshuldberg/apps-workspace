Yearn Community Guidelines

Last updated 2026-07-11

Yearn is a place to meet people. To keep it safe, everyone who uses Yearn agrees to these guidelines. Breaking them can get your content removed and your account suspended or permanently banned.

Zero-tolerance policy. Yearn has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behavior. There is no warning quota for sexual content involving minors, threats, harassment, hate, or non-consensual intimate imagery. We remove it and ban the account.

Who can use Yearn

  • You must be 18 or older. Accounts found to belong to minors are removed.
  • One real person per account. No impersonation, no bots, no fake profiles.

Not allowed

  • Any sexual content involving minors. This is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and law enforcement as required by law.
  • Harassment, threats, stalking, or intimidation of any person.
  • Hate speech or attacks based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, or age.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery, or sharing anyone's private information without consent.
  • Nudity or sexually explicit imagery in profile photos.
  • Solicitation, prostitution, scams, spam, or commercial promotion.
  • Violent, graphic, or self-harm content.

How we enforce this

  • Report any profile from its card. Reports go to our moderation team.
  • Block anyone to immediately stop all contact and hide them from you.
  • We review reported content and act on objectionable content and abusive users within 24 hours, removing content and suspending or banning accounts as warranted.
  • Reported content may be hidden while it is under review.

Reporting an emergency

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. To report child sexual abuse material, you may also contact NCMEC at report.cybertip.org. Report abuse inside Yearn using the report control on any profile, or email safety@yearn.app.
