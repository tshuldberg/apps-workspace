Yearn Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-07-11

This policy explains what Yearn collects, why, and your choices. Yearn runs no third-party analytics and no advertising trackers. We do not sell your data.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account identifier (from Apple, Google, email, or phone sign-in)To create and secure your account
Profile details you enter (name, birthday, pronouns, identity, prompts, interests)To build and show your profile
Photos you uploadTo display on your profile; stored privately and served over short-lived signed links
Approximate (coarse) locationTo show nearby people; we do not store precise GPS coordinates
Likes, passes, matches, and reports/blocksTo operate matching and safety
Message and intro ciphertextTo deliver your conversations; contents are end-to-end encrypted and unreadable by us

End-to-end encryption

Intros and chat messages are encrypted on your device to your match's device key. We store and forward only the ciphertext and cannot read message contents. Your device's private key never leaves your device's secure storage.

How we use data

To provide matching, messaging, and safety features; to enforce our Community Guidelines; and to comply with law. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

We share data only with infrastructure providers that operate the service on our behalf (hosting, database, storage, authentication) under confidentiality obligations, and with law enforcement where legally required, including mandated reporting of child sexual abuse material to NCMEC.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account in the app at any time; this permanently removes your profile, likes, matches, and messages. Some records may be retained where required for legal or safety obligations.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. Contact privacy@yearn.app. Account deletion is available directly in the app.

Children

Yearn is strictly for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 and remove such accounts.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@yearn.app.
