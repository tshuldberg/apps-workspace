DoWork Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026

DoWork is built privacy-first: no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no tracking, and your workout log lives on your device. This policy explains what little we do collect, why, and what control you have.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your workout history, programs, measurements, progress photos, recordings, calculators, and settings are stored in a local database on your device. They are not uploaded unless you explicitly share something. Offline video downloads are stored in the app sandbox on your device.

2. Voice control is on-device

Hands-free playback control uses your device's on-device speech recognition (Apple SFSpeechRecognizer with on-device recognition required, or Android SpeechRecognizer). Audio is processed on the device, is never transmitted to us or stored, and recognition only runs while a video is playing with the feature enabled or while you hold push-to-talk. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

3. Data we store when you use cloud features

  • Account: an anonymous account id; your email address if you attach one.
  • Profile: handle, display name, optional avatar.
  • Community content you post: workout shares, comments, likes, reports, blocks.
  • Trainer platform: trainer profiles and videos; subscription status records from the app stores (via RevenueCat) so paid access works; an append-only purchase ledger for trainer earnings.
  • Coaching: form-check videos and feedback, private to the one client and one trainer on that coaching link, enforced server-side with row-level security and signed, expiring media URLs.
  • Push: a push token if you enable notifications, plus your per-type notification preferences. Transactional coaching notifications default on; marketing is strictly opt-in and off by default.
  • View counts: deduplicated per-hour view marks for trainer videos (used only for creator statistics).

4. What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or behavioral tracking.
  • No advertising and no sale or sharing of personal data for advertising.
  • No audio recording or voice data collection.
  • No contact list, location, or background data collection.

5. Processors

Cloud features run on Supabase (hosting, database, storage, authentication; servers in the United States). Subscription state is processed by RevenueCat and billed by Apple or Google. Push notifications are delivered via Expo's push service and Apple/Google. These processors receive only what is needed to provide their function.

6. Retention and deletion

Cloud data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account in Settings removes your rows, your uploaded media, your push tokens, and your authentication record. Purchase ledger entries required for trainer accounting are retained without your account linkage. Local data is removed by deleting the app; sign-out wipes downloaded videos.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, and similar), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Account deletion is self-serve in the app; for anything else contact support@dowork.app and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

DoWork is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child is using cloud features, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. Changes

We will announce material changes to this policy in the app or by email before they take effect.
