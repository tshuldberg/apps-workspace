DoWork Community Guidelines

Effective date: July 4, 2026

DoWork's community exists so people can share training progress and learn from invite-only, approved trainers. Keep it useful and safe.

Post training, not harm

  • Share workouts, progress, form checks, and coaching content.
  • No harassment, hate, threats, or bullying. Critique form, not people.
  • No nudity or sexual content. Training wear in a training context is fine.
  • No dangerous "challenge" content, disordered-eating promotion, or steroid/PED sales.
  • No spam, scams, impersonation, or off-platform recruitment schemes.
  • Only upload content you have the right to share, and get consent from anyone identifiable in your videos.

Trainers hold a higher bar

  • Trainer accounts are invite-only and approved by DoWork. This confirms an invite was redeemed, not a credential check. Represent your qualifications honestly.
  • Keep client coaching content private; never republish a client's form-check video.
  • Give safety-first guidance. Do not prescribe medical treatment; refer clients to professionals when something is beyond training scope.

Enforcement

Every post, comment, video, and profile carries Report and Block actions. Reports are reviewed and recorded with moderation decisions. Depending on severity we may remove content, hide it from feeds, revoke trainer status, or terminate accounts. Blocked users disappear from your feeds everywhere in the app.

Appeals

If you believe a moderation decision was wrong, email support@dowork.app with the content link or your handle. We review appeals within 7 days.
