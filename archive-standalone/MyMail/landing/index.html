Self-hosted personal email

Your email. Your server.
Your rules.

MyMail is a self-hosted personal email system that gives you complete ownership and privacy over your email. Deploy your own private email server in under 30 minutes with a guided setup wizard. No third-party access, no ads, no scanning.

Features

Everything you need for private email

A complete, production-ready email stack with security, deliverability, and ease of management built in.

@

Private email on your domain

Full ownership of your email address, data, and server. No third party can read your inbox or mine your data.

W

Setup wizard

Guided deployment with automatic configuration and DKIM key generation. Go from zero to a running mail server in 30 minutes.

T

Automatic TLS

Let's Encrypt certificates managed automatically by Caddy. Zero-configuration HTTPS and STARTTLS for all connections.

S

Spam filtering

Rspamd with Bayesian learning, real-time blacklist checks, and automatic spam classification. Keeps your inbox clean.

M

Webmail

Roundcube webmail accessible from any browser. Read and send email from anywhere without installing a dedicated client.

B

Automatic backups

Restic-based encrypted daily backups to S3-compatible storage. One-command restore to recover from any failure.

R

Outbound relay support

Route outgoing email through Amazon SES, SendGrid, or any SMTP relay for maximum deliverability from day one.

D

Modern admin dashboard

Web-based management interface built with SvelteKit. Manage accounts, domains, spam rules, and server health from one place.

Architecture

How MyMail works

Six containers, fully orchestrated with Docker Compose. Every component is open-source and battle-tested.

Component Role Port(s)
CaddyReverse proxy, automatic TLS80, 443
StalwartSMTP, IMAP, JMAP mail server25, 465, 587, 993, 4190
RspamdSpam filtering (milter protocol)11332 (internal)
RedisCache, Bayesian backend6379 (internal)
RoundcubeWebmail (PHP-FPM)9000 (internal)
Admin UIManagement dashboard (SvelteKit)3000 (internal)

Quick Start

Up and running in 30 minutes

Get a VPS, point your DNS, and run these three commands.

# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/yourusername/mymail.git
cd mymail

# Run the guided setup wizard
chmod +x scripts/setup.sh
./scripts/setup.sh
